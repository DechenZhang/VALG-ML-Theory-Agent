# Global Proof

Use this internal skill after `/proof-sketch-review` accepts a sketch and before any `/proof-step` runs.

## Contract

Required inputs:

- `perspective_M/idea_N/setting.md`
- accepted `perspective_M/idea_N/proof_sketch.md`
- accepted `perspective_M/idea_N/proof_sketch_review.md`

Optional inputs:

- `perspective_M/idea_N/proof_tracker.md`
- triggering `perspective_M/idea_N/global_proof_review.md` when rerunning after `REVISE_GLOBAL`
- prior same-branch sketch, global-proof-review, step, assembly, or final review failures when the controller provides them as diagnostic history

Output:

- `perspective_M/idea_N/global_proof.md`

Do not modify sketches, step artifacts, final proofs, trackers, worker logs, or accepted results. Do not write or overwrite `latex_template/`.

## Responsibilities

- Draft a detailed theorem-level proof implied by the accepted sketch.
- Make the draft review-ready for a later global-proof reviewer by exposing claim identity, theorem-level blocks, dependency flow, assumption provenance, cited-tool obligations, quantitative-dependence obligations, and unresolved links.
- Check that the sketch steps cohere globally and plausibly compose to the formalized goal.
- Attempt theorem-critical derivations deeply enough to expose whether generated conditions, recurrences, invariants, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specializations, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claims, scope upgrades, or theorem-closure blocks have a concrete closure mechanism and mechanism source under the declared theorem scope.
- Trace theorem-facing generated outputs from their producer step, theorem-level block, primitive source, accepted-derived source, cited tool with a valid diagnostic discharge path under the Source-To-Claim Adequacy Gate, direct derivation, standard fact or tool, current-notation wrapper, primitive-source derivation with checked source-convention compatibility and raw-assumption feasibility, or explicitly conditional target to every consumer block, closure block, specialization, and final theorem use.
- Expose missing links, hard steps, and likely step-local bottlenecks before step workers run.
- Expose candidate idea-level obstructions for `/global-proof-review` only when the global diagnostic identifies the required theorem-contract change: changed primitive assumptions, changed algorithm/model/procedure, changed theorem scope/mode/metric, changed exposed dependence, changed success criterion, adding a theorem-facing mechanism source not supported by the setting, or weakening the conclusion.
- Route sketch-level or roadmap-level blockers back to `/proof-sketch`.

`global_proof.md` is diagnostic guidance. It is not proof evidence, not a cited result, not an assumption source, and not a substitute for accepted step proofs.

## Workflow

1. Read the exact setting and goal from `setting.md`.
2. Verify `proof_sketch_review.md` accepts the current `proof_sketch.md`; if not, stop as an input contract failure, do not write or overwrite `global_proof.md`, and ask the controller to rerun or hand off the correct upstream artifact or stage.
3. Read every sketch step, dependency, assumption, intended proof tool, output target, rate objective, and blocker.
4. If rerunning after `global_proof_review.md` returned `REVISE_GLOBAL`, read its blocking issues and required repair bundle. Repair only diagnostic-artifact defects under the same accepted sketch; do not change sketch steps, dependencies, assumptions, output targets, generated-output flows, generated-condition interfaces, accumulation behavior, closure mechanisms, mechanism witnesses, mechanism sources, boundary exclusions, theorem scope/mode/metric, exposed dependence, success criterion, or conclusion inside `/global-proof`.
5. Determine goal mode:
   - `exact-goal mode`: `setting.md` states the exact theorem claim.
   - `target-spec mode`: `setting.md` gives a theorem-ready target specification whose exact final bound, constants, or dependence were not fixed.
6. Apply the shared Assumption Provenance Contract and Generated Output Flow Gate from `../_shared/checklists/artifact-contracts.md`. Check whether the accepted sketch proves every theorem-facing generated-object, event, local-validity, recurrence, boundedness, membership, structural condition, or invariant fact needed by an unconditional target from primitive conditions and accepted dependencies. Check whether every theorem-facing generated output consumed by the attempted theorem has a legal producer, consumers, final theorem use, and dependency path.
7. Apply the shared Explicit Rate Contract from `../_shared/checklists/artifact-contracts.md` whenever the setting, goal, sketch, or review exposes quantitative dependence. Apply the shared Baseline Invariance Obligation whenever those artifacts expose a recovery, reduction, specialization, zero-defect, exact-limit, exact/noiseless, or baseline-case conclusion.
8. Apply the shared Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness Gate and Residual-To-Target Adequacy Gate from `../_shared/checklists/artifact-contracts.md`. For every theorem-critical generated condition, recurrence, invariant, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specialization, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claim, scope upgrade, or theorem-closure block, name a derivation path from primitive conditions, accepted dependencies, cited tools, direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, primitive-source derivations, or explicitly conditional targets to the target condition or control, including the claim class and theorem role, closure mechanism, mechanism source, source-to-claim match, source-convention compatibility when a source supplies the mechanism, residual-to-target adequacy when a bridge transfers a produced, baseline, transformed, or surrogate object or control into the consumed target, key positive/control term or structural source, opposing defect terms, closure/dominance/absorption relation, accumulation behavior under the declared scope when repeated, iterated, recursive, limiting, or otherwise accumulated, boundary regimes handled or excluded, and producer-consumer provenance when generated outputs are consumed; otherwise record the missing witness or bridge as a blocker.
9. Apply the shared Exported Interface Feasibility Gate and Residual-To-Target Adequacy Gate from `../_shared/checklists/artifact-contracts.md`. For every theorem-critical downstream-facing output target, generated condition, margin, threshold, simplified bound, basin or membership certificate, recurrence interface, cited-tool wrapper conclusion, direct derivation output, standard fact or tool output, current-notation wrapper output, primitive-source derivation output, or public bridge, expose the raw controls, exported interface, defect terms, controlled and uncontrolled defect classes, residual-to-target adequacy when a bridge transfers a produced, baseline, transformed, or surrogate object or control into the consumed target, dominance or transfer relation, margin or threshold source, source-convention compatibility when a source supplies the output, object-target compatibility when an entry, reference, population, no-error, or baseline object is consumed, branch or boundary handling when applicable, and consumers. If a direct derivation, standard fact or tool, current-notation wrapper, or primitive-source derivation supplies the output, also expose the exact setting convention, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, and raw-assumption-to-output path. If this path or residual-to-target adequacy is missing under the accepted sketch, classify it as `sketch/interface defect` or candidate `idea/theorem-contract defect`.
10. Classify every theorem-critical hard obligation as `step-local`, `sketch/interface defect`, or `idea/theorem-contract defect` under the shared Step-Locality And Theorem-Contract Gate.
11. Write a detailed theorem-level proof draft that follows the sketch dependency order and explicitly marks unresolved links.
12. Build a theorem-level block map and a coverage map from each `Step ID` to the portion of the whole-proof draft it supports.
13. Build the required `## Scope And Closure Certificate` for every theorem-critical generated condition, recurrence, invariant, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specialization, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claim, scope upgrade, or theorem-closure block covered by the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness Gate, including the concrete recurrence/potential or mechanism-specific control relation at diagnostic granularity for every all-time or accumulated certificate row, mechanism source / boundary exclusion, obligation locality classification, noncircular closure status, and entry-state / first-update stress entries when applicable.
14. Build `## Generated Output Flow` for theorem-facing generated outputs consumed by blocks, closure, specialization, or final theorem use.
15. Audit dependency closure, generated-output flow, assumption provenance, cited-tool applicability, quantitative dependence, exported-interface feasibility, scope-accumulation compatibility, early obstruction patterns, and same-setting repair plausibility.
16. Diagnose the smallest required repair level using the status rules below.
17. Write `global_proof.md` using `../_shared/templates/global-proof.md`.

## Global Draft Rules

- Treat the accepted `proof_sketch.md` as binding. Do not search for a new roadmap, change `Step ID`s, change step claims, change dependencies, change assumptions, or change output targets inside `/global-proof`.
- A `REVISE_GLOBAL` rerun from `global_proof_review.md` is a rewrite of the diagnostic only. If the required repair bundle cannot be satisfied without changing the accepted sketch or setting, write the obstruction explicitly and use `SKETCH_BLOCKED` rather than silently repairing the roadmap.
- In `## Attempted Theorem Claim`, state the target theorem or strongest attempted claim faithful to `setting.md`. Include goal mode, theorem-facing assumptions, conclusion, scope, and any exposed quantitative mode or dependence.
- In exact-goal mode, do not weaken the claim unless the draft marks the mismatch as a blocker.
- In target-spec mode, do not guess unsupported rates, constants, convergence modes, or dependence. Draft only the strongest concrete claim that the accepted sketch appears able to support, and flag missing required dependence as a gap.
- Do not weaken or replace an inherited baseline invariance obligation inside the attempted claim. If the diagnostic can support only a conservative, conditional, stopped, finite-scope, or remainder-only surrogate, mark the baseline mismatch as a blocker or candidate idea-level obstruction rather than presenting it as the same theorem contract.
- In `## Whole-Proof Draft`, write a substantive theorem-level diagnostic draft, not a summary of the sketch. Include named theorem-level blocks, the intended logical order, the main derivational transitions, and an assembly paragraph explaining how the blocks would combine to prove the target theorem or where composition fails.
- The draft should normally be long enough to let a future reviewer inspect the mathematical architecture without opening each future step proof. Avoid artificial length quotas, but do not compress nontrivial bridges, condition checks, or quantitative arguments into phrases such as "standard" or "routine".
- Every theorem-critical transition in `## Whole-Proof Draft` must expose the key inequality, implication, recurrence, comparison, or cited-tool instantiation that the transition depends on. A transition is too shallow when the reader cannot see what mathematical interface would turn the inputs into the claimed output.
- For every theorem-critical generated condition, recurrence, invariant, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specialization, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claim, scope upgrade, or theorem-closure block, name the derivation path from primitive conditions, accepted dependencies, cited tools, direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, primitive-source derivations, or explicitly conditional targets to the target condition or control. The path may be diagnostic and may point to future step-local proof units, but it must identify the claim class and theorem role, quantities and interfaces those units would control, key positive/control term or structural source, defect or forcing terms, closure/dominance/absorption relation, accumulation behavior when the target is repeated, iterated, recursive, limiting, or otherwise accumulated, the mechanism source that makes the interface nonvacuous, any source-convention compatibility and raw-assumption-to-claim or output feasibility needed by direct/standard/current-notation/primitive-source sources, producer-consumer provenance when generated outputs are consumed, and any allowed boundary regimes the target must handle or explicitly exclude.
- Map every accepted sketch `Step ID` to a named theorem-level block, direct assembly use, or explicit blocker in `## Sketch-Step Coverage`.
- In `## Theorem-Level Block Map`, include every named theorem-level block needed by the attempted claim, its source `Step ID`s or direct assembly role, dependency inputs, theorem-facing assumptions, and unresolved discharge obligations.
- Every selected high-risk roadmap item must appear as a named block, a named hard step, or an explicit blocker. High-risk classes include structural property claims, approximation or perturbation controls, recursive or invariant maintenance arguments, mode upgrades, explicit dependence in any relevant quantitative category, public simplification of technical quantitative statements, and hidden generated-condition assumptions.
- Every derived condition needed by the final theorem must appear as a named block, a sketch-step conclusion, or an explicit blocker. Do not let theorem-facing admissibility, good-event, local-validity, stability, recurrence, boundedness, membership, structural, or invariant packages serve as assumptions for an unconditional target unless a sketch step proves them from primitive conditions.
- Every theorem-facing generated output consumed by a theorem-level block, closure block, specialization, or final theorem use must appear in `## Generated Output Flow`. Do not classify a missing producer path, missing consumer dependency, or unsupported generated-output export as a hard local step unless the accepted sketch already contains a legal flow and the diagnostic only needs to expose it.
- In `## Scope And Closure Certificate`, certify every theorem-critical generated condition, recurrence, invariant, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specialization, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claim, scope upgrade, or theorem-closure block covered by the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness Gate needed by the attempted theorem. For each target condition or control, record the declared theorem scope, local interface or recurrence, key positive/control term or structural source, defect or forcing terms, accumulation behavior / scope compatibility, closure mechanism, mechanism source / boundary exclusion, source-to-claim adequacy when the target needs a structural lower/sign/support/nondegeneracy claim, obligation locality classification, noncircular closure status when applicable, entry-state / first-update stress result when applicable, primitive controls, generated controls, boundary stress result, and failure mode if the mechanism is absent. Every all-time or accumulated row must expose the concrete recurrence/potential or mechanism-specific control relation at diagnostic granularity, including the accumulated defect or forcing term, sign status, one-step charge/absorption/potential-drop, preservation, projection, coupling, stopping, or conditioning relation, and why that relation has a finite budget or is valid under the declared scope.
- For every theorem-critical exported interface consumed downstream, the certificate or `## Whole-Proof Draft` must expose the raw-controls-to-exported-interface path at diagnostic granularity. A hard step is not `step-local` when the accepted sketch only names a future proof, threshold, margin, wrapper, direct derivation, standard tool, current-notation proof, primitive-source derivation, or absorption but does not identify which available controls dominate which defect terms, which source supplies any remaining margin or threshold, any required object-target bridge and residual-to-target adequacy from produced entry/reference/baseline/transformed/surrogate object to consumed theorem target object, and any exact setting convention, source-convention compatibility, raw-assumption-to-output path, quantitative dominance or transfer relation, branch or boundary handling, and downstream interface needed by direct/standard/current-notation/primitive-source sources.
- When a baseline invariance obligation is present, record the baseline conclusion, the specialization or entry case, the mechanism source preserving the original conclusion, and any first-transition or stationary trace needed to show the original conclusion remains valid.
- For every theorem-critical hard obligation, record obligation locality using only `step-local`, `sketch/interface defect`, or `idea/theorem-contract defect`. `step-local` is valid only when the mechanism source already exists in primitive assumptions, accepted derived outputs, cited tools with a valid discharge path under the Source-To-Claim Adequacy Gate, explicitly conditional targets, or direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, or primitive-source derivations whose source-convention compatibility and raw-assumption-to-claim or output feasibility have already been checked at obstruction-level granularity, and the future proof step only instantiates or derives the claim under unchanged sketch claims, dependencies, assumptions, output targets, generated-output flows, generated-condition interfaces, accumulation behavior, closure mechanisms, mechanism witnesses, mechanism sources, boundary exclusions, theorem scope/mode/metric, exposed dependence, success criterion, and conclusion.
- For every hard step or unresolved local proof bottleneck, the certificate or `## Global Gaps And Hard Steps` must explicitly carry the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness when the step is central to final theorem closure, recurrence, invariant maintenance, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claims, scope upgrades, or quantitative specialization. Without that witness, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`; do not use `PARTIAL_BLOCKED`.
- The closure mechanism must be one or more of: self-contraction, dissipative/restoring recurrence, telescoping, summable control, signed cancellation, monotone potential, reserve/ledger under declared scope, stopping/conditioning argument, projection/nonexpansive maintenance, algebraic coupling, structural lower/upper comparison, or explicitly conditional target. If none applies, record the absence as a blocker rather than deferring it. A closure category such as monotone potential, reserve/ledger, finite-budgeted, first-exit, stopped/conditioned, locally absorbed, projection, dissipative recurrence, algebraic coupling, or small-gain is not enough unless the certificate states the concrete mechanism-specific control relation and finite-budget or declared-scope validity justification.
- Valid mechanism sources include a primitive condition, accepted derived control, cited tool with a valid diagnostic discharge path under the Source-To-Claim Adequacy Gate, direct derivation, standard fact or tool, current-notation wrapper, primitive-source derivation with checked source-convention compatibility and raw-assumption feasibility, or explicitly conditional theorem target. Merely naming a closure category, direct derivation, standard tool, current-notation proof, or primitive-source derivation is not enough.
- For repeated, iterated, recursive, limiting, or otherwise accumulated targets, the certificate must classify the defect behavior as locally absorbed, contractive, telescoping, summable, signed-cancelled, monotone-potential controlled, finite-budgeted under the declared scope, source-excluded, stopped/conditioned, explicitly conditional, or unsupported. A barrier, reserve, ledger, invariant, stability, bootstrap, first-exit, Lyapunov, finite-budget, small-gain, projection, dissipative recurrence, algebraic coupling, or future-step label is not a concrete proof interface without this accumulation classification, mechanism source, named accumulated defect, sign status, budget/potential or mechanism-specific control relation, one-step charge/absorption/potential-drop, preservation, projection, coupling, stopping, or conditioning relation, and finite-budget or declared-scope validity justification.
- If an all-time or accumulated certificate row only names a closure category or says a future local proof will supply the controlling relation, use `Status = SKETCH_BLOCKED` and record the row as a `sketch/interface defect` or candidate `idea/theorem-contract defect`; do not use `PARTIAL_BLOCKED`.
- For all-time, uniform, limsup, invariant, stability, recurrence, support, basin, boundedness, and generated-condition claims, apply the Noncircular Closure Gate. The certificate must name the noncircular producer or mechanism source, the exit/defect/control relation, and the dependency path to each consumer; if the proposed source assumes the target closure, generated condition, or final theorem consequence, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`.
- For recursive, iterative, descent, contraction, convergence, all-time, recurrence closure, invariant, basin/support, mode-conversion, and exact/noiseless specialization claims, apply the Entry-State / Activation Trace Gate. The certificate or `## Early Obstruction And Repair Plausibility` must name an allowed entry, initial, stationary, null, degenerate, exact/noiseless, or boundary state, trace the first update, transition, or stationary behavior at diagnostic granularity, and state whether the mechanism source is active before the target conclusion is consumed. If the mechanism is inactive while the theorem-facing conclusion remains false, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED` and label candidate idea-level evidence for `/global-proof-review` only when repair would require the required theorem-contract change: changed primitive assumptions, changed algorithm/model/procedure, changed theorem scope/mode/metric, changed exposed dependence, changed success criterion, adding a theorem-facing mechanism source not supported by the setting, or weakening the conclusion.
- The mechanism source must be adequate for the claim class. Upper bounds, smallness, finite budgets, local boxes, admissibility inequalities, or generic regularity are not by themselves adequate sources for positive lower, coercive, nondegenerate, signed, or support claims unless paired with a primitive, earlier-derived, cited, direct derivation, standard fact or tool, current-notation wrapper, primitive-source derivation, or conditional source of the required lower/sign/support/conditioning content with the required feasibility checks.
- A statement such as "a later step closes the generated condition" is invalid unless the certificate names the actual closure mechanism, the quantities controlled, the defect accumulation behavior, and the mechanism source. If a recurrence, interface, accumulation, or defect term is needed, the certificate must show why it is compatible under the declared theorem scope or explain the failure.
- In `## Early Obstruction And Repair Plausibility`, run obstruction-level checks only. Identify contract contradictions, missing theorem-critical mechanisms or mechanism sources, missing exported-interface feasibility, missing residual-to-target adequacy, unsupported mode or dependence upgrades, generated-condition provenance failures, limiting or boundary stress failures, entry-state trace failures, and whether repair appears possible under the same setting and goal. Do not attempt substantial local step proofs.
- If the strongest diagnosis is a candidate idea-level obstruction, keep `Status = SKETCH_BLOCKED` and `Suggested Routing = /proof-sketch`, because `/global-proof` has no `/subagent-idea-generator` route. Explicitly label the obstruction as candidate idea-level evidence for `/global-proof-review` to classify independently.
- If any theorem-critical hard obligation is classified as `sketch/interface defect` or `idea/theorem-contract defect`, use `Status = SKETCH_BLOCKED` and `Suggested Routing = /proof-sketch`. For `idea/theorem-contract defect`, explicitly label the finding as candidate idea-level evidence for `/global-proof-review`; do not emit `IDEA_FAIL` from `global_proof.md`.

## Audit Rules

- In `## Dependency And Assumption Audit`, classify theorem-facing conditions as primitive conditions, accepted derived conclusions from sketch dependencies, local conditional hypotheses, or missing derived-condition bridges. A final unconditional target may not assume missing derived conditions.
- In `## Citation And Tool Audit`, for each cited theorem, standard fact, technical tool, direct derivation, current-notation wrapper, primitive-source derivation, or dependency use, restate the source or name, current-branch objects, assumptions, conclusion needed, source-convention compatibility when theorem-critical, object-target compatibility when an entry, reference, population, no-error, or baseline object is consumed, and where each assumption would have to be discharged. For theorem-critical cited results, also record source identity, version or stable locator when relevant, exact label or stable statement identifier when used, statement role, source-object mapping, hypothesis discharge, conclusion-interface match, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, known non-output boundaries, and any bridge or wrapper obligation before treating the cited result as a `step-local` mechanism source. For theorem-critical direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, and primitive-source derivations, record the exact setting convention, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, raw-assumption-to-claim or output path, quantitative dominance or transfer relation when needed, branch or boundary handling, and downstream interface before treating the output as `step-local`.
- In `## Quantitative Dependence Audit`, when quantitative dependence is exposed or a baseline invariance obligation is present, record exposed variables, allowed hidden-constant dependence, fixed quantities, probability or stochastic mode, horizon or limiting mode, norm or metric mode, auxiliary tolerances, public specialization obligations, baseline-reduction obligations, and whether inherited baseline/recovery conclusions are preserved. Write `None` only when no quantitative dependence is exposed and no baseline invariance obligation is present.
- Track notation, quantifier order, constants, parameter dependence, stochastic or convergence mode, boundary cases, and interchanges of limits, expectations, suprema, derivatives, integrals, or conditionings when any are theorem-critical.
- In `## Scope And Closure Certificate`, do not classify a transition as a hard local step unless the theorem-level interface is concrete enough for a step worker to prove under the unchanged sketch and the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness is explicit, source-convention compatible when a source supplies the mechanism, and scope-compatible. The interface must name the controlled quantities, accepted inputs, generated outputs, accumulation or recurrence shape when present, defect behavior, accumulation mode, closure mechanism category, mechanism source, key positive/control term or structural source, opposing defect terms, closure/dominance/absorption relation, boundary regimes handled or excluded, and entry-state / first-update stress result when the Entry-State / Activation Trace Gate applies.
- In `## Scope And Closure Certificate`, do not classify an exported-interface gap as a hard local step. If proving the downstream-facing output requires a new bridge step, changed output target, changed dependency, new margin or threshold source, changed cited-tool wrapper conclusion, changed direct/standard/current-notation/primitive-source output conclusion, separation of defect classes, changed dominance/transfer relation, object-target compatibility or residual-to-target adequacy not exposed by the accepted sketch when an entry, reference, population, no-error, baseline, transformed, or surrogate object is consumed, or a same-target bridge for a transformed, weighted, preconditioned, whitened, reference-operator-modified, or otherwise surrogate produced object, source-convention compatibility not exposed by the accepted sketch, or raw-assumption/output feasibility not exposed by the accepted sketch, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`.
- In `## Generated Output Flow`, do not classify a missing producer-consumer path as a hard local step. A theorem-facing generated output must have a legal producer block/step/source, consumer block/step/final use, dependency path, and provenance class. If a new bridge, changed dependency, changed output target, or changed generated-condition interface is needed, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`.
- In `## Early Obstruction And Repair Plausibility`, distinguish a hard local proof obligation from a missing theorem-level mechanism. A hard obligation is step-local only when the accepted sketch already assigns the needed conclusion to a step, the theorem-level interface is source-adequate under unchanged scope and dependence, any cited tool has a valid discharge path under the Source-To-Claim Adequacy Gate, any required object-target compatibility has already been exposed, any transformed, weighted, preconditioned, whitened, reference-operator-modified, or surrogate produced object already has a same-target bridge with residual-to-target adequacy to the consumed interface, and any direct derivation, standard fact or tool, current-notation wrapper, or primitive-source derivation has checked source-convention compatibility and raw-assumption-to-claim or output feasibility.
- Before classifying a hard local step as `PARTIAL_BLOCKED`, run the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness Gate plus mechanism-source nonvacuity, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility and residual-to-target adequacy when an entry, reference, population, no-error, baseline, transformed, or surrogate object is consumed, and source-to-claim adequacy checks. The diagnostic must state why the claim is not false in allowed null, degenerate, exact/noiseless, or boundary regimes, and identify the primitive, earlier-derived, cited-tool, direct/standard/current-notation/primitive-source, or explicitly conditional source that prevents the mechanism from vanishing while the target conclusion would remain false or the theorem-critical obstruction would remain present. If the produced object is transformed, weighted, preconditioned, whitened, reference-operator-modified, or otherwise surrogate relative to the consumed target and no same-target bridge with residual-to-target adequacy is already exposed, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`. If the witness is missing, scope-incompatible, lacks required object-target compatibility, lacks required residual-to-target adequacy, lacks required source-convention compatibility or raw-assumption feasibility, or the source is only an upper/size/budget/local-validity/small-gain control and no lower/sign/support/conditioning source is available under the unchanged sketch, use `SKETCH_BLOCKED`.
- For each hard `Step ID`, outline the likely local proof units needed later by `/proof-step`, using provisional unit roles such as lemma, proposition, claim, hypothesis, invariant, cited-result application, or assembly bridge.
- Do not hide difficult obligations behind phrases such as "clearly", "obviously", "standard", "similarly", or "by routine arguments"; name the exact missing local unit, cited-result condition, quantitative bridge, assumption-provenance bridge, or assembly link instead.
- Local units and cited-result applications in `global_proof.md` are diagnostic planning guidance only. They must be independently proved, checked, or discharged by `/proof-step` before they can be used as proof evidence.

## Self-Check Before Writing

Before writing or finalizing `global_proof.md`, verify:

- Sketch faithfulness: the draft follows the accepted sketch without silently repairing claims, dependencies, assumptions, output targets, or rate objectives.
- Claim fidelity: the attempted claim does not strengthen assumptions, narrow the regime, change quantifiers, drop exposed dependence, or drift away from `setting.md`.
- Step coverage: every accepted sketch step has a coverage-map entry and a role in the whole-proof draft or an explicit blocker.
- Block coverage: every theorem-level block needed for the attempted claim is mapped to accepted sketch steps, direct assembly use, or an explicit blocker.
- Dependency flow: theorem-level blocks respect the accepted dependency order and identify any missing dependency closure.
- Assumption provenance: every theorem-facing generated-object, event, local-validity, recurrence, boundedness, membership, structural, or invariant condition is primitive, proved by a sketch step, diagnostic-only inside a conditional lemma, or named as a blocker.
- Scope and closure: every theorem-critical generated condition, recurrence, invariant, stability, boundedness, membership, convergence, quantitative specialization, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claim, scope upgrade, or theorem-closure block covered by the Theorem-Critical Mechanism Witness Gate has a certificate with a named derivation path, a concrete interface or recurrence when relevant, concrete recurrence/potential or mechanism-specific control relation for every all-time or accumulated row, key positive/control term or structural source, a valid closure mechanism category, a valid mechanism source / boundary exclusion, source-to-claim adequacy, source-convention compatibility when a source supplies the mechanism, object-target compatibility and residual-to-target adequacy when an entry, reference, population, no-error, baseline, transformed, or surrogate object is consumed, any direct/standard/current-notation/primitive-source raw-assumption feasibility when relevant, entry-state / first-update stress result when applicable, and a boundary stress result.
- Theorem-critical mechanism witness: every hard step or theorem-level block central to closure, recurrence, invariant maintenance, structural lower/sign/coercivity/nondegeneracy/support claims, scope upgrades, or quantitative specialization has an explicit, scope-compatible witness, or is routed as `SKETCH_BLOCKED`.
- Generated-output flow: every theorem-facing generated output consumed by the attempted theorem, block, closure, or specialization has a legal producer, consumer, final use, dependency path, and provenance class, or is named as a blocker.
- Scope accumulation: every theorem-critical repeated, iterated, recursive, limiting, or otherwise accumulated condition or control has a certificate that classifies defect behavior and explains why it is compatible with the declared theorem scope by naming the accumulated defect, sign status, budget/potential or mechanism-specific control relation, one-step charge/absorption/potential-drop, preservation, projection, coupling, stopping, or conditioning relation, and finite-budget or declared-scope validity justification, or records the noncompatible defect as a blocker.
- Citation and source discipline: cited tools are not treated as discharged unless their diagnostic source identity, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, and interface preflight are stated. Direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, and primitive-source derivations are not treated as `step-local` unless their diagnostic source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, and raw-assumption feasibility are stated. For theorem-critical cited results, this includes source identity, version or stable locator when relevant, exact label or stable statement identifier when used, statement role, source-object mapping, hypothesis discharge, conclusion-interface match, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when an entry, reference, population, no-error, or baseline object is consumed, known non-output boundaries, and any required bridge or wrapper obligations. For theorem-critical direct derivations, standard facts or tools, current-notation wrappers, and primitive-source derivations, this includes exact setting convention, source-convention compatibility, object-target compatibility when applicable, raw-assumption-to-claim or output path, quantitative dominance or transfer relation when needed, branch or boundary handling, and downstream interface.
- Quantitative discipline: exposed quantitative dependence and public simplification obligations are preserved or marked as gaps.
- Baseline discipline: inherited recovery, reduction, specialization, zero-defect, exact-limit, exact/noiseless, or baseline-case conclusions are preserved or marked as blockers; weaker surrogates are not presented as target-preserving.
- Early obstruction discipline: candidate idea-level obstructions are not hidden as generic hard steps; same-setting repair plausibility is explicitly recorded for reviewer classification.
- Diagnostic boundary: the artifact says it is not proof evidence, not a cited result, not an assumption source, and not authority to change step claims.
