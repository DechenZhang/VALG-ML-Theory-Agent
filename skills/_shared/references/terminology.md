# Terminology

- **Perspective**: a normalized theory lens selected from literature gaps
- **Idea**: a branch-local `(setting, goal)` candidate under one perspective
- **Theory tracker**: the cross-branch idea tracker
- **Proof tracker**: the branch-local proof retry tracker
- **Technical survey**: branch-specific technical references gathered during theorem proving
- **Public skill**: the controller that owns workflow order and routing
- **Internal skill**: a focused skill that owns one artifact family or reasoning step
