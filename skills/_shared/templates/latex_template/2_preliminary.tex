\section{Preliminaries}

Define only useful and meaningful notation derived from the formalized setting
introduced in the previous section and needed to state or read the main theorem.
Each new symbol, abbreviation, or operator must state what it denotes, why it is
introduced, and where it is used in the main theorem. Do not place proof-only
shorthand, one-off constants, or non-conceptual constants here; define them
directly in the main theorem statement or in the local proof where they are
needed. Prefer full setting-derived expressions over aliases when the alias only
compresses a simple quantity or hides a proof obligation. Do not add new
assumptions, proof claims, or proof obligations in this section.
