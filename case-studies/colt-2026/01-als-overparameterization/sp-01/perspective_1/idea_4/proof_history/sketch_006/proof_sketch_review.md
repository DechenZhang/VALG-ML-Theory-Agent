## Blocking Issues

1. The J1 conditional Rademacher premise is not discharged
   (proof_sketch.md:936-962). For q_real > 0, target coordinates within a
   mode are correlated Gaussian variables, so conditioning on magnitudes does
   not make their signs independent Rademachers. Scores, displacements, and
   graph membership are invariant under obvious global vector sign flips, but
   the sketch does not exhibit a symmetry that independently flips each
   off-target coordinate after the nonlinear proposal and selection map.
   Downstream effect: E_row is not a legal generated event with the stated
   conditional law. Smallest repair: prove an exact or approximate conditional
   sign law with bias terms under the actual Gram, or replace it by a valid
   Gaussian/vector concentration argument within the same setting.
2. The displayed Bernstein inputs do not imply the J1 row-l1 bound or its
   probability (proof_sketch.md:947-961). The supplied square-sum bound is
   independent of r, while the row norm is a sum of absolute entries.
   Column bounds on F_M do not by themselves control the E' circ E''
   contribution in a row, and Cauchy-Schwarz would introduce sqrt(r).
   A constant threshold 4 q_* chi_max + 16 q_*^2 has no displayed log(r)
   or variance decay capable of yielding 12 r^-6. Downstream effect:
   eta_in_sel and p_0 = 1/4 are unsupported. Smallest repair: derive a
   genuine row-l1 concentration or deterministic row budget with explicit
   per-entry bounds, variance, tail, and union calculation.
3. The residual-to-tube export has a notation and scale defect
   (proof_sketch.md:462-499, 985-1003). The token {bt}norm_T is malformed.
   More substantively, G_M is used as a unit-direction Gram in H_M=G_M-I
   but M_M^* is called the raw balanced factor matrix in the gauge section.
   If it is raw, its diagonal is not I and D_M-I and the 1-q_*^2 bounds do
   not apply. The Delta-perpendicular and G2 component bounds are asserted
   across this ambiguity. Smallest repair: distinguish normalized directions
   from raw balanced factors, state the relative norm and kappa_0 scaling,
   and prove every G2 component in one convention.
4. G1 and A1-A2 still defer a hard local wrapper
   (proof_sketch.md:439-490, 536-585). Equal-norm balancing is algebraically
   unique, but existence throughout the absolute 1/512 tube, nonzero-column
   margins, the factor-scale dependence of G1, and the absorption of gauge
   components into the displayed norm_T inequalities are not proved.
   Downstream effect: pairwise contraction and tube invariance remain
   conditional on an unverified metric interface. Smallest repair: give a
   lemma-sized chart and norm-equivalence proof, then derive A1-A2 with the
   cited source convention and boundary cases.
5. Public provenance is still stale (proof_sketch.md:1305-1310 and
   1280-1294). Step_006's output row still lists eta_in_sel, flow rows cite
   S3-S5, and no row records E_row, its 12 r^-6 failure, or p_0. The
   narrative sole-producer correction is therefore not exported to the
   consumed interfaces. Downstream effect: the Generated Output Flow and
   Explicit Rate gates fail even if J1 is later proved. Smallest repair:
   make step_007 the sole producer everywhere and add E_row, J2, G2, and
   p_0 with their consumers and nested probability path.
