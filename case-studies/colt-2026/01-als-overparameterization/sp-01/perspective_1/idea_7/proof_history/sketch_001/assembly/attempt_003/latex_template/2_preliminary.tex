\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

For a matrix $H$, use the induced row and column masses
\[
 \|H\|_{\rm row,1}:=\max_i\sum_j|H_{ij}|,qquad
 \|H\|_{\rm col,1}:=\max_j\sum_i|H_{ij}|.
\]
For unit-column matrices $A$ and $B$, the Khatri--Rao product $A\odot B$
has column $a_j\otimes b_j$. Thus
\[
 (A\odot B)^\top(A\odot B)=(A^\top A)\circ(B^\top B),
\]
where $\circ$ denotes the Hadamard product. This identity is used for the
three target designs in the static and landing arguments.

For a target index $j$ and a state $h=(p,q,s)$ with nonzero target
correlations, write
\[
 A_{\ell\mid j}(p)={|\langle u_\ell,p\rangle|\over
 |\langle u_j,p\rangle|},\quad
 B_{\ell\mid j}(q)={|\langle v_\ell,q\rangle|\over
 |\langle v_j,q\rangle|},\quad
 C_{\ell\mid j}(s)={|\langle w_\ell,s\rangle|\over
 |\langle w_j,s\rangle|}.
\]
The associated generated trajectory quantities are
\[
 R_j(h):=\max_{\ell\ne j}\max\{A_\ell B_\ell,A_\ell C_\ell,B_\ell C_\ell\},
\]
\[
 S_j(h):=\max\left\{\sum_{\ell\ne j}A_\ell B_\ell,
 \sum_{\ell\ne j}A_\ell C_\ell,
 \sum_{\ell\ne j}B_\ell C_\ell\right\}.
\]
They are used only after the denominator-persistence result has established
that the displayed ratios are defined.

For selected representatives, let $P_U=P$, $P_V=Q$, $P_W=S$ and
$G_U=U$, $G_V=V$, $G_W=W$. The same-state coefficient and residual arrays
are defined by
\[
 K_M=D_\lambda\bigl((G_{M'}^\top P_{M'})\circ
 (G_{M''}^\top P_{M''})\bigr),qquad
 D_M=\operatorname{diag}(\|G_MK_M(:,a)\|_2).
\]
After the proof-only product-one gauge, the exact identity is
\[
 P_MD_M=G_MK_M+{\cal R}_M.
\]
The appendix derives its row and column consequences; the identity itself is
not used as an unproved invariant in the public statement.

The observable balanced seed has positive scales
\[
 d_{M,j}^0=\|X_M^0(:,j)\|_2,qquad
 D_M^0=\operatorname{diag}(d_{M,j}^0),qquad
 P_M^0=X_M^0(D_M^0)^{-1},
\]
and common represented products
$\gamma_j^0=d_{U,j}^0d_{V,j}^0d_{W,j}^0$.  The logarithmic product error is
$s_j^0=\log(\gamma_j^0/\lambda_j)$ and
$E^0=\operatorname{diag}(e^{s_j^0/3})$, so the exact balance convention is
\[
 D_U^0=D_V^0=D_W^0=D_\lambda^{1/3}E^0.
\]
On the generated target-span seed, the exact orthogonal decomposition used
in the landing bridge is written
\[
 P_M^0=G_M(I+C_M^0)+N_M^0,qquad G_M^\top N_M^0=0.
\]
This equation defines the coefficient and perpendicular fields; their
numerical bounds are conclusions of the certificate and seed results, not
assumptions in the main theorem.

For a positive balanced target-span state, the quotient distance used in the
landing and cyclic arguments is the maximum of the modewise coefficient
errors and the absolute product-log error.  If
$e=(C_U,C_V,C_W,s)$ and $\widetilde e=(\widetilde C_U,\widetilde C_V,
\widetilde C_W,\widetilde s)$, define
\[
d_Q(e,\widetilde e):=\max\left\{
\max_{M\in\{U,V,W\}}\left(
\|C_M-\widetilde C_M\|_{\rm row,1}\vee
\|C_M-\widetilde C_M\|_{\rm col,1}\vee
\max_j\|G_M(C_M-\widetilde C_M)(:,j)\|_2\right),
\|s-\widetilde s\|_\infty\right\}.
\]
The exact target quotient is $0=(0,0,0,0)$; the cyclic refresh map is
defined by the active ALS updates in the appendix.  No quotient condition is
assumed here.

The fixed cyclic chart radius used by the procedure is
$\rho_{\rm ALS}:=1/1024$.  The inequality
$4096\omega<\rho_{\rm ALS}/3$ is proved by the landing result and is not a
primitive admissibility condition.

For the baseline clause, write $\rho_{\rm sm}$ for the smoothing scale $\rho$.
The exact baseline clause uses the following explicitly scoped deterministic
specialization, denoted by $\mathsf{ExactCertifiedSeed}$:
\[
\rho_{\rm sm}=0,\qquad U^\top U=V^\top V=W^\top W=I_r,qquad
\lambda_1=\cdots=\lambda_r=:\lambda>0,
\]
and the proposal has supplied the exact certified representatives
$(p_j,q_j,s_j)=(u_j,v_j,w_j)$ up to a permutation and a product-one sign
gauge.  Thus the frozen seed is the exact certified seed, rather than merely
an element of a positive-radius neighborhood.  This is a limiting baseline
scope condition and is not an additional assumption in the positive-smoothing
theorem.

Finally, the original stopping metric is
\[
 \operatorname{res}(X,Y,Z):=
 {\left\|T-\sum_{i=1}^k x_i\otimes y_i\otimes z_i\right\|_F\over\|T\|_F},
\]
whenever $\|T\|_F>0$.  All public rates below use the ambient Frobenius norm,
the one-drawn instance probability $1-\delta_{\rm sm}$, and conditional
restart probability $1-\delta_{\rm init}$.

Write $\mathcal F_{\rm sm}$ for the sigma-field generated by the once-drawn
smoothing perturbations. Conditional probabilities with respect to
$\mathcal F_{\rm sm}$ keep the instance event separate from proposal and
restart randomness.
