\section{Main Theorem}\label{sec:main}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional strictly subquadratic synchronized landing]
\label{thm:main}
There are universal choices of
$C_{\rm rank},C_{\rm burn},C_{\rm cert},C_{\rm stop},C_{\rm rep}>0$ such
that the following statement holds uniformly over every deterministic base
triple satisfying Assumptions~\ref{assump:base-scale},
\ref{assump:cumulative-gram}, and \ref{assump:base-weight-balance}, and
over every parameter choice satisfying Assumptions~ef{assump:gaussian-smoothing}
--\ref{assump:accuracy-confidence}.

With probability at least $1-\delta_{\rm sm}$ over the once-drawn smoothing
perturbations, there is a generated event $E_{\rm sm}$ on which every realized
column has norm at least $(2\kappa _0)^{-1}$,
\[
 q_{\rm real}\le q_*,\qquad \Gamma\le1.01,qquad
 \lambda_{\min}\bigl((G_{M'}\odot G_{M''})^\top
 (G_{M'}\odot G_{M''})\bigr)\ge1-q_*^2
\]
for each mode $M$ and its two complementary modes $M',M''$.  The proof of
this event exposes separately the linear directional, independent quadratic,
and normalization terms at scales
$\kappa _0\rho\sqrt{L_{\rm sm}/n}$,
$\kappa _0^2\rho^2\sqrt{L_{\rm sm}/n}$, and
$\kappa _0^2\rho^2$, including the row-sum and finite-union factors.

Conditional on any fixed instance in $E_{\rm sm}$, each target and proposal
slot has a jointly observable window event whose three target raw
coordinates have absolute values in
$[\sqrt{a_*\log r},\sqrt{a_*\log r}+1/\sqrt{a_*\log r}]$ and whose
competing pair products are at most $b_*\log r$.  Its probability lies
between universal positive multiples of
$r^{-5/3}(\log r)^{-3/2}$.  Consequently the rank choice in
Assumption~\ref{assump:subquadratic-rank} gives a universal positive
one-run probability of simultaneous all-target coverage.  On every covered
slot the generated ratios satisfy
\[
 R_0\le {19\over20},\qquad S_0\le rR_0,
\]
and, whenever $\Gamma R_t<1$,
\[
 R_{t+1}\le\left({\Gamma R_t+q_{\rm real}\over
 1-\Gamma q_{\rm real}R_t}\right)^2,qquad
 S_{t+1}\le { (\Gamma R_t+q_{\rm real})
 \{\Gamma(1+q_{\rm real})S_t+q_{\rm real}\}\over
 (1-\Gamma q_{\rm real}R_t)^2}.
\]
The denominator condition persists, the target chart contracts, and the
finite burn/certification horizons produce a stored state with
$\zeta\le\tau_r$.  The score graph and minimum-residual rule return one
representative per target without labels.  After the proof-only permutation
and product-one sign gauge, the selected state and best-scalar seed satisfy
\[
 \max_M\bigl(\|C_M^0\|_{\rm row,1}\vee
 \|C_M^0\|_{\rm col,1}\bigr)\le48\omega,qquad
 \max_{M,j}\|N_M^0(:,j)\|_2\le\tau_r,qquad
 \|s^0\|_\infty\le128\omega.
\]

Before any landing solve, all three frozen designs formed from the same seed
simultaneously satisfy the deterministic reserves
\[
\delta_L=(1+q_*)48\omega,\quad
\delta_F=2(1+q_*)48\omega+(1+q_*)(48\omega)^2+r\tau_r^2,
\]
\[
\delta_{FL}=(1+q_*)48\omega+(1+q_*)(48\omega)^2+r\tau_r^2,\quad
\alpha_0=q_*+\delta_F,\quad \eta_J=\alpha_0^2,\quad
\eta_{QJ}=2\delta_{FL}+\delta_L^2+\delta_F^2,\quad
\eta_A={\eta_{QJ}\over1-\eta_J}.
\]
In particular, the normalized pair-Gram inverse reserves and the direct
$Q-J$ cancellation are valid in both induced orientations, and the three
Moore--Penrose outputs are well defined.  The synchronized joint commit and
one product-preserving rebalance produce an exact target-span state with
\[
 d_Q(e^{\rm land},0)\le4096\omega<\rho_{\rm ALS}/3,
\]
where $\rho_{\rm ALS}$ is the explicit chart radius in the cyclic argument.
More explicitly, for $F_M^0=(P_M^0)^\top P_M^0$,
$J_M^0=F_{M'}^0\circ F_{M''}^0$,
$Q_M^0=(G_{M'}^\top P_{M'}^0)\circ(G_{M''}^\top P_{M''}^0)$, and
$\ell_{M,j}^0=\lambda_j^{1/3}e^{-2s_j^0/3}$, and
$\widetilde X_M:=T_{(M)}H_M^0((H_M^0)^\top H_M^0)^\dagger$, the generated reserves give
\[
 \|J_M^0-I\|_{\rm row,1}\vee\|J_M^0-I\|_{\rm col,1}\le\eta_J\le{1\over64},
 \quad
 \|(J_M^0)^{-1}\|_{\rm row,1}\vee\|(J_M^0)^{-1}\|_{\rm col,1}
 \le{1\over1-\eta_J},
\]
\[
 \|Q_M^0-J_M^0\|_{\rm row,1}\vee\|Q_M^0-J_M^0\|_{\rm col,1}\le\eta_{QJ},qquad
 (1-\Gamma\eta_A)\ell_{M,j}^0\le\|\widetilde x_{M,j}\|_2
 \le(1+\Gamma\eta_A)\ell_{M,j}^0.
\]
No landing output, post-solve scale, comparator, or reserve is used as an
input to another landing call.

From this generated exact-span state, every chronological U/V/W cyclic sweep
is quotient-equivalent to the literal active ALS orbit, remains in the
$\rho_{\rm ALS}$ chart, and contracts by a universal factor strictly below
$1/16$.  A good covered run therefore reaches the original stopping test
\[
 \left\|T-\sum_{i=1}^k\widehat x_i\otimes\widehat y_i\otimes\widehat z_i
 \right\|_F\le\epsilon\|T\|_F
\]
before the pre-registered cap.  Every tape, including singular
Moore--Penrose branches, unsuccessful proposals, and nonhitting paths,
terminates at that cap.  With the independent restart count selected from
$C_{\rm rep}\log(1/\delta_{\rm init})$, the conditional probability of a
successful returned run is at least $1-\delta_{\rm init}$.  The returned rank
is at most $k=U(r)$, and the total arithmetic work is polynomial in
\[
 n,r,k,\kappa _0,\rho^{-1},\log(1/\epsilon),
 \log(1/\delta_{\rm init}),
\]
with hidden constants depending only on the fixed universal choices and the
allowed polynomial envelopes for $\kappa _0$ and $\rho^{-1}$.  In particular,
the rank and these envelopes make the work polynomial in
$n,r,\log(1/\epsilon),\log(1/\delta_{\rm init})$.

In the exact orthogonal equal-weight specialization, every frozen normalized
pair and cross Gram is the identity, the three landing outputs equal the
target factors, the common-product error and all refreshed product logs are
zero, the cyclic state is fixed, and the original residual is exactly zero
for every positive $\epsilon$.
\end{theorem}

The probability statement is nested: the first probability is over the one
smoothed instance, and the second is conditional on that instance over
independent proposal and restart tapes.  All displayed rates expose their
dependence on $n,r,\kappa _0,\rho,\epsilon$ and the two confidence levels;
no generated comparator, tube, reserve, or condition number is hidden in a
constant.
