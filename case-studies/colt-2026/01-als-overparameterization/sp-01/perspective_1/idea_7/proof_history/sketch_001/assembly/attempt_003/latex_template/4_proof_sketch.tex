\section{Proof Sketch}\label{sec:proof-sketch}

The proof is organized in thirteen local results whose complete derivations
are given in the appendix.  First, Gaussian smoothing, normalization, and a
finite union bound produce the event $E_{\rm sm}$ with the realized norm,
Gram, weight, and Khatri--Rao spectral interfaces.  Gaussian regression and a
three-variable product-tail calculation then give the target/slot window mass
$\Theta(r^{-5/3}(\log r)^{-3/2})$.  Independence only across slots and a
target-index union bound turn this mass into a universal positive
all-target coverage probability.

On a covered slot, the simultaneous old-state Jacobi map gives the displayed
recurrences for $R_t$ and $S_t$.  A scalar envelope proves denominator
persistence and finite chart entry; the resulting stored certificate is
converted into a signed same-state equation.  The score ledger, observable
graph, and product-one gauge select one representative per target without
labels.  Projecting the same-state equation in both induced orientations
closes the coefficient masses with one $r\tau_r$ residual charge.  A best
scalar and the equal-norm convention then produce the observable seed bounds.

The landing argument is deliberately frozen-input.  The three pair Grams,
cross-Gram cancellation, Neumann inverses, scale congruences, and normalizer
floors are established before any solve.  The three independent
Moore--Penrose outputs are committed together and one rebalance preserves
their represented products, yielding exact target-span membership and the
$4096\omega$ quotient entry.

Finally, scale equivariance identifies each chronological refresh with the
literal cyclic ALS block.  The target-span chart is invariant and a full
sweep contracts by a factor below $1/16$.  A balanced-chart residual transfer
converts quotient decay to the original Frobenius residual, while a finite
observable cap handles arbitrary tapes.  Independent completed runs amplify
the one-run coverage probability.  The last appendix subsection verifies the
orthogonal equal-weight fixed point directly, without replacing equality by a
finite tolerance envelope.
