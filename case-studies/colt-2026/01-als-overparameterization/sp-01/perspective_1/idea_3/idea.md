# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple: `(analysis_target: global_convergence, model_class: third_order_asymmetric_cp_least_squares, data_assumption: gaussian_smoothed_factors, regime: strictly_subquadratic_overparameterization, algorithm: parallel_als)`

## Added Dimensions

- Primitive geometry: bounded cumulative, rather than zero, modewise Gram interference. For normalized base factors define $\bar q=\max_{M\in\{\bar U,\bar V,\bar W\}}\max_j\sum_{\ell\ne j}|(M^\top M)_{j\ell}|$ and require $\bar q\le q_*/4$, where $q_*=1/4096$.
- Base scale: expose $\kappa_0\ge1$ with $\kappa_0\le\operatorname{poly}(r)$ and require every modewise base-column norm to lie in $[\kappa_0^{-1},\kappa_0]$.
- Algorithm subtype: extreme-start proposal-and-refinement parallel ALS (EP-ALS), with Gaussian rank-one ALS proposal slots, fully data-driven score filtering and clustering, followed by joint local CP-ALS on the selected active slots.
- Rank exponent: $c=2/3$ and $U(r)=\lceil C r^{5/3}(\log r)^{5/2}\rceil$ per run; confidence amplification uses independent runs and does not enlarge $U$.
- Mechanism source: the additional $r^{2/3}$ proposal budget pays for a three-mode product-Gaussian extreme event of probability $\Omega(r^{-5/3}(\log r)^{-3/2})$; the cumulative Gram bound converts that event into a cyclic ALS margin rather than assuming capture or basin entry.

## Setting

- Context: Replace generic Jacobi feature spanning by a staged parallel-ALS mechanism in which rare large-margin random entries identify all components under a nonorthogonal but cumulatively weak-interference geometry, after which the original joint CP least-squares dynamics supplies arbitrary-accuracy refinement.
- Model: $T=\sum_{j=1}^r a_j\otimes b_j\otimes c_j\in\mathbb R^{n\times n\times n}$ with the source rank-$k$ CP least-squares loss. Normalize each realized factor direction as $u_j,v_j,w_j$ and write $\lambda_j=\lVert a_j\rVert\lVert b_j\rVert\lVert c_j\rVert$.
- Data: For each base component define $\bar\lambda_j=\lVert\bar a_j\rVert\lVert\bar b_j\rVert\lVert\bar c_j\rVert$. Require every base-column norm to lie in $[\kappa_0^{-1},\kappa_0]$, the normalized base direction matrices to satisfy $\bar q\le q_*/4$, and $\max_j\bar\lambda_j/\min_j\bar\lambda_j\le1+1/800$. Add exactly the source's independent $\mathcal N(0,\rho^2 I_n/n)$ perturbations. Require $\rho=1/\operatorname{poly}(r)$,
  $\kappa_0\rho\le q_*/128$, and
  $r(\kappa_0\rho+\kappa_0^2\rho^2)\sqrt{\log(9r^2/\delta_{\rm sm})/n}\le q_*/32$.
  The proof must derive with probability $1-\delta_{\rm sm}$ that every perturbed column retains norm at least $(2\kappa_0)^{-1}$, normalization changes the cumulative Gram row sums by at most $3q_*/4$, the realized cumulative Gram bound is at most $q_*$, the realized weight ratio $\Gamma$ is at most $1.01$, and the cyclic Khatri--Rao Grams are uniformly nonsingular. The bridge must track the linear directional term $O(\kappa_0\rho/\sqrt n)$, the quadratic term $O(\kappa_0^2\rho^2/\sqrt n)$, and the normalization correction $O(\kappa_0^2\rho^2)$ rather than treating perturbations as scale free.
- Algorithm: Draw $k=U(r)\le n$ independent normalized Gaussian triples. In the proposal phase, all slots run in parallel for $L_0=C_0\log\log r$ cycles; a slot is temporarily gated as the sole nonzero term while each of its three updates exactly minimizes the corresponding rank-one block of the rank-$k$ least-squares loss. Compute only observable quantities: normalized contraction score $s_i=|\langle T,x_i\otimes y_i\otimes z_i\rangle|$ and one-cycle displacement. With $s_{\max}=\max_i s_i$, retain proposals having displacement at most $64q_*$ and $s_i\ge0.85s_{\max}$, cluster retained proposals when all three modewise absolute correlations exceed $1-64q_*$, and select the largest-score representative of each cluster. Activate the resulting $r$ representatives, set the other $k-r$ terms exactly to zero, and run cyclic CP-ALS on the active blocks of the original rank-$k$ loss until relative residual $\epsilon$; zero padding is not updated during this terminal phase. If the data-driven filter returns fewer than $r$ clusters, declare that run unsuccessful. Repeat independent full runs and return the smallest-residual output to amplify initialization success to $1-\delta_{\rm init}$.
- Regime: $r<k=\Theta(r^{5/3}(\log r)^{5/2})=o(r^2)$, $k\le n$, $\kappa_0\le\operatorname{poly}(r)$, and $0<\epsilon<1$. Exposed variables are $n,r,k,\rho,\kappa_0,\epsilon,\delta_{\rm sm},\delta_{\rm init}$. The rank constant is universal once $q_*$ and $c=2/3$ are fixed; hidden concentration, numerical-conditioning, and runtime constants may depend polynomially on $\kappa_0$ and on no other unlisted quantity.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Conditional end-to-end random-initialization convergence upper bound for EP-ALS.
- Informal statement: Prove that for every deterministic base triple satisfying the primitive cumulative-Gram, weight-balance, and scalar smoothing conditions above, with probability at least $1-\delta_{\rm sm}$ over the once-drawn smoothed instance and at least $1-\delta_{\rm init}$ over EP-ALS initialization conditional on that instance, EP-ALS returns at most $k=U(r)$ rank-one terms in $\operatorname{poly}(n,r,\log(1/\epsilon),\log(1/\delta_{\rm init}))$ time and satisfies $\lVert T-\sum_{i=1}^k x_i\otimes y_i\otimes z_i\rVert_F\le\epsilon\lVert T\rVert_F$. Derive the extreme-entry coverage, all-three-mode cyclic contraction, component-versus-mixture score gap, data-driven recovery of one representative per component, entry into the joint local CP-ALS basin, and terminal linear contraction from the primitive conditions; independent full-run restarts add only an $O(\log(1/\delta_{\rm init}))$ runtime factor and do not alter $U$.

## Notes

- Parent foundation papers: *On the Global Convergence of the Alternating Least Squares Method for Rank-One Approximation to Generic Tensors*; *Local Convergence of the Alternating Least Squares Algorithm for Canonical Tensor Approximation*
- Transfer map: preserves rank-one alternating least-squares proposals and quotient-Hessian local CP-ALS; changes the entry mechanism to a parallel rare-extreme pool and reuses exact block minimization, Gaussian anti-concentration, and local Hessian conditioning.
- Source alignment: preserves the asymmetric source tensor, rank-$k$ least-squares loss, source Gaussian smoothing, random initialization, strictly subquadratic rank, polynomial runtime, arbitrary relative error, and separate instance/initialization/restart quantifiers. It adds a cumulative-Gram and near-balanced-weight condition and a staged EP-ALS protocol, so it is conditional rather than a full arbitrary-base resolution; the remaining gap is removal of those static conditions and recovery by the unmodified Jacobi protocol.
- Progress type: conditional
- Materiality: The strongest claimed sufficient-rank rate itself is $U(r)=O(r^{5/3}(\log r)^{5/2})$. Unlike a loose envelope, the $r^{2/3}$ excess rank and $(\log r)^{3/2}$ tail factor invert the large-margin event, while the final $\log r$ covers all components; this tolerates nonzero cumulative interference beyond modewise orthogonal bases while retaining arbitrary-$\epsilon$ recovery.
- Significance: This route does not reuse generic feature spanning, exact block support, Orth-ALS replica averaging, algebraic warm starts, or an assumed attraction cone. It replaces the missing alignment source by a primitive row-sum geometry plus an explicit random-entry calculation and then hands off to a separately sourced local ALS mechanism.
- Feasibility: Put $a=2(1+c)/3=10/9$ and $b=(1+a)/2=19/18$. For a fixed component, the event that its three scaled Gaussian correlations all exceed $\sqrt{a\log r}$ has probability $\Theta(r^{-3a/2}(\log r)^{-3/2})=\Theta(r^{-5/3}(\log r)^{-3/2})$, while the product-Gaussian tail makes every competing pair product at most $b\log r$ with probability $1-O(r^{1-b})$. The resulting margin is $a/b=20/19$. Under cumulative Gram row sum $q_*$ and $\Gamma\le1.01$, current-coordinate expansion of the three ALS block formulas gives the proof obligation $R_{t+1}\le\Gamma(R_t^2+2q_*)/(1-2\Gamma q_*)$ from $R_0\le19/20$, which decreases to $O(q_*)$. The correlated-Gaussian version is sourced by Gaussian regression: row-sum $q_*$ bounds each conditional mean shift by $q_*\sqrt{a\log r}$ and keeps conditional covariance eigenvalues in $[1-q_*,1+q_*]$, so it changes the rare-event probability only by universal factors. A union bound with the stated $U$ covers all $r$ components. For observable selection, an equal-weight orthogonal mixture supported on $s\ge2$ components has normalized contraction score at most $1/\sqrt{s}\le1/\sqrt2$; the $q_*$ and $1.01$ perturbations leave a constant gap below the near-component scores, which the data-driven threshold must certify. Khatri--Rao Gram row sums are at most $q_*^2$, sourcing the local Hessian margin used for terminal refinement. These cyclic-margin, score-gap, coverage, and local-basin statements are proof obligations, not assumptions.
- Consistency: The novel primitive geometry is example-verified by three explicitly nonorthogonal, correctly scaled families. Equicorrelated frames with Gram off-diagonal $\mu=q_*/(4(r-1))$ and unit column scales have row sum $q_*/4$, $\kappa_0=1$, and $\bar\lambda_j=1$. Banded directions $u_j=(e_j+\alpha e_{j+1})/\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}$ with $\alpha\le q_*/16$ have two-neighbor row sum at most $q_*/4$; assigning modewise scales $(t_j,t_j^{-1},1)$ with $t_j\in[\kappa_0^{-1},\kappa_0]$ gives every base-column norm in the required interval and $\bar\lambda_j=1$. Block-correlated directions with block size $2\le s\le\sqrt r$, within-block off-diagonal $\mu=q_*/(4(s-1))$, and zero cross-block Gram entries also have row sum $q_*/4$; unit scales give $\bar\lambda_j=1$, and independent rotations in the three modes make these dense, asymmetric examples. In every family the interference term in a mode update is bounded by $q_*\max_{\ell\ne j}|s_\ell|$, so the $20/19$ extreme margin is active, while the displayed $\kappa_0$-dependent inequalities keep normalized smoothed directions and weights inside the realized margins. Source smoothing is fitted rather than discarded, proposal labels never use true components, and the active-$r$/zero-padding split is explicit.
