# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple: `(analysis_target: global_convergence, model_class: third_order_asymmetric_cp_least_squares, data_assumption: gaussian_smoothed_factors, regime: strictly_subquadratic_overparameterization, algorithm: parallel_als)`

## Added Dimensions

- Base-class specialization: each deterministic base matrix has pairwise orthogonal columns with all column norms in $[1,\kappa_0]$ for a fixed constant $\kappa_0$; the Gaussian perturbations remain exactly those of the source.
- Static scale condition: with fixed $\eta=1/4$ and instance-failure level $\delta_{\rm sm}$, require $n\ge C r\log(r/\delta_{\rm sm})$ and $(\rho+\rho^2)\sqrt{\log(r/\delta_{\rm sm})/n}\le c_0 r^{-2-\eta}$.
- Algorithm subtype: normalized asymmetric Orth-ALS slots updated in parallel, with periodic modewise QR separation, rerandomization of unrecovered slots, data-driven clustering, and terminal local CP-ALS refinement.
- Sufficient rank: $U(r,\delta_{\rm init})=\lceil C r\log(r/\delta_{\rm init})\rceil$, whose sharp reported power exponent is $\beta=0$ up to the logarithm; for the requested fixed $c\in(0,1)$ certificate take $c=1/2$, giving the valid but looser envelope $U=O(r^{1+1/2}\log(r/\delta_{\rm init}))$.

## Setting

- Context: Seek an end-to-end strictly subquadratic upper bound in the source-mentioned near-orthogonal special case, where the Orth-ALS separation mechanism has a literature-supported source and can be perturbed to asymmetric Gaussian-smoothed factors.
- Model: $T=\sum_{j=1}^r a_j\otimes b_j\otimes c_j\in\mathbb R^{n\times n\times n}$ with the source rank-$k$ CP least-squares objective. Write normalized realized factors as $u_j=a_j/\lVert a_j\rVert$, $v_j=b_j/\lVert b_j\rVert$, $w_j=c_j/\lVert c_j\rVert$ and component weights $\lambda_j=\lVert a_j\rVert\lVert b_j\rVert\lVert c_j\rVert$.
- Data: For every deterministic triple $\bar A,\bar B,\bar C$ whose columns are pairwise orthogonal within each mode and have norms in $[1,\kappa_0]$, add the source's mutually independent $\mathcal N(0,\rho^2 I_n/n)$ column perturbations, with $\rho=1/\operatorname{poly}(r)$ and the displayed static scale condition. Concentration must derive, rather than assume, bounded weight ratio and modewise coherence $\max_{i\ne j}\max\{|\langle u_i,u_j\rangle|,|\langle v_i,v_j\rangle|,|\langle w_i,w_j\rangle|\}\le c_1 r^{-2-\eta}$.
- Algorithm: Allocate $k=U(r,\delta_{\rm init})\le n$ independently Gaussian-initialized normalized slots. In each epoch, update every slot by asymmetric rank-one ALS contractions against the current CP residual, in parallel across slots, and periodically QR-separate estimates in each mode against accepted estimates. With $\tau=c_2r^{-1-\eta}$, cluster slots whose three pairwise mode correlations are all at least $1-\tau$; after a cluster representative has one-epoch displacement at most $\tau$ and positive residual decrease, accept the representative having the largest normalized tensor-contraction score and rerandomize unaccepted slots. After $r$ representatives are accepted, solve their scalar weights by least squares and run cyclic local CP-ALS on the original rank-$k$ objective to tolerance $\epsilon$, keeping unused slots at zero.
- Regime: $r<k=O(r\log(r/\delta_{\rm init}))=o(r^2)$, $0<\epsilon<1$, and $k\le n$. The theorem exposes $n,r,k,\rho,\epsilon,\delta_{\rm sm},\delta_{\rm init}$; hidden constants may depend only on $\kappa_0$, the fixed incoherence slack $\eta$, and universal Orth-ALS/local-convergence constants.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Material-partial random-initialization global-convergence upper bound.
- Informal statement: Prove that, under the setting above, with probability at least $1-\delta_{\rm sm}$ over the once-drawn smoothed instance and at least $1-\delta_{\rm init}$ over the algorithm's Gaussian initialization conditional on that instance, parallelized asymmetric Orth-ALS returns in $\operatorname{poly}(n,r,\log(1/\epsilon),\log(1/\delta_{\rm init}))$ time a decomposition using at most $k=U(r,\delta_{\rm init})$ terms and satisfying $\lVert T-\sum_{i=1}^k x_i\otimes y_i\otimes z_i\rVert_F\le\epsilon\lVert T\rVert_F$. Establish an $\Omega(1/r)$ per-slot component-capture probability from random initialization, derive distinct-component coverage via QR separation and a coupon-collector bound, prove perturbative entry into the local CP-ALS basin from the static smoothed-incoherence event, and prove linear terminal contraction to arbitrary $\epsilon$; independent full-run restarts amplify any constant-success implementation with an $O(\log(1/\delta_{\rm init}))$ runtime factor and do not change the rank bound.

## Notes

- Parent foundation papers: *Orthogonalized ALS: A Theoretically Principled Tensor Decomposition Algorithm for Practical Use*; *Local Convergence of the Alternating Least Squares Algorithm for Canonical Tensor Approximation*
- Transfer map: preserves Orth-ALS correlation growth, QR separation, and rerandomization together with quotient-Hessian local ALS convergence; changes the symmetric matched-rank model to three asymmetric smoothed modes and uses $k$ parallel candidate slots before an active rank-$r$ refinement.
- Source alignment: preserves the source tensor, rank-$k$ least-squares loss, Gaussian smoothing, random initialization, iterative ALS protocol, strictly subquadratic rank, polynomial runtime, relative Frobenius error, and separate instance/initialization/restart quantifiers. It narrows arbitrary or merely well-conditioned bases to modewise orthogonal, bounded-norm bases and uses an orthogonalized ALS subtype, so it is not a full resolution; the remaining gap is the unmodified parallel-ALS theorem for arbitrary or arbitrary well-conditioned smoothed bases.
- Progress type: material_partial
- Materiality: The theorem would give the requested end-to-end upper-bound certificate with the sharper $O(r\log r)$ slot count, hence the explicit $c=1/2$ subquadratic envelope, on a source-mentioned nontrivial smoothed special case rather than only a local or exact-recovery statement.
- Significance: Full candidates based on diagonal projection, generic multi-round alignment, or sparse interaction graphs were rejected because their off-diagonal leakage or basin-entry bridges had no source under the full smoothed model. The selected branch uses the weakest surveyed positive random-entry ALS mechanism that remains compatible with an upper bound.
- Feasibility: Base orthogonality, Gaussian concentration, QR separation, rerandomization, correlation clustering, least-squares weight fitting, correlation growth, and local quotient-Hessian contraction are standard/literature-derived from the parent papers and standard Gaussian bounds. The asymmetric extension is novel example-verified for three independently rotated orthogonal frames with equal weights, three such frames with bounded diagonal rescalings, and embedded canonical frames; in each case the noiseless contraction equations decouple componentwise, QR removes duplicate captures, and the displayed static scale inequality supplies the perturbative slack. Incoherence, capture, coverage, basin entry, stability, and contraction remain named proof obligations, not theorem assumptions.
- Consistency: The base class and scale condition are primitive and independently checkable before the trajectory, and none is equivalent to the desired output. The algorithm is a parallel ALS refinement rather than an algebraic recovery method, unused slots only pad the accepted rank-$r$ representation to rank $k$, and the theorem keeps arbitrary-$\epsilon$ recovery. This candidate is not a duplicate of the previous obstruction and does not claim coverage of general smoothed bases.
