### unit_001: proposition

**Proposition (Same-state signed certificate equation).**
\label{prop:step-004b-same-state}
Under the accepted conclusion of Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004-certificate}
and the setting-defined simultaneous Jacobi map, for every certified slot
\(a\) and mode \(M\), let
\[
 f_{M,a}:=T(\mathord{\cdot}_M,h_{M',a},h_{M'',a}),\qquad
 d_{M,a}:=\|f_{M,a}\|_2>0.
\]
If \(\varepsilon_{M,a}\) minimizes the projective residual, then
\[
 p_{M,a}d_{M,a}
 =\varepsilon_{M,a}f_{M,a}+R_{M,a}^{\rm raw},\qquad
 \|R_{M,a}^{\rm raw}\|_2\le d_{M,a}\tau_r.                 \tag{1}
\]
Moreover \(\varepsilon_{U,a}=\varepsilon_{V,a}=\varepsilon_{W,a}
=\operatorname{sign}(\theta_a)\), where
\[
\theta_a=\langle T,p_a\otimes q_a\otimes s_a\rangle,
\]
and \(\theta_a\ne0\).

**Proof / justification.**
By multilinearity and the setting's same-state pair matrix,
\[
 f_{M,a}=G_MK_M^{\rm raw}(:,a),\qquad
 K_M^{\rm raw}
 =\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)
   ((G_{M'}^\top P_{M'}^{\rm raw})
    \circ(G_{M''}^\top P_{M''}^{\rm raw})).
\]
The definition of E_cert gives
\(R_{M,a}^{\rm raw}=d_{M,a}(p_{M,a}
-\varepsilon_{M,a}F_{M,a}/d_{M,a})\), which proves (1). In matrix
form, with \(\Sigma_M=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon_{M,a})\),
\[
 P_M^{\rm raw}D_M
 =G_MK_M^{\rm raw}\Sigma_M+R_M^{\rm raw},\qquad
 \|R_M^{\rm raw}(:,a)\|_2\le D_M(a,a)\tau_r.                \tag{2}
\]
The right multiplication by \(\Sigma_M\) is columnwise; it never acts on
the rows of \(G_M\).

For a certified column, \(\|p_{M,a}-\varepsilon_{M,a}J_{M,a}\|_2^2
=2-2\varepsilon_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle\le\tau_r^2\).
Since \(\tau_r<10^{-6}<\sqrt2\),
\(\varepsilon_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle>0\). But
\[
\theta_a=\langle p_{M,a},f_{M,a}\rangle
       =d_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle.
\]
Thus the sign of \(\theta_a\) is \(\varepsilon_{M,a}\), independently of
the mode, and \(\theta_a\ne0\). This also proves
\[
 |\theta_a|=d_{M,a}\left(1-\frac12\zeta_{M,a}^2\right)
 \in[d_{M,a}(1-\tau_r^2/2),d_{M,a}].                       \tag{3}
\]
The proof uses only the stored same-state contraction and does not invoke a
target label, score filter, or clustering rule. \(\square\)

### unit_002: lemma

**Lemma (Span projection and scale bounds).**
\label{lem:step-004b-span}
Under E_sm and Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state}, define
\[
 H_M:=G_M^\top G_M,\quad L_M:=G_MH_M^{-1},\quad
 c_M:=L_M^\top p_M,\quad s_M:=\|c_M\|_2,\quad
 y_M:=c_M/s_M.
\]
Then \(L_M^\top G_M=I\), \(p_M=G_Mc_M+p_M^\perp\),
\(\|p_M^\perp\|_2\le\tau_r\), and
\[
 {1-\tau_r^2\over1+q}\le s_M^2\le {1\over1-q},\qquad
 1-{q\over2}-\tau_r^2\le s_M\le1+q,\qquad
 |s_M-1|\le q_* .                                             \tag{4}
\]
Also, with \(a_M=d_M/\lambda_{\min}\),
\[
 0.7\le a_M\le\Gamma(1+q)<1.02
 \quad\hbox{whenever }|\theta|\ge0.7\lambda_{\min},              \tag{5}
\]
and, for \(\widehat\alpha_M:=a_Ms_M/(s_{M'}s_{M''})\),
\[
 0.69\le\widehat\alpha_M\le1.03,\qquad
 0.995\le {s_M\over s_{M'}s_{M''}}\le1.005.                       \tag{6}
\]

**Proof / justification.**
The matrix \(\Pi_M:=G_ML_M^\top=G_MH_M^{-1}G_M^\top\) is the orthogonal
projector onto \({\rm range}(G_M)\). Apply \(I-\Pi_M\) to (1); the
contraction \(f_M\) lies in this range, so
\[
 p_M^\perp=(I-\Pi_M)p_M=(I-\Pi_M)R_M^{\rm raw}/d_M,
\]
which gives the residual bound. Orthogonality gives
\(1=c_M^\top H_Mc_M+\|p_M^\perp\|_2^2\). The spectrum interval in E_sm
then proves the squared bounds in (4).  For the lower scalar endpoint,
convexity of \((1+q)^{-1/2}\) at zero and
\(\sqrt{1-\tau_r^2}\ge1-\tau_r^2\) give
\[
 1-{q\over2}-\tau_r^2
 \le(1-\tau_r^2)(1-q/2)
 \le\sqrt{(1-\tau_r^2)/(1+q)}
 \le s_M\le(1-q)^{-1/2}\le1+q.                              \tag{4a}
\]
Here the first inequality drops the nonnegative product
\(q\tau_r^2/2\), and the final inequality follows from
\(q(1-q-q^2)\ge0\).  Since \(q\le q_*\) and
\(\tau_r^2\le q_*/2\), (4a) proves \(|s_M-1|\le q_*\), including the
allowed boundary \(q=0\), where the lower loss is the finite-certificate
term \(\tau_r^2\), not a realized-Gram term.

For the upper contraction bound, put \(z_{M'}=G_{M'}^\top p_{M'}\).
Every coordinate of \(z_{M'}\) is at most one in absolute value and
\(\|z_{M'}\|_2\le\sqrt{1+q}\). Therefore
\[
 d_M=\|G_M\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)(z_{M'}\circ z_{M''})\|_2
 \le(1+q)\lambda_{\max}.
\]
The lower bound \(d_M\ge|\theta|\) is Cauchy--Schwarz. This proves (5).
The ratio bounds in (6) follow from (4) and
\[
 {1-q/2-\tau_r^2\over(1+q)^2}>0.995,\qquad
 {1+q\over(1-q/2-\tau_r^2)^2}<1.005,
\]
and then from \(0.7\le a_M\le1.02\). All constants are uniform in the
slot, target, rank, and base triple. \(\square\)

### unit_003: lemma

**Lemma (Coefficient-vector Gram and certificate defect split).**
\label{lem:step-004b-defects}
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-span}, write \(H_M=I+\mathcal G_M\) and
\(\mu_i=\lambda_i/\lambda_{\min}\). For a non-LOW slot set
\[
 x_M:=|y_M|,\qquad
 \eta_G:=2q_*+q_*^2,\quad
 \eta_N:=5q_*^2,\quad
 \eta_C:=2\sqrt r\,\tau_r,\quad
 \eta_0:=\eta_G+\eta_N+\eta_C,\quad
 \eta_{\rm rel}:=8\eta_0.                                      \tag{7}
\]
Then, for each mode, define the signed coordinate defect
\[
 E_{M,i}:={\widehat\alpha_M\over\mu_i}x_{M,i}
       -x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}.                                      \tag{8}
\]
It obeys
\[
 |E_{M,i}|\le |e_{M,i}|,\qquad \sum_i|e_{M,i}|\le\eta_0,            \tag{8b}
\]
where \(e_{M,i}\) is only a nonnegative majorant.  Thus the exact identity
\[
 {\widehat\alpha_M\over\mu_i}x_{M,i}
 =x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}+E_{M,i}                                    \tag{8a}
\]
is available before any division by a coordinate product.
The scalar normalization is exact in \(\widehat\alpha_M\); \(\eta_N\) is
an explicit reserve for its second-order expansion and is not a hidden
first-order Gram term.

**Proof / justification.**
From \(G_M^\top p_M=H_Mc_M\) and \(c_M=s_My_M\),
\[
 {H_{M'}c_{M'}\circ H_{M''}c_{M''}\over s_{M'}s_{M''}}
 =y_{M'}\circ y_{M''}+g_M,
\]
where
\[
 g_M=(\mathcal G_{M'}y_{M'})\circ y_{M''}
 +y_{M'}\circ(\mathcal G_{M''}y_{M''})
 +(\mathcal G_{M'}y_{M'})\circ(\mathcal G_{M''}y_{M''}).
\]
The induced-norm and Hadamard inequalities give
\[
 \|g_M\|_1\le q_*+q_*+q_*^2=\eta_G.                              \tag{9}
\]
Taking \(L_M^\top\) of (1), dividing by
\(\lambda_{\min}s_{M'}s_{M''}\), and multiplying by the common sign
\(\varepsilon_M=\operatorname{sign}(\theta)\), gives
\[
 \widehat\alpha_M(\varepsilon_M y_M)
 =\operatorname{diag}(\mu)
   (y_{M'}\circ y_{M''}+g_M)+r_M,
 \quad
 r_M={\varepsilon_M L_M^\top R_M^{\rm raw}
       \over\lambda_{\min}s_{M'}s_{M''}}.                       \tag{10}
\]
Since \(\|L_M^\top\|_2\le(1-q)^{-1/2}\), (4)-(6) imply
\(\|r_M\|_2<2\tau_r\).  Taking absolute values in (10), dividing by
\(\mu_i\), and using \(\mu_i\ge1\), define \(E_{M,i}\) as in (8).
The reverse triangle inequality gives
\[
 |E_{M,i}|\le |g_{M,i}|+|(r_M)_i|/\mu_i.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sum_i|E_{M,i}|\le \eta_G+2\sqrt r\,\tau_r\le\eta_0,             \tag{10a}
\]
so (8b) holds with \(e_{M,i}:=|g_{M,i}|+|(r_M)_i|/\mu_i\).

For explicit scalar provenance, write \(s_M=1+\nu_M\). If the scalar
\(s_{M'}s_{M''}/s_M\) is expanded through first order, its exact
second-order remainder is
\[
 {\,\nu_{M'}\nu_{M''}-\nu_{M'}\nu_M-\nu_{M''}\nu_M+\nu_M^2
  \over 1+\nu_M}.
\]
Because \(|\nu_M|\le q_*\), its magnitude is below
\(4q_*^2/(1-q_*)<5q_*^2=\eta_N\). We retain this reserve when the
coordinate inequalities are summed; no first-order scalar term is charged
to \(g_M\). \(\square\)

### unit_004: proposition

**Proposition (No-label threshold and reciprocal-profile calculus).**
\label{prop:step-004b-threshold}
Under Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-defects}, let
\[
 \vartheta={1\over8},\qquad
 \vartheta_*=\vartheta-8\eta_{\rm rel},\qquad
 I:=\{i:\min_Mx_{M,i}\ge\vartheta\}.
\]
Then \(\vartheta_*>1/16\).  For every non-LOW slot, \(I\) is nonempty,
every index in \(I^c\) has all three coordinates below \(\vartheta\), and
the following statements hold:

1. For \(i\in I\), every coordinate pair is positive.  With
   \[
   \xi_{M,i}:={E_{M,i}\over x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}},
   \]
   the exact multiplicative identity
   \[
   {\widehat\alpha_M\over\mu_i}x_{M,i}
   =x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}(1+\xi_{M,i})                         \tag{11a}
   \]
   holds and \(|\xi_{M,i}|\le16\eta_0<1/128\).
2. For all \(M\) and \(i,\ell\in I\),
   \[
   \left|{\mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2\over\kappa_I^2}-1\right|
   \le144\eta_0,\qquad
   \kappa_I:=\left(\sum_{\ell\in I}\mu_\ell^{-2}\right)^{-1/2}. \tag{11b}
   \]
3. For every \(i\notin I\), let \(u_i:=\max_{M\in\{U,V,W\}}x_{M,i}\), and set
   \(B:=32.3\eta_0^2\).  The outside-mass conclusion exported by this
   proposition is
   \[
   \sum_{i\notin I}u_i^2<15\eta_0^2,\qquad
   \max_M\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2
      \le\sum_{i\notin I}u_i^2<15\eta_0^2\le B.                 \tag{15}
   \]
   Thus both (15) and the named envelope \(B\) are proposition
   conclusions available to downstream units; they are not proof-local
   aliases.
4. If a modewise maximum exceeds \(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\), its
   index lies in \(I\).  Hence mismatched top indices force
   \(|I|\ge2\); otherwise every modewise maximum is at most
   \(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\), and
   \[
   {\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}
   \le\Gamma(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}+16\eta_0).                 \tag{12}
   \]

**Proof / justification.**
The numerical values follow directly from (7):
\[
 \eta_0\le2q_*+6.0002q_*^2<4.89\cdot10^{-4},\quad
 \vartheta_*>0.0937,\quad
 {\eta_0\over0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0}
 \le16\eta_0<1/128.                                             \tag{13}
\]
Put \(A_{M,i}:=\widehat\alpha_M/\mu_i\).  By (6),
\(0.68\le A_{M,i}\le1.03\).  For an index in \(I^c\), sort its
three coordinates as \(u\ge v\ge w\), with corresponding defects
\(E_u,E_v,E_w\).  Since \(w<\vartheta\), the first two instances of
(8a) give
\[
 0.68u\le vw+|E_u|,\qquad 0.68v\le uw+|E_v|,
\]
and hence, with \(D:=0.68^2-\vartheta^2=0.446775\),
\[
 u\le {0.68\over D}|E_u|+{\vartheta\over D}|E_v|
 <1.53|E_u|+0.28|E_v|.                                          \tag{14}
\]
Therefore the per-index maximum itself satisfies
\[
 u^2<2(1.53^2+0.28^2)(|E_u|^2+|E_v|^2).
\]
Summing over the finite index set and observing that each selected defect
is one of the three mode-index defects, then using
\(\sum_{M,i}|E_{M,i}|^2\le3\eta_0^2\) yields
\[
 \sum_{i\notin I}u_i^2
 \le2(1.53^2+0.28^2)\sum_{M,i}|E_{M,i}|^2
 <15\eta_0^2,
 \qquad
 \max_M\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2
 \le\sum_{i\notin I}u_i^2
 <15\eta_0^2\le B,\qquad B:=32.3\eta_0^2.                      \tag{15}
\]
Moreover \(u<1.81\eta_0<\vartheta\), so every index in \(I^c\) has
all three coordinates below \(\vartheta\).  If \(I\) were empty, (15)
would contradict \(\|x_M\|_2=1\); hence \(I\ne\varnothing\) for every
non-LOW slot.

For \(i\in I\), (8a), \(A_{M,i}\ge0.68\), and
\(x_{M,i}\ge\vartheta\ge\vartheta_*\) imply
\[
 x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}\ge0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0>0,\qquad
 |\xi_{M,i}|\le
 {\eta_0\over0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0}\le16\eta_0.                 \tag{16}
\]
Thus division is legal.  Dividing the exact identity (11a) for two
retained indices gives
\[
 {x_{M,i}\over x_{M,\ell}}
 ={ \mu_i\over\mu_\ell}
 {x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}\over x_{M',\ell}x_{M'',\ell}}
 {1+\xi_{M,i}\over1+\xi_{M,\ell}}.                              \tag{17}
\]
Let \(\phi_{M,i\ell}:=(1+\xi_{M,i})/(1+\xi_{M,\ell})\).  Then
\[
 |\phi_{M,i\ell}-1|
 \le {2(16\eta_0)\over1-16\eta_0}<33\eta_0.                      \tag{18}
\]
Multiplying (17) over the three modes and solving the product identity
for one squared mode ratio cancels that mode's \(\phi\), so
\[
 \left|
 {(\mu_i x_{M,i})^2\over(\mu_\ell x_{M,\ell})^2}-1
 \right|
 \le {1\over(1-33\eta_0)^2}-1<68\eta_0.                         \tag{19}
\]
The last numerical inequality is checked at
\(\eta_0=2q_*+6.0002q_*^2\), where its left side divided by \(\eta_0\)
is \(<67.64\).

Choose a reference \(\ell_0\in I\), set
\(\beta_M:=\mu_{\ell_0}^2x_{M,\ell_0}^2>0\), and write
\[
 \mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2=\beta_M(1+\Delta_{M,i}),\qquad
 |\Delta_{M,i}|\le\delta:=68\eta_0.
\]
Let \(Z:=\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}\),
\(Z_M:=\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}(1+\Delta_{M,i})\), and
\(m_M:=\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2\).  The unit norm identity gives
\(\beta_MZ_M=1-m_M\), while (15) gives \(m_M\le B\).  Thus
\[
 \left|{\beta_MZ\over1}-1\right|
 \le B+{\delta\over1-\delta}<71\eta_0,                           \tag{20}
\]
because \(Z/Z_M\in[(1+\delta)^{-1},(1-\delta)^{-1}]\).  This is the
required additive omitted-mass plus reciprocal-error calculation; no
maximum shortcut or coefficient-vector \(c_M\) is used.
Finally,
\[
 \left|{\mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2\over\kappa_I^2}-1\right|
 =|\beta_MZ(1+\Delta_{M,i})-1|
 \le B+{\delta\over1-\delta}
   +\left(1+B+{\delta\over1-\delta}\right)\delta
 <144\eta_0,                                                     \tag{21}
\]
where \(\kappa_I^2=Z^{-1}\).  At the endpoint the preceding expression
is \(<140.7\eta_0\), leaving numerical slack.

It remains to make the straddling and LOW branch explicit.  Every index
with one coordinate at least \(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\) lies in \(I\)
by (15); if two modes have different such top indices, both indices lie
in \(I\), so \(|I|\ge2\).  Otherwise
\(\min_M\|x_M\|_\infty\le\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\).  Put
\(A_{\rm sign}:=\sum_i\mu_i y_{U,i}y_{V,i}y_{W,i}\) and
\(A_{\rm gram}:=\sum_i\mu_i(H_Uy_U)_i(H_Vy_V)_i(H_Wy_W)_i\).
The Hadamard expansion gives
\[
 |A_{\rm gram}-A_{\rm sign}|
 \le\Gamma((1+q)^3-1)\le4\Gamma q,\qquad
 s_Us_Vs_W\le(1+q)^3\le1+4q.                                   \tag{22}
\]
Also \(|A_{\rm sign}|\le\Gamma\min_M\|x_M\|_\infty\), by
Cauchy--Schwarz on the other two unit vectors.  Since
\(\sigma/\lambda_{\min}=s_Us_Vs_W|A_{\rm gram}|\), (22) and
\(q\le q_*\) imply
\[
 {\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}
 \le\Gamma(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}+16\eta_0).                 \tag{23}
\]
At the declared endpoint,
\[
 1.01\bigl(1/8+8(8\eta_0)+16\eta_0\bigr)<0.17,
\]
so the right side is \(<0.17\), not \(<0.14\);
it is nevertheless \(<0.7=s_{\rm gate}\).  Thus this branch is LOW,
and the four support alternatives are exhaustive. \(\square\)

### unit_005: proposition

**Proposition (Exhaustive LOW/core/tail ledger).**
\label{prop:step-004b-ledger}
Under Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-span}, Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-defects},
and Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold}, including its exported
outside-mass conclusion (15) and named envelope \(B\), every certified
slot has the following proof-only classification, with no target label.  A slot with
\(\sigma<0.7\lambda_{\min}\) is declared LOW.  For a slot with
\(\sigma\ge0.7\lambda_{\min}\), Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-defects} applies,
and the remaining alternatives below are exhaustive.

1. LOW: \(\sigma:=|\theta|<0.7\lambda_{\min}\).
2. Singleton core: \(I=\{j\}\), with
   \[
   \min_M x_{M,j}\ge1-64\eta_0,\qquad
   \max_M\sum_{i\ne j}x_{M,i}
   \le2.02\eta_0+{\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over4\vartheta_*^2}
   <1.43\cdot10^{-3}
   <a_{\rm cert}:=7q_*+8\tau_r<1/512,                       \tag{20}
   \]
   \[
   s_-\lambda_j\le\sigma\le s_+\lambda_j,\qquad
   s_-:=1-96\eta_0-8\tau_r,\quad
   s_+:=1+96\eta_0+8\tau_r.
   \]
3. Multi-support core: \(|I|\ge2\), with
   \[
   \operatorname{tail\_score}:=
   \sum_{i\notin I}\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
   \le8.08\eta_0+{2\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over\vartheta_*}
   <4.29\cdot10^{-3}<16\eta_0,                                  \tag{21}
   \]
   and the profile (11b) on \(I\) from
   Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold}.
4. Threshold straddling: if a top coordinate lies in the buffer
   \([\vartheta_*,\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel})\), either it joins the
   multi-support case or the score is LOW by (12). An empty I is LOW.

**Proof / justification.**
The first alternative is the definition of LOW.  In the complementary
non-LOW case, Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-defects} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold} supply the exact defect identity,
the nonempty support alternatives, and the profile.  We prove the singleton
fields, including the exact core floor and score expansion.

For \(I=\{j\}\), set
\[
 \ell_M:=\sum_{i\ne j}x_{M,i},\qquad
 t_M^2:=\sum_{i\ne j}x_{M,i}^2.
\]
Summing the exact defect identity (8a) over \(I^c\), and using
\(\widehat\alpha_M/\mu_i\ge0.68\), gives
\[
 0.68\ell_M\le t_{M'}t_{M''}+\sum_{i\ne j}|E_{M,i}|
 \le B+\eta_0.                                                \tag{24}
\]
Consequently, with
\[
 \ell_*:={\eta_0+B\over0.68},
\]
we have \(\ell_M\le\ell_*\le1.494\eta_0\) and
\(t_M^2\le B\).  Since \(B=32.3\eta_0^2<64\eta_0\),
\[
 x_{M,j}=\sqrt{1-t_M^2}\ge1-t_M^2\ge1-64\eta_0.                 \tag{25}
\]
Moreover \(\ell_*\le2.02\eta_0+\eta_{\rm rel}^2/(4\vartheta_*^2)\);
at the endpoint the latter right side is \(<1.43\cdot10^{-3}\), and
it is below \(a_{\rm cert}=7q_*+8\tau_r<1/512\).
This proves the required singleton tail and core-floor fields.  The
source split behind the displayed envelope is
\[
 2\Gamma\eta_G+2\Gamma\eta_N+2\eta_C
 +{\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over4\vartheta_*^2}
 \le2.02\eta_0+{\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over4\vartheta_*^2};            \tag{26}
\]
the direct bound (24) is stronger, while (26) records separately the
two Gram terms, scalar reserve, one finite-support certificate conversion,
and the discarded-coordinate products.
For the singleton score, write \(y_{M,j}=\rho_Mx_{M,j}\) with
\(\rho_M\in\{\pm1\}\), put \(\chi:=\rho_U\rho_V\rho_W\), and define
\[
 A_0:=\sum_i\mu_i y_{U,i}y_{V,i}y_{W,i},\qquad
 A_H:=\sum_i\mu_i(H_Uy_U)_i(H_Vy_V)_i(H_Wy_W)_i.
\]
The signed core/tail decomposition and (25) give
\[
 |A_0-\chi\mu_j|
 \le\mu_j(1-x_{U,j}x_{V,j}x_{W,j})
   +\Gamma\sum_{i\ne j}x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
 \le\Gamma(3\ell_*^2+\ell_*).                                \tag{27}
\]
For \(H_M=I+\mathcal G_M\), expanding the three factors and using
\(\|\mathcal G_M\|_2\le q\) gives
\[
 |A_H-A_0|\le\Gamma(3q+3q^2+q^3)\le4\Gamma q.                  \tag{28}
\]
The scalar projection norms require a separate finite-certificate term at
the allowed boundary \(q=0\).  From (4a),
\(|s_M-1|\le q+\tau_r^2\), and hence
\[
 |s_Us_Vs_W-1|
 \le3(q+\tau_r^2)+3(q+\tau_r^2)^2+(q+\tau_r^2)^3
 \le4(q+\tau_r^2),                                             \tag{28a}
\]
while the one-sided bound \(s_Us_Vs_W\le1+4q\) remains valid.  Since
\(\sigma/\lambda_{\min}=s_Us_Vs_W|A_H|\), the reverse triangle
inequality, (27), (28), and (28a) give
\[
 \left|{\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}-\mu_j\right|
 \le\Gamma\{(1+4q)(4q+\ell_*+3\ell_*^2)+4(q+\tau_r^2)\}
 \le\Gamma(12q+4\ell_*+3\ell_*^2+4\tau_r^2).                  \tag{29}
\]
For the last inequality, the difference between its inner right side and
the preceding braces is
\(4q(1-4q)+\ell_*(3-4q-12q\ell_*)\ge0\).  Thus no scalar loss is hidden
inside the Gram term.  Since \(q\le\eta_0/2\),
\(\ell_*\le1.494\eta_0\), and
\(\eta_0\ge2\sqrt r\,\tau_r\), direct endpoint arithmetic gives
\[
 12q+4\ell_*+3\ell_*^2\le12.1\eta_0,
\]
\[
 \Gamma(12q+4\ell_*+3\ell_*^2+4\tau_r^2)
 \le1.01(12.1\eta_0+4\tau_r^2)
 \le \mu_j(96\eta_0+8\tau_r).                                  \tag{30}
\]
The final, deliberately conservative \(8\tau_r\) reserve therefore
retains the finite certificate transient in the public score interval; it
is not used to justify the scalar inequality (28a).
Thus \(s_-\lambda_j\le\sigma\le s_+\lambda_j\) with the advertised
\(s_\pm\).

For the multi-support tail, for each \(i\notin I\) let \(u_i\) be its
largest coordinate and let \(E_{u_i,i}\) be the defect in that mode.
The corresponding instance of (8a) yields
\[
 x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
 \le1.03u_i^2+u_i|E_{u_i,i}|.
\]
Using the exported outside-mass conclusion (15) of
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold}, Cauchy--Schwarz, and
\(\sum_{M,i}|E_{M,i}|^2\le3\eta_0^2\), we obtain
\[
    \operatorname{tail\_score}
    \le1.01\{1.03(15)+\sqrt{15\cdot3}\}\eta_0^2
    <23\eta_0^2.                                                   \tag{31}
\]
The declared domain bound from (13) gives
\[
 0\le\eta_0<4.89\cdot10^{-4}<\min\{16/23,\,8.08/23\},
\]
and therefore, explicitly,
\[
 23\eta_0^2\le16\eta_0,\qquad
 23\eta_0^2\le8.08\eta_0
 \le8.08\eta_0+{2\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over\vartheta_*}.             \tag{31a}
\]
Consequently the exported downstream envelope in the ledger statement is
\[
   \operatorname{tail\_score}
   \le8.08\eta_0+{2\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over\vartheta_*}
   <4.29\cdot10^{-3}<16\eta_0.                                  \tag{32}
\]
Indeed, using \(\eta_{\rm rel}=8\eta_0\), \(\vartheta_*>0.0937\),
\(\eta_0<4.89\cdot10^{-4}\), and \(\eta_0\ge2q_*=1/2048\),
\[
 8.08\eta_0+{128\eta_0^2\over\vartheta_*}
 <8.08(4.89\cdot10^{-4})
   +{128(4.89\cdot10^{-4})^2\over0.0937}
 <4.29\cdot10^{-3}< {16\over2048}\le16\eta_0.                 \tag{32a}
\]
The ratio/profile calculation is valid on every retained coordinate by
(16)--(21).  The threshold proposition makes the alternatives exhaustive:
a non-LOW singleton has a common top index by the exported outside-mass
conclusion (15), a mismatched high-score
top index has \(|I|\ge2\), and an empty or buffer-only case is LOW by (23).
 All labels here are proof-only; no target label, filter, or cluster is
invoked in this producer step.
\(\square\)

### unit_007: proposition

**Proposition (Retained-core score bridge for multi-support slots).**
\label{prop:step-004b-multiscore}
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-ledger}, suppose that a non-LOW slot has
\(m:=|I|\ge2\).  With
\[
 \operatorname{tail\_score}:=\sum_{i\notin I}
 \mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i},\qquad
 \kappa_I:=\left(\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}\right)^{-1/2},
\]
the exact score obeys
\[
 {\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}
 \le\kappa_I+144\eta_0+\operatorname{tail\_score}
 \le{\Gamma\over\sqrt m}+160\eta_0,                              \tag{33}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \sigma\le s_{\rm mix}\lambda_{\max},\qquad
 s_{\rm mix}:={1\over\sqrt2}+160\eta_0.                           \tag{34}
\]

**Proof / justification.**
Let \(\delta_p:=144\eta_0<1\) and define
\[
 w_i:=\kappa_I^2\mu_i^{-2}\quad(i\in I),\qquad
 a_{M,i}:={\mu_i x_{M,i}\over\kappa_I}.
\]
Then \(\sum_{i\in I}w_i=1\), the reciprocal profile in
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-threshold} (equation (11b)) gives
\(|a_{M,i}^2-1|\le\delta_p\), and
\[
 \sum_{i\in I}w_i a_{M,i}^2=\sum_{i\in I}x_{M,i}^2\le1.           \tag{35}
\]
The retained absolute score is
\[
 \sum_{i\in I}\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
 =\kappa_I\sum_{i\in I}w_i a_{U,i}a_{V,i}a_{W,i}.
\]
Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz and
\(\max_i a_{U,i}\le\sqrt{1+\delta_p}\) imply
\[
 \sum_{i\in I}\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
 \le\kappa_I\sqrt{1+\delta_p}
 \le\kappa_I+72\eta_0.                                          \tag{36}
\]
Here \(\kappa_I\le1\) because \(m\ge2\) and
\(\mu_i\le\Gamma\le1.01\).
To pass from \(x\) to the exact score, put
\[
 S_x:=\sum_i\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i},\qquad
 S_H:=\sum_i\mu_i(H_Uy_U)_i(H_Vy_V)_i(H_Wy_W)_i.
\]
The same three-factor expansion as in (28) gives
\(|S_H|\le S_x+4\Gamma q\), while
\(s_Us_Vs_W\le1+4q\).  Since \(S_x\le\Gamma\),
\[
 {\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}
 =s_Us_Vs_W|S_H|
 \le S_x+\Gamma(8q+16q^2)
 \le S_x+5\eta_0,                                                \tag{37}
\]
where, explicitly,
\[
 \Gamma(8q+16q^2)\le1.01(8q+16q^2)
 \le5(2q+q^2)\le5\eta_0.                                        \tag{37a}
\]
Splitting \(S_x\) into its retained part and
\(\operatorname{tail\_score}\), (36) gives the first inequality in
(33), with \(72\eta_0+5\eta_0\le144\eta_0\).
Finally \(\mu_i\le\Gamma\) implies
\(\kappa_I^{-2}=\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}\ge m/\Gamma^2\), so
\(\kappa_I\le\Gamma/\sqrt m\).  The exported tail envelope in
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-ledger} supplies
\(\operatorname{tail\_score}\le16\eta_0\).  Since
\(\lambda_{\min}\Gamma=\lambda_{\max}\) and \(m\ge2\), (34) follows.
\(\square\)

### unit_006: lemma

**Lemma (Proof-only barred post-score equation).**
\label{lem:step-004b-bars}
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state}, let
\(\Omega_\theta=\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{sign}\theta_a)\). Define
base proof-only signs \(B_U=I,B_V=I,B_W=\Omega_\theta\). More generally,
after a
score-selection step let diagonal signs \(\Gamma_M\) satisfy
\(\Gamma_U\Gamma_V\Gamma_W=I\), and put
\[
 \bar P_M=P_M^{\rm raw}B_M\Gamma_M,\qquad
 \bar K_M=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)
 ((G_{M'}^\top\bar P_{M'})\circ(G_{M''}^\top\bar P_{M''})),
 \quad
 \bar R_M=R_M^{\rm raw}B_M\Gamma_M.                              \tag{22}
\]
Then
\[
 \bar P_MD_M=G_M\bar K_M+\bar R_M,\qquad
 \|\bar R_M(:,a)\|_2\le D_M(a,a)\tau_r.                           \tag{23}
\]
The represented rank-one term is unchanged, and its scalar is
\(\bar\theta_a=|\theta_a|\).

**Proof / justification.**
All diagonal signs commute with \(D_M\). The product of the three total
column signs \(B_M\Gamma_M\) is \(\Omega_\theta\). Therefore the sign
multiplying the raw pair coefficient in mode M,
\(\Omega_\theta(B_M\Gamma_M)\), equals the product of
the two other total signs. Multiplying (2) on the right by
\(B_M\Gamma_M\) gives (23), exactly with the redefined \(\bar K_M\).
The product of the three factor signs is \(\Omega_\theta\), so the tensor term is
multiplied by sign(theta), converting \(\theta_a\) to \(|\theta_a|\);
the original algorithmic initialization already places this sign in its
z factor. Positive product-one reorientations preserve the represented
tensor and all residual norms. No target label is required for this
algebraic identity. \(\square\)
