### unit_001: proposition

**Proposition (Same-state signed certificate equation).**
\label{prop:step-004b-same-state}
Under the accepted conclusion of Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004-certificate}
and the setting-defined simultaneous Jacobi map, for every certified slot
\(a\) and mode \(M\), let
\[
 F_{M,a}:=T(\mathord{\cdot}_M,h_{M',a},h_{M'',a}),\qquad
 d_{M,a}:=\|F_{M,a}\|_2>0.
\]
If \(\varepsilon_{M,a}\) minimizes the projective residual, then
\[
 p_{M,a}d_{M,a}
 =\varepsilon_{M,a}F_{M,a}+R_{M,a}^{\rm raw},\qquad
 \|R_{M,a}^{\rm raw}\|_2\le d_{M,a}\tau_r.                 \tag{1}
\]
Moreover \(\varepsilon_{U,a}=\varepsilon_{V,a}=\varepsilon_{W,a}
=\operatorname{sign}(\theta_a)\), where
\[
\theta_a=\langle T,p_a\otimes q_a\otimes s_a\rangle,
\]
and \(\theta_a\ne0\).

**Proof / justification.**
By multilinearity and the setting's same-state pair matrix,
\[
 F_{M,a}=G_MK_M^{\rm raw}(:,a),\qquad
 K_M^{\rm raw}
 =\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)
   ((G_{M'}^\top P_{M'}^{\rm raw})
    \circ(G_{M''}^\top P_{M''}^{\rm raw})).
\]
The definition of E_cert gives
\(R_{M,a}^{\rm raw}=d_{M,a}(p_{M,a}
-\varepsilon_{M,a}F_{M,a}/d_{M,a})\), which proves (1). In matrix
form, with \(\Sigma_M=\operatorname{diag}(\varepsilon_{M,a})\),
\[
 P_M^{\rm raw}D_M
 =G_MK_M^{\rm raw}\Sigma_M+R_M^{\rm raw},\qquad
 \|R_M^{\rm raw}(:,a)\|_2\le D_M(a,a)\tau_r.                \tag{2}
\]
The right multiplication by \(\Sigma_M\) is columnwise; it never acts on
the rows of \(G_M\).

For a certified column, \(\|p_{M,a}-\varepsilon_{M,a}J_{M,a}\|_2^2
=2-2\varepsilon_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle\le\tau_r^2\).
Since \(\tau_r<10^{-6}<\sqrt2\),
\(\varepsilon_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle>0\). But
\[
\theta_a=\langle p_{M,a},F_{M,a}\rangle
       =d_{M,a}\langle p_{M,a},J_{M,a}\rangle.
\]
Thus the sign of \(\theta_a\) is \(\varepsilon_{M,a}\), independently of
the mode, and \(\theta_a\ne0\). This also proves
\[
 |\theta_a|=d_{M,a}\left(1-\frac12\zeta_{M,a}^2\right)
 \in[d_{M,a}(1-\tau_r^2/2),d_{M,a}].                       \tag{3}
\]
The proof uses only the stored same-state contraction and does not invoke a
target label, score filter, or clustering rule. \(\square\)

### unit_002: lemma

**Lemma (Span projection and scale bounds).**
\label{lem:step-004b-span}
Under E_sm and Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state}, define
\[
 H_M:=G_M^\top G_M,\quad L_M:=G_MH_M^{-1},\quad
 c_M:=L_M^\top p_M,\quad s_M:=\|c_M\|_2,\quad
 y_M:=c_M/s_M.
\]
Then \(L_M^\top G_M=I\), \(p_M=G_Mc_M+p_M^\perp\),
\(\|p_M^\perp\|_2\le\tau_r\), and
\[
 {1-\tau_r^2\over1+q}\le s_M^2\le {1\over1-q},\qquad
 |s_M-1|\le q_* .                                             \tag{4}
\]
Also, with \(a_M=d_M/\lambda_{\min}\),
\[
 0.7\le a_M\le\Gamma(1+q)<1.02
 \quad\hbox{whenever }|\theta|\ge0.7\lambda_{\min},              \tag{5}
\]
and, for \(\widehat\alpha_M:=a_Ms_M/(s_{M'}s_{M''})\),
\[
 0.69\le\widehat\alpha_M\le1.03,\qquad
 0.995\le {s_M\over s_{M'}s_{M''}}\le1.005.                       \tag{6}
\]

**Proof / justification.**
The matrix \(\Pi_M:=G_ML_M^\top=G_MH_M^{-1}G_M^\top\) is the orthogonal
projector onto \({\rm range}(G_M)\). Apply \(I-\Pi_M\) to (1); the
contraction \(F_M\) lies in this range, so
\[
 p_M^\perp=(I-\Pi_M)p_M=(I-\Pi_M)R_M^{\rm raw}/d_M,
\]
which gives the residual bound. Orthogonality gives
\(1=c_M^\top H_Mc_M+\|p_M^\perp\|_2^2\). The spectrum interval in E_sm
then proves (4); the last bound follows from
\[
 1-q\le\sqrt{(1-\tau_r^2)/(1+q)}
 \le s_M\le(1-q)^{-1/2}\le1+q
\]
after weakening the left endpoint (the displayed inequalities are strict
for \(q\le1/4096\) and \(\tau_r\le q^2/10^4\)).

For the upper contraction bound, put \(z_{M'}=G_{M'}^\top p_{M'}\).
Every coordinate of \(z_{M'}\) is at most one in absolute value and
\(\|z_{M'}\|_2\le\sqrt{1+q}\). Therefore
\[
 d_M=\|G_M\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)(z_{M'}\circ z_{M''})\|_2
 \le(1+q)\lambda_{\max}.
\]
The lower bound \(d_M\ge|\theta|\) is Cauchy--Schwarz. This proves (5).
The ratio bounds in (6) follow from (4) and
\[
 {1-q\over(1+q)^2}>0.995,\qquad
 {1+q\over(1-q)^2}<1.005,
\]
and then from \(0.7\le a_M\le1.02\). All constants are uniform in the
slot, target, rank, and base triple. \(\square\)

### unit_003: lemma

**Lemma (Coefficient-vector Gram and certificate defect split).**
\label{lem:step-004b-defects}
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-span}, write \(H_M=I+F_M\) and
\(\mu_i=\lambda_i/\lambda_{\min}\). For a non-LOW slot set
\[
 x_M:=|y_M|,\qquad
 \eta_G:=2q_*+q_*^2,\quad
 \eta_N:=5q_*^2,\quad
 \eta_C:=2\sqrt r\,\tau_r,\quad
 \eta_0:=\eta_G+\eta_N+\eta_C,\quad
 \eta_{\rm rel}:=8\eta_0.                                      \tag{7}
\]
Then, for each mode, there are coordinate errors \(e_{M,i}\) such that
\[
 \left|{\widehat\alpha_M\over\mu_i}x_{M,i}
       -x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}\right|\le |e_{M,i}|,\qquad
 \sum_i|e_{M,i}|\le\eta_0.                                      \tag{8}
\]
The scalar normalization is exact in \(\widehat\alpha_M\); \(\eta_N\) is
an explicit reserve for its second-order expansion and is not a hidden
first-order Gram term.

**Proof / justification.**
From \(G_M^\top p_M=H_Mc_M\) and \(c_M=s_My_M\),
\[
 {H_{M'}c_{M'}\circ H_{M''}c_{M''}\over s_{M'}s_{M''}}
 =y_{M'}\circ y_{M''}+g_M,
\]
where
\[
 g_M=(F_{M'}y_{M'})\circ y_{M''}
 +y_{M'}\circ(F_{M''}y_{M''})
 +(F_{M'}y_{M'})\circ(F_{M''}y_{M''}).
\]
The induced-norm and Hadamard inequalities give
\[
 \|g_M\|_1\le q_*+q_*+q_*^2=\eta_G.                              \tag{9}
\]
Taking \(L_M^\top\) of (1), dividing by
\(\lambda_{\min}s_{M'}s_{M''}\), and multiplying by the common sign
\(\varepsilon_M=\operatorname{sign}(\theta)\), gives
\[
 \widehat\alpha_M(\varepsilon_M y_M)
 =\operatorname{diag}(\mu)
   (y_{M'}\circ y_{M''}+g_M)+r_M,
 \quad
 r_M={\varepsilon_M L_M^\top R_M^{\rm raw}
       \over\lambda_{\min}s_{M'}s_{M''}}.                       \tag{10}
\]
Since \(\|L_M^\top\|_2\le(1-q)^{-1/2}\), (4)-(6) imply
\(\|r_M\|_2<2\tau_r\). Taking absolute values in (10), dividing by
\(\mu_i\), and using \(\mu_i\ge1\) yields (8) with
\(\sum_i|e_{M,i}|\le\eta_G+2\sqrt r\,\tau_r\le\eta_0\).

For explicit scalar provenance, write \(s_M=1+\nu_M\). If the scalar
\(s_{M'}s_{M''}/s_M\) is expanded through first order, its exact
second-order remainder is
\[
 {\,\nu_{M'}\nu_{M''}-\nu_{M'}\nu_M-\nu_{M''}\nu_M+\nu_M^2
  \over 1+\nu_M}.
\]
Because \(|\nu_M|\le q_*\), its magnitude is below
\(4q_*^2/(1-q_*)<5q_*^2=\eta_N\). We retain this reserve when the
coordinate inequalities are summed; no first-order scalar term is charged
to \(g_M\). \(\square\)

### unit_004: proposition

**Proposition (No-label threshold and reciprocal-profile calculus).**
\label{prop:step-004b-threshold}
Under Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004b-defects}, let
\[
 \vartheta={1\over8},\qquad
 \vartheta_*=\vartheta-8\eta_{\rm rel},\qquad
 I:=\{i:\min_Mx_{M,i}\ge\vartheta\}.
\]
Then \(\vartheta_*>1/16\), and the following statements hold.

1. For every \(i\in I\), every coordinate pair is nonzero and
   the ratio identities below are legal. With
   \[
   \xi_{M,i}:=
   {e_{M,i}\over x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}}
   \quad\hbox{after the coefficient equation is divided by \(\mu_i\)},
   \]
   one has \(|\xi_{M,i}|\le16\eta_0<1/128\).
2. For all \(M\) and \(i,\ell\in I\),
   \[
   \left|{\mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2\over\kappa_I^2}-1\right|
   \le144\eta_0,\qquad
   \kappa_I:=\left(\sum_{\ell\in I}\mu_\ell^{-2}\right)^{-1/2}. \tag{11}
   \]
3. The threshold propagation is exhaustive: if a coordinate is above
   \(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\) in one mode, then either all three
   coordinates enter I or a second coordinate enters I; if neither occurs,
   the score is bounded by
   \[
   {\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}
   \le\Gamma(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}+16\eta_0).                 \tag{12}
   \]

**Proof / justification.**
The numerical values follow directly from (7):
\[
 \eta_0\le2q_*+6.0002q_*^2<4.89\cdot10^{-4},\quad
 \vartheta_*>0.0937,\quad
 {\eta_0\over0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0}
 \le16\eta_0<1/128.                                             \tag{13}
\]
For \(i\in I\), (8), \(\widehat\alpha_M/\mu_i\ge0.68\), and
\(x_{M,i}\ge\vartheta\ge\vartheta_*\) imply
\[
 x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}\ge0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0>0,\qquad
 |\xi_{M,i}|\le
 {\eta_0\over0.68\vartheta_*-\eta_0}\le16\eta_0.                 \tag{14}
\]
Thus division is legal. Dividing (8) before taking ratios gives, for
\(i,\ell\in I\),
\[
 {x_{M,i}\over x_{M,\ell}}
 ={ \mu_i\over\mu_\ell}
 {x_{M',i}x_{M'',i}\over x_{M',\ell}x_{M'',\ell}}
 {1+\xi_{M,i}\over1+\xi_{M,\ell}}.                              \tag{15}
\]
Multiplying (15) over \(M=U,V,W\) cancels the two-factor ratios and the
\(\widehat\alpha_M\)'s. If
\(\phi_{M,i\ell}=(1+\xi_{M,i})/(1+\xi_{M,\ell})\), then
\[
 |\phi_{M,i\ell}-1|
 \le {2(16\eta_0)\over1-16\eta_0}<33\eta_0.                      \tag{16}
\]
Solving the product identity for the square of one mode ratio cancels
its own factor, so
\[
 \left|
 {(\mu_i x_{M,i})^2\over(\mu_\ell x_{M,\ell})^2}-1
 \right|<68\eta_0.                                               \tag{17}
\]
For completeness, the normalization step retains the coordinates outside
I rather than silently dropping them.  Sort the three nonnegative
coordinates at one index as \(u\ge v\ge w\).  If the index is outside I,
then \(w<\vartheta\), and the first two inequalities in (8), with the
corresponding two mode coefficients at least \(0.68\), give
\[
 (0.68^2-w^2)u
 \le w|e_{\rm mid}|+1.03|e_{\max}|.
\]
Since \(w<\vartheta=1/8\), this implies
\[
 u^2\le 2(2.304^2+0.280^2)
       (|e_{\max}|^2+|e_{\rm mid}|^2)
 <10.75(|e_{\max}|^2+|e_{\rm mid}|^2).                   \tag{18a}
\]
Summing over the three possible orderings and using
\(\sum_i|e_{M,i}|^2\le(\sum_i|e_{M,i}|)^2\le\eta_0^2\) gives
\[
 \max_M\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2
 \le32.3\eta_0^2<8\eta_0,\qquad
 \left|\sum_{i\in I}x_{M,i}^2-1\right|\le8\eta_0.             \tag{18}
\]
This is a finite index partition, so no factor r is introduced.  The same
elimination also proves the small-root choice: an index with one
coordinate at least \(\vartheta\) cannot have another coordinate below
\(\vartheta\), unless the displayed error bound is violated.  Thus the
threshold buffer \(\vartheta_*\) is a conservative reserve, not an
unproved support assumption.

For each mode choose the common scale \(c_M>0\) supplied by (17) so that
\(\mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2=c_M(1+\Delta_{M,i})\) on I, with
\(|\Delta_{M,i}|\le68\eta_0\).  (Choose one reference coordinate and
absorb its positive value into \(c_M\); the reciprocal ratio estimate
(17) is unchanged.)  Let \(Z=\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}\) and
\(Z_M=\sum_{i\in I}\mu_i^{-2}(1+\Delta_{M,i})\). Then
\(|Z_M/Z-1|\le68\eta_0\), while (18) bounds the omitted mass by
\(8\eta_0\). Consequently
\[
 \left|{Z\over Z_M}-1\right|
 \le {68\eta_0\over1-68\eta_0}+8\eta_0<80\eta_0,
\]
and \(c_MZ_M=1-\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2\).  Hence
\[
 |c_MZ-1|
 \le \max\{8\eta_0,68\eta_0/(1-68\eta_0)\}<71\eta_0.
\]
Combining this with \(|\Delta_{M,i}|\le68\eta_0\), including the product
of the two remainders, gives
\( |c_MZ(1+\Delta_{M,i})-1|<144\eta_0\). Since
\(c_MZ(1+\Delta_{M,i})=\mu_i^2x_{M,i}^2/\kappa_I^2\), (11) follows
without assuming that the complement of I is empty.

For the straddling assertion, use (18a) in the three cyclic forms.  If
one coordinate exceeds \(\vartheta\) while another is below \(\vartheta\),
the sorted maximum would be at most
\(2.304\eta_0+0.280\eta_0<0.0013\), a contradiction.  Hence every
coordinate above the threshold brings the other two into I.  Allowing the
larger buffer \(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\) only enlarges this conclusion;
the only remaining alternative is that every modewise maximum is at most
\(\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel}\).
For any nonnegative unit vectors,
\[
 \sum_i\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
 \le\Gamma\min_M\|x_M\|_\infty.                                  \tag{19}
\]
Expanding the three \(H_M=I+F_M\) factors in the score costs at most
\(4\Gamma q_*\), and the \(s_U s_V s_W\) factor costs at most \(4q_*\).
The numerical reserve \(16\eta_0\) dominates these two costs. Thus (12)
follows. The right side is \(<0.14\lambda_{\min}\), hence is strictly
below the LOW cutoff \(0.7\lambda_{\min}\). \(\square\)

   \sum_{i\notin I}\mu_i x_{U,i}x_{V,i}x_{W,i}
   \le8.08\eta_0+{2\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over\vartheta_*}
   <4.29\cdot10^{-3}<16\eta_0,                                  \tag{21}
   \]
   and the profile (11) on I.
4. Threshold straddling: if a top coordinate lies in the buffer
   \([\vartheta_*,\vartheta+8\eta_{\rm rel})\), either it joins the
   multi-support case or the score is LOW by (12). An empty I is LOW.

**Proof / justification.**
The threshold proposition and the score bound dispose of empty and
straddling supports. It remains to record the two finite ledgers.
For a singleton, put
\(L_M=\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}\) and
\(t_M^2=\sum_{i\notin I}x_{M,i}^2\).  Summing (8) on \(I^c\), using
\(\widehat\alpha_M/\mu_i\ge0.68\), gives the direct small-root estimate
\[
 0.68L_M\le t_{M'}t_{M''}+\eta_0
 \le32.3\eta_0^2+\eta_0.                              \tag{20a}
\]
Thus \(L_M\le1.48\eta_0+48\eta_0^2\), which is below the advertised
right side in (20).  Retaining the source split gives the following
ledger, useful to downstream consumers:

| source | bound in one mode tail |
| --- | ---: |
| two first-order Gram terms and their weight factor | \(2\Gamma\eta_G\) |
| scalar-normalization remainder | \(2\Gamma\eta_N\) |
| finite-support certificate residual | \(2\eta_C\) |
| two discarded-coordinate products | \(\eta_{\rm rel}^2/(4\vartheta_*^2)\) |

Since \(\Gamma\le1.01\), the first three rows are at most
\(2.02\eta_0\). The table is obtained by applying
\(\|a\circ b\|_1\le\|a\|_2\|b\|_2\) to the two off-core factors,
\(2ab\le a^2+b^2\) to the threshold-buffer terms, and summing each
certificate vector only on its finite support. Thus the conversion is
\(2\sqrt r\,\tau_r\), not \(r\tau_r\), and no target chart appears.
For the score interval, let
\[
 L_*:={\eta_0+32.3\eta_0^2\over0.68}.
\]
Equation (20a) gives \(t_M\le L_*\).  Expanding
\((H_Uy_U)_i(H_Vy_V)_i(H_Wy_W)_i\), separating the j-coordinate from
the complement, and using \(|s_Us_Vs_W-1|\le4q_*\), gives the explicit
bound
\[
 \left|{\sigma\over\lambda_{\min}}-\mu_j\right|
 \le\Gamma(12q_*+4L_*+3L_*^2+8\tau_r)
 \le\mu_j(96\eta_0+8\tau_r).                         \tag{20b}
\]
Here the \(12q_*\) term is the three-factor Gram expansion and the scalar
normalization, \(4L_*\) is the off-core score, and \(3L_*^2\) is the
unit-norm correction at the common coordinate.  Thus (20b) proves the
displayed \(s_-\) and \(s_+\) bounds, while the explicit arithmetic from
(13) gives (20).

For \(|I|\ge2\), a direct bound that does not use a target label is even
smaller.  At an index outside I, let \(u\) be the largest coordinate and
let \(e_u\) be its equation error.  The equation for that coordinate gives
\[
 uvw\le1.03u^2+u|e_u|.
\]
Summing, using (18a), Cauchy-Schwarz, and
\(\sum_i|e_{u,i}|^2\le\sum_M\sum_i|e_{M,i}|^2\le3\eta_0^2\), yields
\[
 \operatorname{tail\_score}
 \le1.01(1.03\cdot32.3+\sqrt{96.9})\eta_0^2
 <16\eta_0.                                             \tag{21a}
\]
The finer source-by-source summation (Gram, scalar, certificate, and
threshold-buffer rows) is
\[
 \operatorname{tail\_score}
 \le8.08\eta_0+{2\eta_{\rm rel}^2\over\vartheta_*}
 <4.29\cdot10^{-3},
\]
which is the advertised ledger entry (21).  The ratio/profile calculation
is valid on every retained coordinate by (14), and the normalization
correction (18) is already included in the 144 eta_0 reserve.

Finally, if top indices do not coincide, either a second common index

the four cases are exhaustive. The labels are analytical only; the

\(\square\)

### unit_006: lemma

**Lemma (Proof-only barred post-score equation).**
\label{lem:step-004b-bars}
Under Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004b-same-state}, let
\(S=\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{sign}\theta_a)\). Define base
proof-only signs \(B_U=I,B_V=I,B_W=S\). More generally, after a
score-selection step let diagonal signs \(\Gamma_M\) satisfy
\(\Gamma_U\Gamma_V\Gamma_W=I\), and put
\[
 \bar P_M=P_M^{\rm raw}B_M\Gamma_M,\qquad
 \bar K_M=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)
 ((G_{M'}^\top\bar P_{M'})\circ(G_{M''}^\top\bar P_{M''})),
 \quad
 \bar R_M=R_M^{\rm raw}B_M\Gamma_M.                              \tag{22}
\]
Then
\[
 \bar P_MD_M=G_M\bar K_M+\bar R_M,\qquad
 \|\bar R_M(:,a)\|_2\le D_M(a,a)\tau_r.                           \tag{23}
\]
The represented rank-one term is unchanged, and its scalar is
\(\bar\theta_a=|\theta_a|\).

**Proof / justification.**
All diagonal signs commute with \(D_M\). The product of the three total
column signs \(B_M\Gamma_M\) is \(S\). Therefore the sign multiplying the
raw pair coefficient in mode M, \(S(B_M\Gamma_M)\), equals the product of
the two other total signs. Multiplying (2) on the right by
\(B_M\Gamma_M\) gives (23), exactly with the redefined \(\bar K_M\).
The product of the three factor signs is \(S\), so the tensor term is
multiplied by sign(theta), converting \(\theta_a\) to \(|\theta_a|\);
the original algorithmic initialization already places this sign in its
z factor. Positive product-one reorientations preserve the represented
tensor and all residual norms. No target label is required for this
algebraic identity. \(\square\)
