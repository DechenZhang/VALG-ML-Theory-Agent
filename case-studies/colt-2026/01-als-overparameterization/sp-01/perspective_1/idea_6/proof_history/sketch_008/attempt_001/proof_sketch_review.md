## Blocking Issues

Without it, the centered diagonal cancellation is not a legal exported interface. Smallest repair: `/proof-sketch`.
2. **The carried reserve is not a defined, valid two-orientation inequality.** `u_C` and `u_J` are used but never defined. Each of the two resolvent contributions is bounded by `u_C+u_J/2`, then the total is identified with that same quantity; the missing factor/term accounting prevents the claimed `ell_c=17/256` bound for row, column, ambient, and log outputs. Smallest repair: `/proof-sketch`.
3. **The generated beta envelope has no explicit bootstrap closure.** `beta_FS` depends on `h_V=epsilon_U` and `h_W=epsilon_U+epsilon_V`, while the epsilon bounds depend on `beta_FS`; the sketch states `beta_FS<2*10^(-8)omega` but does not show a noncircular scalar inequality or fixed-point selection that establishes it before the U/V/W calls. Smallest repair: `/proof-sketch`.
