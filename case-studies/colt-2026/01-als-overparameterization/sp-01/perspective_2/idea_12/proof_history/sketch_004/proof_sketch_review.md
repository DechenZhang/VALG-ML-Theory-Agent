## Blocking Issues

1. **The seed-dominance premise is not derived from the binding setting.** In `proof_sketch.md` the slab uses `a_n = sqrt(L_ang log(2r)/n)` and requires
   `C_seed { Gamma_star (nu_real^2 + r nu_real^3 + b_s) + sqrt(r)e_I } <= tau_min a_n^3 / 8`.
   The eleven IDs give only `nu_real <= 2/(C_nu r log(2r))` and no upper bound on `n`. For an allowed near-margin C-mode source with `nu_real` of order `1/(r log r)` and, for example, `n = r^4`, the structural term is order `1/(r^2 log^2 r)` while `a_n^3` is order `log^(3/2)(r)/r^6` (up to fixed and polynomial kappa factors). Thus the displayed seed score/gain/mu margins and the first-update certificate path do not hold uniformly. The premise is currently an unproved theorem-facing condition, not a consequence of the P hierarchy.
2. **The U3 mixed-support dominance is likewise not discharged at the current scale.** The sketch lower-bounds the mixed selector by `P^(-2D_mu) ||T||_F a_n^6/256` and then says the fixed exponent hierarchy dominates `Gamma_star(nu_real^2+r nu_real^3+b_s)+sqrt(r)e_I`. Since `P` grows with `n`, this lower bound becomes even smaller in the large-n branch above; the hierarchy offsets do not control the structural `nu_real^2` term. Without this comparison, positive-Hessian acceptance cannot exclude mixed support, so the generated assignment and all downstream LP/tape outputs are not unconditional.

Both issues are same-setting sketch/interface defects. They can be repaired without changing the objective, algorithm, primitive assumptions, rank, probability modes, metric, success criterion, or exact baseline, by using a defect-adaptive analysis scale and redoing the local inequalities. They are therefore routed to `/proof-sketch`, not `/subagent-idea-generator`.
