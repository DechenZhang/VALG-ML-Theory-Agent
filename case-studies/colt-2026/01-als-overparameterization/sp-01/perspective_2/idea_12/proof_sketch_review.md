## Blocking Issues

1. Unrestricted-n polynomial-mass obstruction (proof_sketch.md:315-322,
   609-627, 739-755). The binding setting assumes only
   n >= C_dim r log(2r), while the tape has
   N_tx = O(r(nr)^{c_tx} log r) Haar triples. The U3/U5 score and curvature
   inequalities require an angular correlation scale at least comparable to
   the structural defect. On an allowed near-margin C-mode family this scale
   is independent of n (the displayed a_def already has this property).
   Spherical concentration then gives p_star <= exp(-c n a_star^2). For
   sufficiently large allowed n, no fixed polynomial N_tx gives the required
   conditional 1-r^-10 recovery. The sketch honestly records this as a
   blocker, but the formalized goal does not permit the added
   Lambda_star <= C_mass log(nr) condition. This is an
   idea/theorem-contract defect, not a missing proof step.
2. U3 mixed-support margin is not actually discharged by the repaired scale
   (proof_sketch.md:491-526). The displayed Hessian source is
   |a_j a_l|/||T||, with the adaptive threshold giving order a_star^6.
   The only stated defect lower-scale condition is a_def^3 >= 64 mathfrak D.
   When mathfrak D is small, this does not imply a_star^6 dominates the
   defect term. A sixth-root scale or a new single-coefficient curvature
   mechanism is required. The sixth-root repair enlarges the seed window and
   therefore cannot remove blocker 1 under unrestricted n.
3. U5 certificate margin exponent is incomplete (proof_sketch.md:330-341).
   At the displayed seed lower
   bound, Delta F_s/D_s^2 is of order a_star^6/kappa_T^2. The claimed
   lower bound 4P^(-D_mu) requires a_star^3 at least of order
   kappa_T P^(-D_mu/2), whereas a_mu^3 is only
   8 kappa_T P^(-D_mu). This is a repairable exponent/interface issue on a
   restricted branch, but its correction also increases the required scale
   and cannot restore the all-n theorem.
4. Shrinking-prefix typing remains incomplete (proof_sketch.md:83-121,
   547-575). A_s is indexed on U_s, but
   A_{s+1}=A_s+Delta A_s is written without the restriction/zero-extension
   map to U_{s+1}=U_s minus {p_s}, and chi_<s+1 is not explicitly emitted.
   This is a sketch/interface defect for a conditional version, not the
   reason for the idea-level failure.
