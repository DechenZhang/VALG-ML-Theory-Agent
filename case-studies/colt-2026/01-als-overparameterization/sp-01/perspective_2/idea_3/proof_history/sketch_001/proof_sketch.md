# Proof Sketch

## Formalized Setting

The branch uses the exact homogeneous, asymmetric, third-order CP least-squares
protocol in perspective_2/idea_3/setting.md.  The observed tensor is

\[
T=\sum_{j=1}^{r}a_j\otimes b_j\otimes c_j,\qquad
\Phi(X,Y,Z)=\sum_{i=1}^{k}x_i\otimes y_i\otimes z_i,\qquad
F=\frac12\|T-\Phi(X,Y,Z)\|_F^2 .
\]

The factors are Gaussian perturbations of deterministic bases.  The primitive
conditions are assump:base-conditioning, assump:base-kr-isometry,
assump:gaussian-smoothing, assump:dimension-regime,
assump:smoothing-scale, assump:perturbation-margin, assump:rank-regime,
assump:random-tape, and assump:accuracy-range.  Realized norm bounds,
cyclic Khatri--Rao conditioning, the triple-Gram estimate, nonzero target,
and exact-representation cap membership are generated by the instance event
and are not primitive assumptions.

Every nonzero factor triple is re-gauged by the tensor-preserving balanced map
\(\mathcal B\), with common radius
\(g_i=(\|x_i\|\|y_i\|\|z_i\|)^{1/3}\).  A partial-zero triple is mapped to the
joint-zero triple.  The initialization and every epoch refresh use one
pre-sampled finite Haar tape.  Protected current radii are required to stay
below \(R_{\max}=8\kappa\), while unparked probationary current radii are
required to stay below \(2\theta\).  Trial guards are \(2R_{\max}\) and
\(4\theta\), respectively.  An accepted probationary trial reaching
\(2\theta\) is projected once to exactly \(2\theta\), parked, and removed from
the common search for the rest of that epoch.  Project, reset, and pruning
changes are charged to
\[
B_{\mathrm{disc}}\le 140\|T\|_F P^{-59}.
\]

## Formalized Goal

The target is the conditional guarded-cap reachable-landscape and
trajectory-convergence theorem in setting.md.  First derive, with probability
at least \(1-r^{-10}\) over the once-drawn Gaussian instance, the simultaneous
realized norm, pair-Gram, triple-Gram, target-norm, and exact balanced
representation event.  Conditional on every such instance, derive a tape
success event of probability at least \(1-r^{-10}\) for the one finite Haar
tape.  On that event, no line-search or protected-cap failure occurs, every
accepted common step is at least \(P^{-d_{\mathrm{step}}}\) for a fixed
positive integer \(d_{\mathrm{step}}\), and every discrete event is charged
to the displayed budget.

The burn-in conclusion is a generated reachable tube, an injective
residual-correlated protected representative system, a nonnegative
discrete-budgeted Lyapunov decrease or promotion alternative, a positive
protected cap margin, and entry into a same-target regular chart.  The
post-burn-in protected-only objective obeys a balanced-Jacobian PL inequality
and reaches
\[
\|T-\Phi(X,Y,Z)\|_F\le\epsilon\|T\|_F
\]
in a polynomial burn-in plus polynomial-times-\(\log(1/\epsilon)\) refinement
horizon.  The exact zero-residual balanced representation and arbitrary
\(\epsilon\) baseline are preserved.  No landscape statement is made for
cancelling or partially active states outside the generated tube.

## Proof Roadmap

The selected roadmap keeps the homogeneous activation and finite refresh
mechanism, and repairs the prior equality-only cap obstruction by using a
fixed trial guard and a one-time project-and-park transition.  A probationary
component therefore either remains below \(2\theta\), or crosses the fixed
boundary in one accepted trial and is removed from subsequent common searches.
The distance to the boundary cannot generate an unbounded sequence of
shrinking dyadic steps.  The guard also supplies a polynomial smoothness
domain for a common Armijo lower bound.  The projection and refresh operations
are discontinuities in factor coordinates, so their tensor effects are carried
by the explicit finite budget \(B_{\mathrm{disc}}\), not hidden in a later
invariant.

The causal chain is:

1. E0 derives the simultaneous smoothed instance event.
2. Z0 constructs the exact balanced zero-residual reference and cap margins.
3. C0 proves guarded smoothness, Armijo existence, inverse-polynomial steps,
   and a finite project/reset/prune event budget.
4. J0 handles joint-zero and partial-zero states and records the cubic
   activation identity.
5. W0 proves conditional fresh-tape anti-concentration and radial/angular
   proposal growth up to a project-and-park transition.
6. D0 converts proposals into an injective sequential promotion system and
   reduces unresolved target mass.
7. L1 closes a discrete-budgeted Lyapunov epoch recurrence.
8. P1 proves protected persistence, signed noncancellation, leakage control,
   and a strict protected-cap margin.
9. B1 constructs the reachable tube and same-target active chart and proves
   chart preservation and balanced-Jacobian PL.
10. R0 performs protected-only PL refinement and the separate probability and
    runtime conversion with no accuracy floor.

All theorem-facing conditions are derived along this order.  In particular,
the tape success event is only the event that the finite random source obeys
the anti-concentration inequalities after the deterministic bridges have been
proved; it does not assume channel assignments, persistence, chart membership,
or convergence.

## Mechanism-Source And Boundary Stress

### E0: smoothed instance event

- Step ID: step_001.
- Claim class: concentration, conditioning, and nondegeneracy.
- Theorem role: supplies the realized object on which every later tensor
  comparison is made.
- Mechanism source: direct Gaussian concentration for the perturbations,
  Schur-product/Khatri--Rao perturbation bounds, and the primitive margins
  assump:base-conditioning and assump:base-kr-isometry.
- Source-to-claim adequacy: normalized column perturbations are bounded
  uniformly; the pair-Gram perturbations are transferred by the explicit
  \(\rho\kappa^6\) margin, and the triple-Gram estimate follows by
  \(\|G_{ABC}-I\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le1/32\).  The source convention is the
  Euclidean normalized-column convention used in setting.md.
- Residual-to-target adequacy: the produced target is the actual
  unnormalized \(T\); target norm and triple-Gram residuals are compared in
  Frobenius norm with explicit \(31/32,33/32\) factors.
- Key positive/control term or structural source: the base \(1/64\)
  Khatri--Rao margin and Gaussian tail exponent.
- Opposing defect terms: column perturbation, normalization denominators,
  and the union over three modes and \(r\) columns; each is bounded by the
  stated smoothing margin and tail budget.
- Closure/dominance/absorption relation: choose the fixed concentration
  threshold so the total perturbation is at most \(1/64\), yielding \(1/32\);
  union failure is at most \(r^{-10}\).
- Accumulation behavior / scope compatibility: static event, no iterative
  accumulation.
- Obligation locality classification: step-local.
- Noncircular closure status: direct from primitive data and Gaussian laws.
- Entry-state / first-update stress result: nonzero columns are included;
  the zero-probability Gaussian null case is covered by the norm event.
- Baseline conclusion preserved: exact target \(T\) and its balanced
  representation remain unchanged.
- Producer-consumer provenance: step_001 feeds step_002, step_003, and all
  later residual/Gram comparisons.
- Null or boundary regime tested: smallest allowed \(r=2\), perturbations at
  the smoothing-margin threshold, and \(T\ne0\).
- Target conclusion false or theorem-critical obstruction present if source
  vanishes: without the realized Gram margin, noncancellation and the chart
  lower bound have no source.
- Repair route if source is unsupported: strengthen the static base margin or
  record an instance-level blocker; no later step may assume this event.

### C0: guarded globalization and discrete budget

- Step ID: step_003.
- Claim class: smoothness, Armijo existence, lower step bound, cap
  invariance, and finite event accounting.
- Theorem role: exports the step-size and defect interfaces consumed by every
  repeated dynamic.
- Mechanism source: polynomial gradient and Hessian bounds on the fixed
  guarded domain, dyadic Armijo backtracking, and the \(2R_{\max}/4\theta\)
  guard slack in setting.md.
- Source-to-claim adequacy: on a trial domain with radii bounded by
  \(2R_{\max}\) and \(4\theta\), direct differentiation gives
  \(G_{\max}\) and \(L_{\max}\) polynomial in \(P\).  A trial at
  \(\lambda_{\min}\) satisfies both the guard and
  \(F^+\le F-\lambda\|\nabla F\|^2/4\); dyadic maximality gives the accepted
  lower bound.  The protected trial is required to stay below
  \(R_{\max}\), and a derived strict margin is proved by the P1 recurrence.
- Residual-to-target adequacy: cap projection is compared in the actual
  tensor Frobenius norm; one crossing changes a probationary tensor by at
  most \(56a_{\mathrm{act}}\), and resets/pruning by the displayed terms.
- Key positive/control term or structural source: fixed guard slack
  \(4\theta-2\theta=2\theta\), \(2R_{\max}-R_{\max}=R_{\max}\), and
  Armijo descent.
- Opposing defect terms: second-order Taylor remainder, simultaneous
  component coupling, and discrete projection jumps.  The first two are
  absorbed by \(\lambda_{\min}\); the last is excluded from the gradient
  step and charged to \(B_{\mathrm{disc}}\).
- Closure/dominance/absorption relation: with
  \(\lambda\le\min\{R_{\max}/(4G_{\max}),2\theta/(4G_{\max}),
  1/(4L_{\max})\}\), the remainder is at most one half of the linear
  decrease.  Every component is projected at most once per epoch and reset
  at most once, so the cumulative charge is at most \(B_{\mathrm{disc}}\).
- Accumulation behavior / scope compatibility: the accumulated forcing is
  \(D_e\le140\|T\|_FP^{-59}\) per full run; the event count is at most
  \(kS\), and the Lyapunov step L1 absorbs the signed/unsigned parts.
- Obligation locality classification: step-local.
- Noncircular closure status: the step lower bound uses only the fixed trial
  domain; protected strict margin is exported later by P1 and is not used
  to prove the raw trial existence.
- Entry-state / first-update stress result: at initialization
  \(g_i=\delta<\theta\), so the guard is nonempty; at a near-cap state the
  fixed guard remains available even when the current slack tends to zero.
- Baseline conclusion preserved: discrete defects are summable and do not
  alter the exact target or the no-floor refinement.
- Producer-consumer provenance: step_003 feeds steps_004 through step_010.
- Null or boundary regime tested: zero gradient, all parked components, and
  a trial at the guard boundary; zero gradient accepts a zero-change trial,
  while the finite dyadic grid still has a feasible positive trial whenever
  an update is required.
- Target conclusion false or theorem-critical obstruction present if source
  vanishes: without guard slack, the equality-only cap can force
  exponentially small steps, as diagnosed in Idea 2.
- Repair route if source is unsupported: none under Idea 3; a failure would
  be an idea/theorem-contract defect requiring a protocol change.

- Obligation locality classification: step-local.

- Producer-consumer provenance: step_004 feeds W0 and the epoch recurrence

- Null or boundary regime tested: \(R=0\) and zero correlation; both produce

### W0: conditional proposal growth

- Step ID: step_005.
- Claim class: anti-concentration, positivity, radial growth, and angular
  control.
- Theorem role: turns one fresh independent tape block into a
  promotion-ready candidate.
- Mechanism source: a direct current-notation derivation of degree-three Haar
  polynomial anti-concentration (using the Haar sphere measure in
  assump:random-tape), the decomposition
  \(R_e=\sum_{j\in J_e}\tau_jv_j+E_e\), and homogeneous cubic gradient
  identities.  No external theorem is used as a hidden dynamic assumption.
- Source-to-claim adequacy: condition on the full adaptive past; the fresh
  directions remain independent Haar.  A proposal cone has probability at
  least \(p_{\mathrm{prop}}\ge c/(r\log r)\) for each unresolved channel.
  Before any later persistence claim is needed, the current guarded
  coordinates and E0 triple-Gram bound give a coarse
  \(\|E_e\|_F\) estimate; if that estimate is not small enough, the
  no-promotion branch supplies the strict C0/L1 decrease instead.  Thus W0
  never assumes a future chart or persistence event.
- Residual-to-target adequacy: \(v_j\) is the actual target component and
  \(E_e\) is the explicitly split current off-channel, duplicate, and
  discrete-event term.  The coarse bound available before export gives
  \(\langle R_e,v_j\rangle\ge\tau_j-\|E_e\|_F\) at the threshold
  \(\|T\|_FP^{-30}\); L1 and P1 later sharpen the same decomposition but are
  consumers, not producers, of this W0 interface.
- Key positive/control term or structural source: cubic radial speed
  \(g^2s\) and the fresh-tape cone mass.
- Opposing defect terms: angular drift, duplicate components, off-span
  residual, and projection at \(2\theta\).  The first three are bounded
  before the crossing; projection terminates the radial phase.
- Closure/dominance/absorption relation: while \(g<2\theta\),
  \(g^+\ge g+\lambda g^2s/2\); once \(g^+\ge2\theta\), C0 permits one
  projection and no further cap-constrained updates.  With
  \(k/r=\Theta(\sqrt r\log^2r)\), the failure probability for a missing
  proposal in an epoch is at most \(r^{-20}\) after the conditional union
  bound.
- Accumulation behavior / scope compatibility: each proposal is charged to
  one fresh block; no adaptive reuse occurs, and at most S blocks per index
  are consumed.
- Obligation locality classification: step-local.
- Noncircular closure status: the past event is generated by earlier steps;
  conditional independence supplies the new proposal without assuming its
  future promotion.
- Entry-state / first-update stress result: from \(g=\delta\), the first
  positive-score update has a cubic increase; at \(g=\theta\), the same
  inequality remains valid until the guarded crossing.
- Baseline conclusion preserved: proposals are only auxiliary components;
  final refinement still uses the exact target.
- Producer-consumer provenance: step_005 feeds D0 and L1.
- Null or boundary regime tested: unresolved residual score zero, duplicated
  direction, and \(g=2\theta\); these are routed to a different proposal or
  to project-and-park.
- Target conclusion false or theorem-critical obstruction present if source
  vanishes: absent fresh anti-concentration leaves no rank guarantee.
- Repair route if source is unsupported: increase the tape block count or
  weaken the rank claim; neither is needed under the stated rank regime.

### L1: discrete-budgeted Lyapunov closure

- Step ID: step_007.
- Claim class: repeated-scope recurrence, signed descent, and promotion-or-
  decrease closure.
- Theorem role: closes the finite burn-in induction without treating
  persistence or tube membership as assumptions.
- Mechanism source: the explicit potential
  \[
  \mathcal L_e=F_e+\Lambda_uU_e+
  \Lambda_{\mathrm{disc}}\|T\|_F B_{\mathrm{rem},e},
  \]
  where \(B_{\mathrm{rem},e}\) is the unused discrete-event reserve.
- Source-to-claim adequacy: C0 supplies Armijo decrease, D0 supplies mass
  removal, and the reserve term pays for projection/reset/pruning jumps.
- Residual-to-target adequacy: \(F_e\) is the actual objective residual;
  every tensor jump is bounded in the same Frobenius metric by
  \(B_{\mathrm{disc}}\).
- Key positive/control term or structural source: Armijo decrease plus
  \(\Lambda_uU_e\) decrease or a promotion.
- Opposing defect terms: unsigned discrete jumps and angular/off-span
  leakage; choose \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{disc}}\) and the P1 leakage coefficient
  so each is absorbed by the reserve or the mass term.
- Closure/dominance/absorption relation: for a completed epoch outside the
  active chart,
  \[
  \mathcal L_{e+1}\le\mathcal L_e-\|T\|_F^2P^{-d_{\mathrm{dec}}},
  \]
  unless D0 records a new injective promotion.  The reserve decreases by

## Step Specifications

### step_001 (E0)

On the event that all \(3r\) Gaussian perturbations have the prescribed norm
and inner-product deviations, transfer the base pair-Gram margin to the
realized pair Grams.  Apply the Schur-product identity to obtain the triple
Gram bound and then compare \(\|T\|_F^2\) with \(\|\tau\|_2^2\).  The event is
chosen with failure at most \(r^{-10}\), and every constant is independent of
\(\epsilon\).

### step_002 (Z0)

Insert the normalized realized columns with common radius
\(\tau_j^{1/3}\), and fill the remaining \(k-r\) slots with joint zeros.
Balancing preserves each tensor summand exactly.  E0 implies the true radii
are below \(2\kappa<R_{\max}\), while the protocol definitions imply
\(4\theta\le2R_{\max}\).  This is the exact baseline object used in all
same-target comparisons.

### step_003 (C0)

Differentiate the cubic map on the domain with protected trial radii at most
\(2R_{\max}\) and probationary trial radii at most \(4\theta\).  Let
\(G_{\max}\) and \(L_{\max}\) be the resulting polynomial bounds.  A trial
with the displayed lambda_min satisfies both guard inequalities and the
Armijo Taylor inequality.  Dyadic maximality proves the accepted lower bound.
Each crossing is projected once and each component is reset once per epoch;
the tensor changes are charged to B_disc.  The strict protected margin is
left as a persistence output of P1, not assumed here.

### step_004 (J0)

Expand the loss after injecting a radius-t joint-zero triple.  The cubic
correlation is the first nonzero term, while the sixth-order term is bounded
at the seed scale.  Apply the same balancing identity to partial-zero trials.
This gives a valid first-update interface for W0, including the null branch
where the score is nonpositive.

### step_005 (W0)

Condition on the complete adaptive history and expose the next independent
Haar block.  A channel proposal cone simultaneously controls three mode
inner products and angular leakage.  Anti-concentration gives
\(p_{\mathrm{prop}}\ge c/(r\log r)\).  The radial recurrence grows from
\(\delta\) to \(\theta\), and either crosses \(2\theta\) in a guarded trial or
remains a controlled unresolved component.  Projection ends the radial phase,
so the old cap-slack accumulation cannot recur.

### step_006 (D0)

Use the largest residual score to select one candidate and assign it to a
previously unresolved target channel.  Triple-Gram separation bounds the
score contribution of all other channels.  Remove the assigned channel from
the unresolved set and quantify the resulting U_e decrease.  If no score
passes threshold, export the residual lower bound needed by L1's descent
branch.

### step_007 (L1)

Combine the Armijo decrease over the epoch, the U_e reduction from D0, and
the exact event ledger from C0.  Choose fixed weights so projection/reset/
pruning defects are paid by the unused reserve and all leakage terms are
absorbed.  The resulting recurrence is promotion-or-decrease with decrement
\(\|T\|_F^2P^{-d_{\mathrm{dec}}}\), and it is valid for all \(S\) declared
epochs because both U_e and the reserve are finite.

### step_008 (P1)

Maintain one signed protected representative per assigned channel.  Expand
each score into assigned mass, duplicate mass, off-span leakage, and event
defect.  E0 and L1 control the latter three terms.  A signed potential
\(\Psi\) contracts up to a summable forcing, yielding coefficient lower
bounds, angular leakage bounds, and a strict distance from the protected
failure cap.  These are generated invariants for B1, not assumptions.

### step_009 (B1)

Define the reachable tube by the P1 coefficient, direction, radius, and
event-budget inequalities.  After final pruning, map the protected factors
to the same target T and show the frozen tensor is below the chart margin.
Fix signs and permutation labels and compute the balanced Jacobian.  The
triple-Gram margin gives a polynomial singular-value lower bound; the
quadratic chart remainder is absorbed, yielding the protected PL inequality
and preservation under C0 steps.

### step_010 (R0)

Use Armijo and PL to obtain a geometric recurrence for the actual objective.
Choose \(Q_{\mathrm{ref}}\) polynomial in P so that the residual is at most
\(\epsilon\|T\|_F\).  Since the tape and instance events are separate, first
condition on E0 and then apply the tape probability bound.  Count tape
generation, burn-in, dyadic trials, and refinement to obtain polynomial work;
the only epsilon dependence is \(\log(1/\epsilon)\).
