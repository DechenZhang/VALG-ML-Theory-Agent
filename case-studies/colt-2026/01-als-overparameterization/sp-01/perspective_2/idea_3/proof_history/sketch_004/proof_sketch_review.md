## Blocking Issues

1. **W0's relative-winner event has the wrong probability scale.** In the
   equal-weight orthogonal instance, one product-normal magnitude is not a
   constant factor larger than the l1 sum of the other r-1 magnitudes with
   probability `c/(r log r)`. The event is exponentially rare in a power of r,
   so `k=poly(r)` cannot give the claimed block failure. The absolute-value
   conditions also omit the positive signed lead needed by J0/P0.
2. **P0 consumes initial conditions not exported by W0.** W0's displayed cone
   yields competitor ratio at most `1/8`, while P0 starts the declared competitor
   ratio at `P^-24`. Gamma and chi are not normalized back to the W0 variables,
   and the recurrence is not summed to show that its Gamma loss remains below
   the claimed `P^-20` floor over `P^103` updates. D0's physical mass transfer
   is valid, but its high-probability winner input is therefore absent.
3. **B1 does not yet prove PL or discrete chart preservation.** A lower singular
   value for J(h) does not alone lower-bound `J(h)^*R` because the adjoint has an
   ambient nullspace; use the residual expansion and current-J closeness to
   prove the gradient inequality. Entry loss below boundary loss also does not
   by itself prevent a discrete iterate from jumping across the boundary.
   Add an annular coercivity or step-displacement/intermediate-descent bridge.
4. **Source and flow artifacts remain overstated.** The technical survey still
   identifies attempt 2 and lacks attempt 4's mechanisms. The sketch's
   exported-interface and flow tables record `None` for W0, P0, B1, and R0 even
   though their theorem-critical producer relations fail above.
