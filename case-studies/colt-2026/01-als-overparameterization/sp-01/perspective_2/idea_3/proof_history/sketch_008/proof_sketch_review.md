## Blocking Issues

1. **The pre-parking gain inequality has the wrong logical direction.** The
   displayed radial lower bound says a unit of b produces at least a certain
   multiplicative radius increase. It can upper-bound how much b is needed to
   reach `2 theta`; it cannot lower-bound the accumulated b before parking.
   Add a matching radial upper bound and check exact constants against the
   fixed `(40/3) log P` radius ratio before claiming `chi <= P^-8`.
2. **The weighted W0 wrapper and all-candidate filter remain unsupported.**
   Symmetry of a queued projection does not give a uniform
   `abs(eta)/z <= C sqrt(r)` event when Q and the remaining U target differ by
   P powers. P0 also gives no equation showing that every candidate outside a
   mild basin has boundary score below q0/4; mixed candidates remain eligible
   under the global largest-score rule.
3. **B1's adjoint PL line remains invalid.** A smallest singular value for a
   tall J does not imply a lower bound for `J^*R` on ambient R. Expand
   `J(h)^*[-Jstar h-N]` and dominate D/N relative to
   `Jstar^*Jstar h` using the displayed bounds.
4. **B1 still lacks a valid accepted-step annular bridge.** The
   `1/(4Lmax)` term supplies a guaranteed feasible scale and hence a lower
   bound on the largest accepted dyadic; it does not upper-bound that accepted
   lambda. Partial descent for every point up to the accepted endpoint is not
   produced. Prove a fixed eta upper smoothness bound or use annular coercivity
   plus step displacement.
5. **Attempt 7 does not implement the requested repair, and metadata/flow remain
   inconsistent.** The proof-sketch diff from archived attempt 6 changes only
   the attempt number and still points to `sketch_005` as its trigger. The
   survey changes only its current-idea line, while its attempt-5 flow heading
   and content omit the phase split. The proof-sketch
   interface and flow tables record `None` for P0, D0, B1, and R0 despite the
   missing producers above.
