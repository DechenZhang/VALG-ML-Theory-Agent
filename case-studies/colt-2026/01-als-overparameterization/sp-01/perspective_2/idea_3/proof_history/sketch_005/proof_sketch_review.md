## Blocking Issues

1. **The Gamma/chi recurrence cannot prove its own invariant.** With defects
   removed, the displayed Gamma lower recurrence carries the same factor
   `(1-c a_t)` whose integrated gain is required to satisfy
   `A_H >= 80 log P`. Its iterated lower bound decays polynomially in P and
   cannot yield `Gamma_t >= 1/2`. The additive chi-dependent term only worsens
   the bound. A lead-preserving recurrence with a different sign or scale is
   required.
2. **The winner amplification occurs after the decision it is meant to
   justify.** A probationary component is parked at radius `2 theta` until the
   epoch boundary. Target-scale growth and the claimed remaining-H amplification
   occur only after promotion in the next epoch, while D0 needs the global
   winner filter at the previous boundary. Separate and prove a pre-parking
   basin/filter recurrence; use a different next-epoch recurrence for queued
   maturation. The asserted all-non-basin candidate filter also needs an actual
   inequality, not only a label.
3. **B1's PL lower bound is invalid as written.** For a tall Jacobian,
   `sigma_min(J)` does not imply `norm(J^*R) >= sigma_min(J) norm(R)` for an
   ambient R. Expand `J(h)^*[-J_star h-N]` and dominate the D/N terms relative
   to `J_star^*J_star h`.
4. **B1's partial-path premise is not produced by C0.** The
   `1/(4L_max)` term defines a guaranteed feasible lower trial scale; the
   algorithm accepts the largest feasible dyadic and may accept a larger
   lambda. Therefore partial descent for every `lambda' <= lambda` does not
   follow. Prove an upper smoothness cap for every accepted step from the fixed
   eta, or use annular coercivity/step displacement to prevent discrete exit.
5. **The interface tables still overstate closure.** The synchronized survey
   correctly lists W0/P0/B1 as open, but the sketch tables record `None` for
   P0 amplification, D0 winner, B1, and R0. W0's adaptive probability wrapper
   must also account uniformly for unequal target weights and the queued term
   included in eta.
