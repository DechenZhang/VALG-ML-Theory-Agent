## Blocking Issues

1. **W0 does not yet export the target-cone event consumed by D0/L1.** The
   pairwise `P^-32` coordinate gap is not an inequality showing that one
   weighted target contribution dominates the sum of competing targets,
   queued error, `E_e`, angular drift, and self interaction at score `q_0`.
   Moreover, an arbitrary no-cone Haar block cannot deterministically supply a
   uniform `P^-18` integrated gradient certificate. Define one nonanticipating
   tape event, state exactly which blocks it covers, and prove its failure
   probability and score outputs in step_006.
2. **P0 and L1 do not close quantitatively.** The displayed values of
   `lambda`, `q_0`, and `theta` yield the conservative queued-growth scale
   `P^103`, not `P^75`, unless W0 supplies a stronger score. P0 then amplifies
   event loss by `P^12`, while L1 includes only the unscaled C0 reserve and
   nevertheless claims an exact epoch decrement. Repair the queued recurrence,
   event-reserve coefficient, per-epoch charge, final-Q count, and full
   `N_dec+r+1 <= S` exhaustion in steps_007-step_009.
3. **L1 does not produce B0's chart-entry scale.** I0's `P^-32` coarse tensor
   error and P0's stated `P^-38` squared event-forcing budget are not converted
   into balanced coordinate distance `P^-36/4`. `B_disc` alone controls
   historical probationary tensor path/loss, not final protected coordinates.
   Add an explicit protected polishing recurrence over the remaining burn-in
   epochs and a target-error-to-balanced-coordinate transfer before step_010.
4. **B1/R0 lack the basin and public-metric transfers.** PL plus objective
   descent does not by itself prove that a quarter-radius entry cannot cross a
   polynomially ill-conditioned chart boundary. State an energy barrier or a
   direct coordinate contraction with sufficient entry slack. Then convert the
   B0 output into relative Frobenius residual; the current claim that
   `epsilon >= P^-36` stops immediately does not follow from coordinate distance.
5. **The exported-interface and generated-flow tables overstate closure.** They
   record `None` at W0, P0, L1, B0, B1, and R0 despite the missing raw-control,
   accumulation, entry, and metric bridges above. Update those tables and the
   step specifications after the quantitative repairs rather than deferring the
   interfaces to step proofs.
