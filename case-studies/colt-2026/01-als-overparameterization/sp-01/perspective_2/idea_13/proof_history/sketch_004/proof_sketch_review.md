## Blocking Issues

1. **The observable curvature gate is false on an allowed baseline (`setting.md:246-272`, `proof_sketch.md:328-369`).** For the exact orthogonal equal-weight source, the first residual has Frobenius norm `sqrt(r)`, the aligned candidate has score and radial root `s=g^3=1`, and the angular Hessian has minimum eigenvalue `1` (up to the common weight scale). In fact the diagonal tensor has spectral norm one, so every positive-score candidate satisfying the radial test has `g^3=O(1)`; no candidate can inflate the Hessian to the residual scale. Therefore `curv_I <= 1/sqrt(r)`, while the claimed power gap forces the certificate threshold to be at least `1/8`. For large `r` no candidate can pass the required observable certificate, so the tape cannot commit even the first label. This directly contradicts the required exact/no-floor baseline and the unconditional recovery chain. The smallest repair changes the certificate normalization/threshold, the residual definition, the initialization/procedure, or the theorem scope; it is an idea/theorem-contract change, not a sketch-only bridge.
2. **The stated order event does not imply the stated initial ratio (`proof_sketch.md:218-238`).** Since the realized weight ratio is bounded by `2 Gamma_star`, `Y_p >= 4 Gamma_star max Y_j` gives `rho_0 <= 1/2`, not `1/4`. Even granting `rho_0=1/4`, `rho_{l+1} <= 4 rho_l^2 + Delta_ord` with any positive `Delta_ord` is not a contracting invariant at `1/4`; `4(1/4)^2 + Delta_ord > 1/4`. Strengthening the order gap or changing the recurrence is a same-setting sketch repair, but it is a repeated unresolved activation blocker.
3. **The selector witness does not establish a uniform curvature or assignment margin (`proof_sketch.md:340-373`).** One signed mixed tangent gives at most one quadratic-form value. It does not prove a lower bound on the full Hessian spectrum, and the `sign` of a zero mixed block is undefined. The displayed `m_mix` scale is not connected to the constant `curv_I` threshold. This is sketch-repairable only after the protocol-level curvature obstruction is removed.
4. **Several public ledger and rate interfaces remain asserted rather than source-adequate (`proof_sketch.md:385-443`, `454-471`).** The `LP` record is not consistently typed, the one-step `Delta A_s`/`h_s` bounds and Neumann gaps are not derived from the exported candidate controls, and the exposed `Gamma_star` dependence is absent from the claimed smallness inequalities. These are ordinary `/proof-sketch` repairs, but they cannot salvage the invalid certificate.
