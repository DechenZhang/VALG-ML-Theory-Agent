## Blocking Issues

1. **Coefficient-relative `C-rel` is still not an exported feasible interface (proof_sketch.md:185-227, 245-257, 419-421, 434-441).** The revision now names `step_002a` and the finite accumulation, but it never defines the signed normalized coefficients or `rho_0`, and it gives no raw bound for `xi_str` or `xi_hist`. The available setting bound is absolute, while `Delta_ord` decreases as `1/n`; the allowed C-mode Gram defect and a history tensor are not shown to gain the extra `1/n` factor. Thus `C-rel` cannot be classified as `step-local` under unrestricted `n`; it is an explicit go/no-go blocker, not an assumption. Smallest repair: either prove a target-frame coefficient-relative estimate with all residual terms and a finite absorption budget, or absorb the fixed C/history leakage into the ideal cyclic map and replace `Delta_ord` by a scale that remains dimension-free. If a proof shows an irreducible absolute defect for an allowed family such as `n=r^4`, then same-setting repair is impossible and the next route is `/subagent-idea-generator`; this review does not make that stronger conclusion without the proof.
2. **Finite Armijo/radial coupling has an incorrect work/error interface (proof_sketch.md:228-240, 399-401).** The setting allows up to `H_pow` accepted updates, each with up to `H_pow` backtracking trials, for each of three modes and each of `L_pow` cycles.
