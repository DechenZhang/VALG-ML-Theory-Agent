   interface.** The exact J update and honest global budget may be valid, but
   the formalized goal explicitly requires the false local audit. Later

   `e h+e^2=O(h^4+e h^2)` for all allowed `h>0`. A valid repair must change
   the local conclusion, the charge/profile definition, or primitive source
   conditions; each is a theorem-contract change.
