The primitive event gives only an upper bound on `nu_real`; it permits arbitrarily small positive `nu_real` (and the exact limiting orthogonal case), while `(J-z)` retains positive off-diagonal padding of order `e`. No fixed hidden constant can absorb `sqrt(r)e` into `nu^2` in that regime. This blocks the declared row interface and its chart/rate consumers, but it is a same-setting sketch/interface issue if the raw term is retained or a derived zero/small-`nu` case is supplied.

The revision should repeat the isolated scope and explain how an embedded larger block is charged by the global `nu_real` ledger, so the `eta_0 h^2` term is not silently treated as `O(h^4)` for an unrelated larger ambient `nu`.
