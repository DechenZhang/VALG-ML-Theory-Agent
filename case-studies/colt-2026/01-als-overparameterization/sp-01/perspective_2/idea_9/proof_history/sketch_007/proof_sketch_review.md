## Blocking Issues

1. **`Act-eq` has a first-order source-frame mismatch (proof sketch lines
   859--877).** With `I=emptyset`, modes B and C orthogonal, and mode A's
   normalized two-label Gram having off-diagonal `h=nu_real`, the exact atom
   `u=m_(1,A), v=m_(1,B), w=m_(1,C)` is a valid certificate for sufficiently
   small numerical tolerance. In the proposed orthonormal frame,
   `a=Mhat_A^T u=G_A^(1/2)e_1`, so `a_2=Theta(h)`, while `b_2=c_2=0`.
   Therefore the second coordinate of `(Act-eq)` has size `Theta(tau_min h)`.
   The declared `eps_act` is `O(tau_max(h^2+r h^3+sqrt(r)e))`; on the derived
   `E_lb` branch choose `h=P^(-D_lb)` and `D_stat>D_lb`, and the claimed bound
   fails for large `P`. This invalidates the source-to-claim match, the
   adaptive partition, the singleton score bridge, and the exchange witness.
   Smallest repair: replace the orthonormalized coordinates by a dual/current
   realized-frame calculation, or retain the exact Gram coupling and prove a
   target-scale cancellation. Merely enlarging `eps_act` to `O(h)` would alter
   the theorem-facing isolation scale.
2. **The support/sign and finite-tangent margin is still only asserted
   (lines 888--941).** `W_L>=s^parallel-4 eps_act` does not by itself prove the
   stated exhaustive common-support/low-score/one-mode-straddling partition,
