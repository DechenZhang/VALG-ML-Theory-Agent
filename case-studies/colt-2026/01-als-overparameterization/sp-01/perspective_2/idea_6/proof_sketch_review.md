## Blocking Issues

1. **The binding pair-local (Led-2) claim is false.** Location:
   setting.md (Led-2) and proof_sketch.md Blocker A, equations (A.1)--(A.5).
   The allowed three-label family has \(\omega_{13}=0\) but an exact
   separated certificate near label 1 with mode-\(A\) error correlation
   \(h^3+O(h^5)\) against unresolved label 3. Large allowed \(n\) makes
   smoothing-induced direct pair terms and \(P^{-D_{\rm stat}}\) smaller
   than this structural chain for every fixed ledger constant and exponent.
   Smallest repair: /subagent-idea-generator.
2. **The generated-output chain fails at the first transition.** The empty
   ledger is valid, but a first certificate cannot export the required
   nonempty ledger. Exact rollback removes only failed probes. Consequently
   valid commits, induction, tape amplification, chart entry, and the public
   theorem have no legal producer.
3. **No same-setting sketch repair exists.** Adding a transitive/cubic term
   changes the theorem-facing ledger and its (Led-4) consumer; imposing a
   graph-transitivity condition changes primitive assumptions/source scope.
   Constants, exponents, line-search analysis, seed events, and step
   decomposition leave the exact \(h^3\not\lesssim0\) comparison unchanged.
4. **Reusable local modules are insufficient for acceptance.** Static
   concentration, weighted leakage, radial bracketing, exact rollback, and
   conditional local PL remain plausible, but cannot form an end-to-end proof
   while the first dynamic export is false.

   \(\sum_t|\gamma^B_{jt}\gamma^C_{jt}\gamma^A_{t\ell}|\), and it must audit

   \(\omega_{j\ell}\). This narrows the source class and must be recorded as
   a changed assumption/scope in a new idea and setting.
3. **Stress-test the repaired output before proof allocation.** Recheck zero

   one-step and \(r\)-commit accumulation inequality rather than using a
