## Blocking Issues

1. **The binding `(R-update)` is false on an allowed first transition.**
   Location: setting.md `(R-charge)`--`(R-update)` and proof_sketch.md
   `(O.1)`--`(O.8)`. For `p = ell = 1` and unresolved `j = 2`, the exact
   accepted-history increment is `2 h^4 + O(h^6) > 0`, while the declared
   charge is zero under the zero-diagonal convention or only
   `O(P^{-D_stat} h^2 + P^{-2 D_stat})` with generous diagonal padding.
   Large allowed `n` makes the latter strictly smaller. Smallest repair:
   /subagent-idea-generator.
2. **The all-commit invariant has no legal first-transition producer.** The
   one-time global `l1` budget may avoid an extra factor `r`, but it cannot
   bound an actual array coordinate omitted by its one-step majorant.
   Absolute values make the defect persistent, while exact rollback applies
   only to failed probes. Thus `(R-hist-budget)`, `(R-budget)`, and `(R-acc)`
   cannot be consumed noncircularly.
3. **Reachability and probability amplification cannot bypass deterministic
   invalidity.** The counterexample is already an exact separated first
   certificate. Better seeds, exact local oracles, stronger line-search
   estimates, or a larger tape cannot make its required history output true.
4. **The terminal module has no generated entry.** Same-target local PL and
   no-floor Armijo refinement may remain reusable conditional material, but
   the current discovery chain does not place the actual committed tuple in
   its chart. Replacing the theorem by conditional terminal convergence
   would weaken the formalized goal.
5. **A same-setting sketch repair cannot preserve the binding target.** The
   correct tested-mode majorant is diagonal-aware. Replacing `H_M` by `J_M`,
   deleting the `ell = p` coordinate, adding a new diagonal mechanism, or
   weakening `(R-update)` changes the formalized generated-output target or
   its success criterion. The valid static resolvent prefix does not supply
   the missing identity.
