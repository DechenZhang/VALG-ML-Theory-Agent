\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

All tensor spaces carry their Euclidean, equivalently Frobenius, inner
products.  Orthogonal projections are denoted by $P_E$, and tensor products of
mode maps act in the order fixed in Section~\ref{sec:setup}.  The notation
$\kappa_1=2\kappa^2$, $C_{\rm CP}(\kappa,R)$, $E_\star$, and the events
$\mathcal E_{\rm init\_norm}$ and $\mathcal C_{\rm path}$ are those defined in
the setup.  They are the only auxiliary quantities needed in the theorem
statement.  All orientation, quotient, truncation, and Taylor-expansion
objects used in the proof are introduced locally in the appendix.
