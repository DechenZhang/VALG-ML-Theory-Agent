## Added Dimensions

- Base-factor interpretation: use the source prose's well-conditioned-base reading, uniformly over deterministic triples whose column-normalized factors have condition number at most a fixed `kappa`; the arbitrary-base formal reading remains an explicit residual gap.
- Parallel protocol: use a fixed relaxed Jacobi sweep with `eta=1/2`, Moore--Penrose minimum-Frobenius-norm block solves, and a product-preserving component gauge. The relaxation is a protocol repair justified by the scalar unrelaxed-Jacobi stress test below, not a hidden regularizer.
- Conditional interface: expose a directly checkable initial Khatri--Rao deficit and a trajectory regularity certificate (Gram conditioning plus finite represented-tensor step energy). The theorem is conditional on this certificate; it does not assume an all-time residual barrier, basin, or trapping invariant.
- Rank and probability: target the universal exponent `c=1/4` for every `r<k<=r^(5/4)`, with probability over the Gaussian smoothing and one independent Gaussian initialization. The unresolved task is to prove that the displayed certificate has a constant lower-bounded probability.

## Setting

- Context: For sufficiently large `r` and `n >= C(kappa,q) r^4 log r`, fit a rank-`r` third-order CP tensor in `R^(n x n x n)` with algorithmic rank `k` in the source interval. The theorem is uniform over the selected deterministic well-conditioned base class and makes no random-base or pathological-single-tensor shortcut.
- Model: `T = sum_{j=1}^r a_j tensor b_j tensor c_j`, with `a_j=bar_a_j+xi^a_j`, `b_j=bar_b_j+xi^b_j`, and `c_j=bar_c_j+xi^c_j`. Each base matrix has column norms in `[kappa^(-1),kappa]` and singular values in `[kappa^(-1),kappa]` after column normalization; otherwise the bases are deterministic and arbitrary.
- Data: Independently over modes and components, `xi^a_j,xi^b_j,xi^c_j` are `N(0,(rho^2/n) I_n)`, with `rho=r^(-q)` for a fixed `q>0`, and are independent of the initialization. This is the source Gaussian-smoothed model under the explicit well-conditioned interpretation.
- Algorithm: Let `U^x_t=Z_t odot Y_t`, `U^y_t=Z_t odot X_t`, and `U^z_t=Y_t odot X_t`, with componentwise Khatri--Rao ordering. Compute all three raw least-squares candidates from the old factors, `Xhat_{t+1}=T_(1)U^x_t(U^{x,T}_tU^x_t)^dagger` and analogously for `Yhat,Zhat`; set `X_{t+1}=(1-eta)X_t+eta Xhat_{t+1}` and analogously for the other modes with fixed `eta=1/2`. The pseudoinverse is the deterministic minimum-Frobenius-norm tie-break. After each sweep, rescale the three columns of each component to a common geometric-mean norm using positive scalings with product one, leaving a zero component zero; this gauge does not change the represented tensor or objective. Initialize every factor entry iid `N(0,1/n)`, independently of smoothing, and run one infinite sequence with no restart or early stopping.
- Regime: Fix `c=1/4` and every integer `r<k<=r^(5/4)`. In latent coefficient coordinates let `D_r=sum_j e_j tensor e_j tensor e_j`; obtain coefficient vectors `alpha_i^t,beta_i^t,gamma_i^t` by the fixed least-squares coordinate maps of the three base matrices, and charge any orthogonal-complement component directly to the objective residual. Let `S_t=span{alpha_i^t tensor beta_i^t tensor gamma_i^t:i in [k]}` and define `Delta_0=dist_F(D_r,S_0)`. For fixed constants `0<gamma<1`, `delta>0`, and `L>0`, define the explicit certificate `C(delta,L,gamma)` to be: (i) `Delta_0>=delta`; (ii) every mode Gram matrix `U^{m,T}_t U^m_t` has spectrum in `[gamma,gamma^(-1)]` after the gauge; and (iii) for `That_t=sum_i x_{i,t} tensor y_{i,t} tensor z_{i,t}`, the represented-tensor step energies `ell_t=||That_{t+1}-That_t||_F^2` obey `sum_t ell_t<=L`. These are observable trajectory conditions, not an assumed residual or basin property. The conditional theorem seeks a deterministic normal-equation lemma that transfers this certificate to a positive objective floor, with an explicit remaining probability obligation for `C` under the stated Gaussian law.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Conditional distributional positive-limit-objective lower bound for a precisely specified relaxed parallel ALS variant.
- Informal statement: There are constants `delta,L,gamma,epsilon>0` (depending only on `kappa,q`) such that, for every admissible base triple and every `r<k<=r^(5/4)`, whenever the stated relaxed Jacobi trajectory satisfies `C(delta,L,gamma)`, the represented objective converges and `lim_{t->infinity} ||T-sum_i x_{i,t} tensor y_{i,t} tensor z_{i,t}||_F^2 >= epsilon ||T||_F^2`. The proof must derive this floor from the initial coefficient deficit, the exact ALS normal equations, and the finite step-energy certificate; it may not assume a residual barrier. If the Gaussian smoothing-plus-initialization law gives `Pr[C(delta,L,gamma)]>=p0>0` uniformly in `r,k`, the conditional result upgrades to the source's constant-probability ALS lower bound. The unrelaxed scalar Jacobi stress test is recorded in `Feasibility` as the reason for fixing the relaxed protocol.

## Notes

- Parent foundation papers: Kolda and Bader, "Tensor Decompositions and Applications" (SIAM Review 2009, DOI:10.1137/07070111X); Arvanitakis, Srinivas, and Vijayaraghavan, "Guarantees for alternating least squares in overparameterized tensor decompositions" (NeurIPS 38, 2025).
- Transfer map: preserves the CP least-squares objective, Gaussian smoothing, random initialization, and Khatri--Rao normal equations from the parent literature; changes the unqualified lower-bound claim to a certificate-conditional theorem and fixes the smallest relaxed Jacobi protocol needed for an objective-limit statement.
- Source alignment: preserves the third-order CP object, `rho=1/poly(r)`, random initialization, the full superlinear rank interval, and the positive-limit objective target. It explicitly chooses the source's well-conditioned-base interpretation and exposes the certificate-probability gap; arbitrary bases and standard unrelaxed Jacobi remain uncovered.
- Progress type: conditional
- Materiality: A full candidate was rejected because the source's arbitrary-base reading admits degenerate factors and standard unrelaxed Jacobi ALS has no supported convergence interface. Under the weakest explicit repairs retained here (well-conditioned bases, fixed relaxation, and a transparent certificate condition), the theorem isolates the exact ALS-specific missing implication: a one-time random Khatri--Rao deficit plus independently checkable Gram/energy controls must force a positive limiting residual. Proving a constant probability for the certificate would resolve the algorithm-specific source gap; even without that probability step, the result is stronger and more honest than assuming the desired residual barrier.
- Significance: This branch preserves the parallel-ALS focus while separating a verifiable coefficient-space interface from the unsupported all-time trapping claim. The scalar recurrence also documents why an unrelaxed simultaneous schedule cannot be silently substituted for the relaxed protocol.
- Feasibility: CP matricization, Moore--Penrose block solutions, gauge invariance, Gaussian initial-margin concentration, and KL convergence for a fixed polynomial map are standard or literature-derived. The only new proof obligation is a deterministic certificate lemma: from `Delta_0>=delta`, the Gram bounds, and `sum_t ell_t<=L`, derive a transfer inequality of the form `dist_F(D_r,S_t) >= delta - C(kappa)(k/r^2)L - C(kappa)rho`, then convert coefficient distance to objective distance through the base conditioning. The certificate itself is not asserted to have constant probability. Its raw inequalities can be checked in three concrete regimes: coordinate orthonormal bases, independent Haar rotations, and normalized iid Gaussian bases on the condition-number event. As a protocol stress test, in the scalar rank-one noiseless case with target scalar `T=1`, unrelaxed simultaneous ALS has normalized product `p_t=x_t y_t z_t` obeying `p_(t+1)=p_t^(-2)`, so a generic initial product does not converge; this exact calculation supports the fixed relaxation without supplying a lower-bound mechanism.
- Consistency: The certificate uses only initial feature geometry, observable Gram spectra, and represented-tensor step energy; it does not package positive loss, basin membership, recurrence, or trapping as a primitive assumption. The balancing gauge preserves every represented rank-one term, and the joint smoothing/initialization probability is explicit. The well-conditioned-base restriction, fixed relaxation, and conditional certificate probability are all marked scope changes, so this branch does not claim a full arbitrary-base resolution or silently promote a conditional statement to constant-probability progress.
