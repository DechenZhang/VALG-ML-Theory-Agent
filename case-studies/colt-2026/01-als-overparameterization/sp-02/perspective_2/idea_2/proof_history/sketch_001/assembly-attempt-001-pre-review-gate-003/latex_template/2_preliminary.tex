\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

Assumption~\ref{assump:base_conditioning} makes each deterministic base
matrix full column rank.  Define the fixed left coordinate maps
\[
\Lambda_A=(\bar A^{\mathsf T}\bar A)^{-1}\bar A^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
\Lambda_B=(\bar B^{\mathsf T}\bar B)^{-1}\bar B^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
\Lambda_C=(\bar C^{\mathsf T}\bar C)^{-1}\bar C^{\mathsf T},
\]
and their modewise tensor product
\[
Q=\Lambda_A\otimes\Lambda_B\otimes\Lambda_C.
\]
The map $Q$ is used in the theorem to compare the ambient residual with a
coefficient-space deficit.  Both coefficient and ambient tensor spaces carry
their Frobenius norms.

For every component and time, set
\[
\alpha_i^t=\Lambda_Ax_{i,t},
\qquad
\beta_i^t=\Lambda_By_{i,t},
\qquad
\gamma_i^t=\Lambda_Cz_{i,t},
\qquad
p_{i,t}=\alpha_i^t\otimes\beta_i^t\otimes\gamma_i^t.
\]
With $e_1,\ldots,e_r$ the standard basis of $\mathbb R^r$, define
\[
D_r=\sum_{j=1}^r e_j\otimes e_j\otimes e_j,
\qquad \|D_r\|_F=\sqrt r,
\]
and
\[
C_t=\sum_{i=1}^k p_{i,t},
\qquad
\mathcal S_t=\operatorname{span}\{p_{i,t}:i\in[k]\},
\qquad
P_t=\operatorname{Proj}_{\mathcal S_t}.
\]
Here $P_t$ is the orthogonal projector in coefficient Frobenius geometry,
including $P_t=0$ if $\mathcal S_t=\{0\}$.  Finally, put
\[
\Delta_0=\operatorname{dist}_F(D_r,\mathcal S_0),
\qquad
E_\rho=QT-D_r.
\]
These quantities are all defined from the setting objects.  In particular,
the adaptive spaces $\mathcal S_t$ are outputs of the unconstrained ALS
trajectory and are not required to remain in $\mathcal S_0$ or in any other
fixed space.

For positive constants $\delta,L_P,\zeta,C_T$ satisfying
\[
L_P<\delta/4,
\qquad
\zeta<\delta/4,
\]
let $\mathsf C_2(\delta,L_P,\zeta,C_T)$ denote exactly the event on which
the following four clauses hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Normalized entry deficit:}
\[
\Delta_0\geq\delta\|D_r\|_F=\delta\sqrt r.
\]
\item \emph{Finite adaptive-projector path:}
\[
\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\|P_{t+1}-P_t\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L_P.
\]
\item \emph{Unsquared finite represented-tensor variation:}
\[
\sum_{t=0}^{\infty}\|\widehat T_{t+1}-\widehat T_t\|_F<\infty.
\]
\item \emph{Relative smoothing and target scale:}
\[
\|E_\rho\|_F\leq\zeta\|D_r\|_F,
\qquad
\|T\|_F\leq C_T\|D_r\|_F,
\]
where $C_T=C_T(\kappa,q)$ is independent of $r,n,k$ and of the admissible
deterministic base triple.
\end{enumerate}
These four clauses are conditional hypotheses of the main theorem, not
primitive assumptions and not claimed consequences of
Assumptions~\ref{assump:dimension}--\ref{assump:independent_initialization}.
They include no Gram condition, factor bound, fixed-subspace restriction,
basin, trapping event, persistent residual premise, positive-loss premise, or
assertion about their own probability.
