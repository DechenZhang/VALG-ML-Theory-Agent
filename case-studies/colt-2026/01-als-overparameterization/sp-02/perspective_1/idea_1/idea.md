# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple:
  - analysis_target: positive_limit_objective_lower_bound
  - model_class: third_order_cp_least_squares
  - data_assumption: gaussian_smoothed_cp_factors
  - regime: r_lt_k_le_r_to_1_plus_c
  - algorithm: iterative_cp_class_containing_als_and_gd

## Added Dimensions

- Method class: use the two-member, explicitly defined Khatri-Rao first-order class consisting of (i) relaxed parallel Jacobi ALS and (ii) balanced full-batch factor gradient descent. The theorem quantifies over both maps and does not infer a class result from one method.
- Base-factor interpretation: resolve the source ambiguity using its prose-level well-conditioned reading, uniformly over every deterministic base triple whose column-normalized factors have condition number at most a fixed `kappa`; no favorable random base is selected.
- ALS protocol: use a simultaneous (Jacobi) sweep with relaxation `lambda_A=1/2`, Moore-Penrose minimum-Frobenius-norm least-squares tie-breaking, no restarts, and a deterministic componentwise balancing gauge after each sweep.
- GD protocol: use full-batch Euclidean factor gradient descent on `F=1/2||T-[[X,Y,Z]]||_F^2` with fixed step `eta_G=eta_0/(n k)` for a universal sufficiently small `eta_0`, followed by the same product-preserving balancing gauge; no restarts or adaptive preconditioning.
- Failure scale: target the universal exponent `c=1/4` and a shared coefficient-space residual barrier for every integer `r < k <= r^(5/4)`.

## Setting

- Context: For sufficiently large `r` and `n >= C_kappa r^2 log r`, study random-initialized fitting of a rank-`r` third-order CP tensor in `R^(n x n x n)` with an algorithmic rank `k` in the source interval. The theorem uses one fixed tensor draw for the two methods and independent initializations for the two trajectories.
- Model: `T = sum_{j=1}^r a_j tensor b_j tensor c_j`, with `A=[a_1,...,a_r]`, `B=[b_1,...,b_r]`, and `C=[c_1,...,c_r]`, and objective `F(X,Y,Z)=1/2||T-sum_{i=1}^k x_i tensor y_i tensor z_i||_F^2`. The deterministic bases `bar A, bar B, bar C` range uniformly over the source prose's well-conditioned class: after column normalization, every mode has singular values in `[kappa^(-1),kappa]` and each original column norm is in `[kappa^(-1),kappa]`. Set `a_j=bar a_j+xi^a_j`, `b_j=bar b_j+xi^b_j`, and `c_j=bar c_j+xi^c_j`.
- Data: All perturbations are mutually independent and `xi^a_j,xi^b_j,xi^c_j ~ N(0,(rho^2/n) I_n)` with `rho=r^(-q)` for any fixed `q>0`; perturbations are independent of all initial factors. This is the stated Gaussian-smoothed model with the base-factor ambiguity made explicit rather than replaced by a random-base assumption.
- Algorithm: For both methods initialize all entries of `X_0,Y_0,Z_0` in `R^(n x k)` iid `N(0,1/n)`. For the ALS member, write `U^x_t=Z_t odot Y_t`, `U^y_t=Z_t odot X_t`, and `U^z_t=Y_t odot X_t`, compute simultaneously
  `Xhat=T_(1) U^x_t (U^{x,T}_t U^x_t)^dagger`, and the analogous `Yhat,Zhat`, then set `X^+=(1-lambda_A)X_t+lambda_A Xhat` and analogously for the other modes. For the GD member take one simultaneous step `X^+=X_t-eta_G grad_X F`, `Y^+=Y_t-eta_G grad_Y F`, `Z^+=Z_t-eta_G grad_Z F`, where `grad_X F=X_t(U^{x,T}_tU^x_t)-T_(1)U^x_t` and the other gradients are the corresponding MTTKRP expressions. After either raw update, rescale each component by positive factors whose product is one so its three mode norms are equal; a zero component remains zero. This gauge leaves the represented tensor and objective unchanged and makes the descent/boundedness interface common to the two maps. The class contains exactly these two specified update maps.
- Regime: Fix `c=1/4` and consider every integer `k` satisfying `r<k<=r^(1+c)`. The constants may depend on `kappa` and `q`, but not on `r,n,k`; the probability is over the shared smoothing and the two independent Gaussian initializations. No early stopping, restart, or assumed basin/trapping event is part of the setting.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Full source-faithful distributional positive-limit-objective lower bound for a rigorously defined class containing both ALS and gradient descent.
- Informal statement: There are `p_0(kappa,q)>0` and `epsilon_0(kappa,q)>0` such that, for all sufficiently large `r`, every admissible deterministic well-conditioned base triple, every `r<k<=r^(5/4)`, and the joint law of the Gaussian smoothing and independent initializations, with probability at least `p_0`, both trajectories in the two-member class have objective values converging and satisfy `lim_{t->infinity} F_M(t) >= epsilon_0 ||T||_F^2` for each method `M` in the class. The proof must derive the positive limit from a Khatri-Rao coefficient-space deficit and descent-controlled leakage, not assume a bad basin, positive residual, or invariant. Under the explicitly selected well-conditioned interpretation of the source's base-factor ambiguity, this is a full universal-c lower bound for both named methods.

## Notes

- Parent foundation papers: Arvanitakis, Srinivas, and Vijayaraghavan, "Guarantees for alternating least squares in overparameterized tensor decompositions" (NeurIPS 38, 2025); Wang, Wu, Lee, Ma, and Ge, "Beyond lazy training for over-parameterized tensor decomposition" (NeurIPS 33, 2020); Bhaskara, Charikar, Moitra, and Vijayaraghavan, "Smoothed analysis of tensor decompositions" (STOC 2014).
- Transfer map: preserves the CP least-squares objective, Gaussian smoothing, random initialization, MTTKRP/Khatri-Rao coordinates, and descent/stability interfaces from the parent papers; changes their upper-bound or one-method convergence questions to a common two-map positive-limit lower bound with explicit tie-breaking, gauge, step size, and joint probability.
- Source alignment: preserves the third-order CP object, `rho=1/poly(r)` smoothing, random initialization, the full interval `r<k<=r^(1+c)`, constant-probability convergence to a strictly positive objective, and conjunctive ALS/GD coverage. The source's arbitrary-versus-well-conditioned base ambiguity is resolved by the prose-level well-conditioned quantifier; no random-factor or pathological-single-tensor shortcut is used.
- Progress type: full
- Materiality: The target directly closes Gap 1 under the stated source interpretation: it would establish one universal superlinear threshold for a class explicitly containing both precisely specified methods, with an actual positive limiting loss and nonvanishing joint probability rather than a stationary-point or finite-time statement.
- Significance: The shared class isolates the unresolved algorithmic reachability bottleneck behind the `O(r^2)` Khatri-Rao-span upper bound and the gradient phase analysis, while preventing an unsupported extrapolation from ALS alone or GD alone.
- Feasibility: CP matricization, MTTKRP identities, relaxed ALS descent, small-step GD descent, component gauge invariance, and Gaussian anti-concentration are standard or literature-derived from Kolda--Bader (2009), Arvanitakis--Srinivas--Vijayaraghavan (2025), Wang--Wu--Lee--Ma--Ge (2020), and Bhaskara--Charikar--Moitra--Vijayaraghavan (2014). The novel theorem-critical mechanism is a joint coefficient-space deficit witness with an ambient residual interface. Let `R_t=span{x_{i,t} tensor y_{i,t} tensor z_{i,t}:i in [k]}` in the full ambient tensor space and define `Delta_t=||Proj_(R_t^perp) T||_F`, with `W_t=Proj_(R_t^perp)T/Delta_t` when `Delta_t>0`. The represented tensor is in `R_t`, so `W_t` is an exact measurable unit-Frobenius residual witness and `Delta_t` is the consumed target interface. Modewise whitening by the well-conditioned base factors maps the planted block to `D_r=sum_j e_j tensor e_j tensor e_j`; anti-concentration lower-bounds the planted contribution to the ambient `Delta_0` without discarding the random initialization components outside the base spans. The exact normal equations and the GD MTTKRP update are then intended to yield the common, noncircular interface
  `Delta_(t+1) >= Delta_t - C (k/r^2) ell_t`,
  `sum_t ell_t <= C_0`, and a smoothing transfer error at most `C rho`, where `ell_t` is the squared represented-tensor step (or its ALS normal-equation analogue) controlled by objective descent. Hence `Delta_infty >= Delta_0-C C_0 k/r^2-C rho`, which is positive for `k<=r^(5/4)` and large `r`; duality transfers it to `lim F_M >= epsilon_0 ||T||_F^2`. Objective convergence follows from the same gauge-bounded descent and analytic/KL limit argument for each fixed map. The novel witness, its coefficient coupling, and its smoothing transfer are to be checked directly in three branch-specific regimes: (i) coordinate orthonormal bases `bar A=bar B=bar C=[e_1,...,e_r]`, (ii) independent Haar rotations of these bases, and (iii) iid Gaussian bases followed by column normalization on the event that all condition numbers are at most `kappa`.
- Consistency: The two update maps use the same tensor, rank, initialization law, and product-preserving gauge, so the ambient witness and its planted-coordinate lower bound have a well-defined producer-consumer path for both methods. The only theorem-facing generated object is the witness/deficit, which is constructed from current factors and derived by the displayed recurrence; no failure event, basin membership, boundedness, recurrence, or positive objective is assumed. The source-control relation is explicit: initial anti-concentration supplies the margin, descent supplies the finite leakage budget, the `k/r^2` coefficient coupling controls accumulation, and `rho` controls the static whitening/smoothing transfer. If that coupling vanished at a null or boundary case, the ambient residual obstruction would remain present even when the planted witness source is inactive, so the boundary is tested rather than hidden. The class is intentionally finite and nonvacuous (one specified ALS map and one specified GD map), and it is distinct from the ALS-only branch because the theorem must establish the same barrier for both update rules.
