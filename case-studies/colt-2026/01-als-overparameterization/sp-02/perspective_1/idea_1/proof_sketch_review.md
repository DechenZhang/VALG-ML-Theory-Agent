## Blocking Issues

1. **Moving-span recurrence is unsupported** (candidate `step_002`, `proof_sketch.md` lines 65--84). Generic rank-one span rotation changes Delta linearly while `ell_t` measures squared represented-sum motion; cancellation makes the mismatch worse. No primitive, cited, direct, or conditional source supplies the required `C_Delta (k/r^2)` interface. This is an idea/theorem-contract defect, not a missing local calculation.
2. **No common all-time descent, boundedness, or convergence source** (candidate `step_003`, lines 86--105). The exact simultaneous Jacobi ALS map can increase the objective on an allowed first update, while balanced fixed-step GD has no primitive factor bound or globally valid smoothness threshold. Changing the map, adding line search/projection/regularization, or adding a new invariant changes the fixed theorem contract.
3. **The absolute smoothing transfer is mis-scaled and target-mismatched** (candidate `step_004`, lines 107--126). On a required coordinate-orthonormal base, first-order perturbations have ambient scale `Theta(sqrt(r) rho)`, and whitening does not identify the consumed adaptive ambient projector. A relative transfer changes the exposed interface in `setting.md`.
4. **Downstream closure is unavailable** (candidate `step_005`, lines 128--147). Without the preceding producers, positivity, objective limits, and a positive-probability intersection for both methods cannot be derived. Pointwise projection duality and marginal initialization events do not discharge the all-time joint theorem.
