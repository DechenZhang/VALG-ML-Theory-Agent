# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple:
  - analysis_target: positive_limit_objective_lower_bound
  - model_class: third_order_cp_least_squares
  - data_assumption: gaussian_smoothed_cp_factors
  - regime: r_lt_k_le_r_to_1_plus_c
  - algorithm: iterative_cp_class_containing_als_and_gd

## Added Dimensions

- Failure-driven repair: retain the valid conditional-on-realized-`T` initial-span calculation from `idea_1`, but replace the moving adaptive span by one fixed initialization subspace; do not reuse the rejected squared represented-sum leakage, common Jacobi descent, or absolute `O(rho)` transfer interfaces.
- Minimal method restriction: constrain only the `X`-mode factors to their Gaussian initialization span. Leave `Y` and `Z` unconstrained. Use sequential exact block ALS and coefficient-parameterized full-batch GD with Armijo backtracking; the single fixed mode is enough for an exact residual witness and avoids an unnecessary all-mode projection.
- Probability and scale: retain the source smoothing, random initialization, joint method probability, and `c=1/4` rank interval. The lower bound is relative to the realized `||T||_F`, so no additive smoothing-transfer term is exposed.

## Setting

- Context: For sufficiently large `r` and `n >= 8 r^(5/4)`, study rank-`r` third-order CP fitting in `R^(n x n x n)` at algorithmic rank `r<k<=r^(5/4)`. Each method receives an independent Gaussian initialization; the smoothed tensor draw is shared when the two method events are intersected.
- Model: `T=sum_{j=1}^r a_j tensor b_j tensor c_j`, with `a_j=bar a_j+xi^a_j`, `b_j=bar b_j+xi^b_j`, and `c_j=bar c_j+xi^c_j`, and `F(X,Y,Z)=1/2||T-sum_{i=1}^k x_i tensor y_i tensor z_i||_F^2`. The deterministic bases are arbitrary fixed `n x r` matrices, resolving the source's formal smoothed-setting interpretation without adding a conditioning restriction.
- Data: The perturbations are mutually independent `N(0,(rho^2/n)I_n)` with `rho=r^(-q)` for fixed `q>0`, and are independent of both initializations. All residual statements use the realized tensor directly and are normalized by `||T||_F`; no absolute `C_rho rho` bridge is assumed.
- Algorithm: For each run draw `G_x,G_y,G_z` with iid entries `N(0,1/n)`, set `Q=orth(G_x)` and `S=range(Q)`, and initialize `X_0=G_x`, `Y_0=G_y`, `Z_0=G_z`. The one-mode constrained ALS member performs a cyclic sweep `X` then `Y` then `Z`: update `X` by the minimum-Frobenius-norm solution of `argmin_{X: col(X) subseteq S} F(X,Y_t,Z_t)`, then update `Y` and `Z` by minimum-Frobenius-norm unconstrained least-squares minimization. For the GD member define `f_Q(U,Y,Z)=F(QU,Y,Z)` and take Euclidean full-batch gradient descent in `(U,Y,Z)`; at each iteration form `V_t=(U_t,Y_t,Z_t)-eta_t grad f_Q(U_t,Y_t,Z_t)`, choose the first `eta_t=2^(-j) eta_max` satisfying `f_Q(V_t)<=f_Q(U_t,Y_t,Z_t)-(eta_t/2)||grad f_Q(U_t,Y_t,Z_t)||_F^2`, and set the new variables to `V_t`, with fixed `eta_max=1`. Thus `X=QU` remains in `S` by parameterization, while `Y,Z` remain ordinary unconstrained factors. Both protocols have no restarts; ALS tie-breaking and GD line search are deterministic.
- Regime: Fix `c=1/4`, all integers `r<k<=r^(5/4)`, and `n>=8r^(5/4)`. Let `P_S=QQ^T` and `P_H=P_S tensor I_n tensor I_n`, the fixed projector onto `H=S tensor R^n tensor R^n`. Conditional on any realized `T`, `S` is a Haar `k`-subspace independent of `T`.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Material partial distributional positive-limit-objective lower bound for a rigorously specified one-mode-constrained ALS/GD class.
- Informal statement: There are universal constants `p_0>=1/4` and `epsilon_0>=1/4` such that, for all sufficiently large `r`, every deterministic base triple in the formal smoothed setting, every `r<k<=r^(5/4)`, and the stated joint law, with probability at least `p_0`, the one-mode constrained ALS and coefficient-parameterized GD objective sequences converge and satisfy `lim_{t->infinity} F_M(t) >= epsilon_0 ||T||_F^2` for each method `M`. Conditional on `T`, Haar isotropy gives `E[||P_H T||_F^2 | T]=(k/n)||T||_F^2`; Markov's inequality gives `||(I-P_H)T||_F^2 >= (1-2k/n)||T||_F^2` with probability at least `1/2` for each independent initialization, and `n>=8r^(5/4)` makes the residual fraction at least `3/4`. Conditional independence gives a constant-probability intersection. Every represented tensor lies in `H`, so the lower bound is a fixed-witness consequence. The result is source-aligned material partial progress, not a claim for unconstrained ALS or unconstrained GD.

## Notes

- Parent foundation papers: Kolda and Bader, "Tensor decompositions and applications" (SIAM Review 2009); Arvanitakis, Srinivas, and Vijayaraghavan, "Guarantees for alternating least squares in overparameterized tensor decompositions" (NeurIPS 38, 2025); Bhaskara, Charikar, Moitra, and Vijayaraghavan, "Smoothed analysis of tensor decompositions" (STOC 2014); Jin, Ge, Netrapalli, Kakade, and Jordan, "How to escape saddle points efficiently" (ICML 2017).
- Transfer map: preserves the CP objective, Gaussian smoothing, random initialization, rank interval, base-factor interpretation, and both-method coverage from `idea_1`; changes only the algorithmic interface to a single fixed-mode subspace constraint, sequential ALS, and Armijo GD, removing the rejected moving-span/descent/absolute-transfer route without retaining the unnecessary all-mode projection or compact ball.
- Source alignment: preserves the source tensor, arbitrary deterministic base matrices, `rho=1/poly(r)`, random initialization, the superlinear rank range, and positive limiting objective for both named method variants. The one-mode initialization-span constraint and sequential/line-search protocol are explicit additional algorithmic restrictions; the residual gap to the unconstrained two-method target is stated rather than hidden.
- Progress type: material_partial
- Materiality: The branch gives a constant-probability positive-limit lower bound for both precisely specified constrained variants and isolates the weakest fixed-witness obstruction currently supported by the failure evidence. It narrows the unresolved question to replacing the imposed one-mode conservation by an endogenous obstruction for unconstrained ALS/GD.
- Significance: A single random `X`-mode span already forces a relative residual of order `1-k/n` while allowing the other two factor modes to adapt freely. This is closer to the source dynamics than an all-mode projected class and directly separates a genuine conservation mechanism from the invalid adaptive-span recurrence.
- Feasibility: Sequential exact block minimization and minimum-norm least-squares selection are standard ALS interfaces from Kolda--Bader (2009); coefficient-parameterized Armijo GD has direct smooth-descent support on each finite iterate from standard nonconvex optimization theory and the smoothness framework of Jin--Ge--Netrapalli--Kakade--Jordan (2017). The novel theorem-critical mechanism is fixed one-mode conservation: `W=(I-P_H)T/||(I-P_H)T||_F` is fixed before iteration, every represented tensor lies in `H`, and `F_t>=1/2||(I-P_H)T||_F^2` for all `t`. Conditional on any realized `T`, Haar-span isotropy gives `E[||P_H T||_F^2|T]=(k/n)||T||_F^2`; Markov's inequality supplies the margin, while sequential ALS and Armijo GD separately supply monotone objective values and hence objective limits. The witness and projection calculation are directly checkable for (i) coordinate orthonormal bases, (ii) independent Haar rotations, and (iii) arbitrary deterministic or normalized iid Gaussian bases; no base-conditioning or smoothing approximation is needed.
- Consistency: The fixed span `S` is generated by primitive initialization and enforced by the `X=Q U` parameterization or the constrained ALS block, so it is not a hidden generated invariant. Exact cyclic ALS decreases `F` at every block update, and the GD backtracking test supplies a signed decrease at every accepted step; objective convergence therefore does not rely on the unsupported common Jacobi descent, factor boundedness, KL on an unbounded map, or a squared path-length charge. The scalar Jacobi first-update counterexample and first-order unconstrained GD span rotation rule out a full same-setting repair, so the one-mode fixed-span protocol is the smallest supported target change. It is weaker than the prior all-mode compact construction and is explicitly labeled material partial; the original unconstrained two-method theorem remains open.
