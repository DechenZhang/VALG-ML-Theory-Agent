\section{Proof Sketch}

The argument separates a static geometric obstruction from the two
method-specific convergence arguments.  First, the Gaussian $X$-initialization
has full column rank almost surely, and its range is a Haar-uniform
$k$-dimensional subspace independent of the shared target.  The constrained
ALS formula and the coefficient parameterization of constrained GD then keep
every represented tensor in the same method-specific tensor subspace; see
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-001-full-rank},
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-001-haar-independence}, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-001-fixed-span}.

Conditionally on an arbitrary realized $T$, Haar symmetry yields
$\mathbb E P_{\mathcal S_M}=(k/n)I_n$.  Hence the expected projected tensor
energy is $(k/n)\|T\|_F^2$.  Markov's inequality gives a probability-$1/2$
event for each method, and conditional independence of the two spans gives a
joint probability of at least $1/4$.  Since $k/n\le1/8$, the joint event forces
at least $3/4$ of the target energy into each fixed orthogonal complement;
this is Proposition~\ref{prop:step-002-joint-event}.

Fixed-span membership gives an exact orthogonal decomposition of the actual
residual.  It follows that every defined objective is at least one half of the
fixed orthogonal-complement energy, with a time-independent normalized witness
whenever that energy is nonzero; see
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-003-pythagorean-floor} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-003-fixed-witness}.

For constrained ALS, compact-SVD least squares proves that all three
pseudoinverse updates are exact minimum-norm block minimizers even for singular
designs.  Their sequential inequalities make the nonnegative objective
monotone, so it has a finite scalar limit.  For constrained GD, the coefficient
objective is polynomial.  At each finite iterate its Hessian is bounded on the
current search segment, which guarantees finite dyadic Armijo acceptance and
monotone objective descent.  Induction gives every finite iterate and again a
finite scalar limit.  These conclusions are stated in
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004-sequential-descent},
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-004-scalar-limit},
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-005-well-defined}, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-005-scalar-limit}.

On the joint projection event, the exact residual-energy constant $3/4$ and
the factor $1/2$ in the objective produce the all-time constant $3/8$.
Passing to the two already established scalar limits loses nothing, and the
tower property preserves the conditional probability $1/4$.  The resulting
quantified statement is Proposition~\ref{prop:step-007-material-partial}.

The fixed-span restriction is essential to this proof.  For an ordinary
unconstrained first update, an explicit outside-span forcing term appears, and
the orthogonal residual becomes the difference between the fixed target
component and a generated tensor component.  Proposition~\ref{prop:step-006-nontransfer}
records this exact non-transfer calculation without making a success or
failure claim for unconstrained dynamics.
