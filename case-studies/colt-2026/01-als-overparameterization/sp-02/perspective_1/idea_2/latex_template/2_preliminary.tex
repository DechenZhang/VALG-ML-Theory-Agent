\section{Preliminaries}

The tensor space is equipped with the Frobenius inner product
$\langle A,B\rangle_F$ and its induced norm.  Matricization only permutes
entries, so it preserves this norm.  For a first-mode subspace
$\mathcal S\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, the orthogonal projector onto
$\mathcal S\otimes\mathbb R^n\otimes\mathbb R^n$ acts on the mode-1
matricization by left multiplication with $P_{\mathcal S}$.

The symbols $S_t^M$ and $F_M(t)$ retain the method superscript because the two
methods use independent initialization spans while sharing the same tensor.
This distinction is essential in the joint probability statement.  All
objective and residual bounds below use the ambient tensor Frobenius norm, and
all horizon-uniform assertions refer to integer iterates $t\ge0$.
