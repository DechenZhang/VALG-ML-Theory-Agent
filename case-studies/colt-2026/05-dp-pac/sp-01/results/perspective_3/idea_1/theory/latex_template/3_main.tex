\section{Main Theorem}\label{sec:main-theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Tagged threshold-product lower bound]\label{thm:main}
There exist absolute constants
\[
a>0,
\qquad c_\delta>0,
\qquad N_0\in\mathbb Z_{\ge2},
\]
for which one may fix
\[
\varepsilon_0=0.1,
\qquad
\alpha_0=\beta_0=2^{-13},
\]
with the following property.  Choose, in this order, integers
$k\ge2$, $N\ge N_0$, and $n\ge1$; a privacy pair
$(\varepsilon,\delta)$; and an arbitrary randomized, computationally
unrestricted, possibly improper map
\[
A:(X_{k,N}\times\{0,1\})^n\longrightarrow\mathcal H_{k,N}.
\]
If Assumptions~\ref{assump:candidate-regime},
\ref{assump:central-dp}, and
\ref{assump:distribution-free-realizable-pac} hold for this same candidate,
then
\[
n\ge a k\log_2^*N.
\tag{1}\label{eq:main-nonasymptotic}
\]

For the diagonal specialization, put
$T_s=\operatorname{Tow}_2(s)$ and
\[
r_0=\min\{s\in\mathbb Z_{\ge2}:T_s\ge N_0\}.
\]
For every integer $r\ge2$,
\[
\VC(C_{r,T_r})=r,
\qquad
\LD(C_{r,T_r})=rT_{r-1},
\qquad
|C_{r,T_r}|=(T_r+1)^r,
\tag{2}\label{eq:main-structure}
\]
and
\[
r-1\le\log_2^*\LD(C_{r,T_r})\le r.
\tag{3}\label{eq:main-ld-logstar}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\frac12r^2
\le
\VC(C_{r,T_r})\log_2^*\LD(C_{r,T_r})
\le r^2.
\tag{4}\label{eq:main-product-scale}
\]
For every $r\ge r_0$, every diagonal candidate with
$k=r$, $N=T_r$ satisfying the same three assumptions obeys
\[
n\ge ar^2
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
n\ge a\,\VC(C_{r,T_r})\log_2^*\LD(C_{r,T_r}).
\tag{5}\label{eq:main-diagonal}
\]
All constants are fixed before $r,k,N,n,\varepsilon,\delta$, and $A$.

The deterministic comparison with the additive expression is
\[
2r-1
\le \VC(C_{r,T_r})+\log_2^*\LD(C_{r,T_r})
\le2r.
\tag{6}\label{eq:main-additive}
\]
At the same time,
\[
r^2=o\!\left(
\VC(C_{r,T_r})2^{\log_2^*\LD(C_{r,T_r})}
\right)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
r^2=o(\log|C_{r,T_r}|).
\tag{7}\label{eq:main-remaining-scales}
\]
\end{theorem}

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is a lower bound for this explicit class family in
the stated candidate regime.  It does not prove the stronger
$\Omega(\VC\,2^{\log_2^*\LD})$ milestone, an
$\Omega(\log|C|)$ lower bound, a universal upper bound, or a matching
combinatorial characterization for every class of finite Littlestone
dimension.  The comparisons in \eqref{eq:main-remaining-scales} concern
expressions on the tower diagonal; they are not upper bounds on the true
sample complexity.
