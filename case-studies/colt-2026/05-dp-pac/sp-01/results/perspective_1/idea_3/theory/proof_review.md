## Formalized Setting

- Basic setup:
  Fix the natural logarithm \(\log\), and fix
  \[
  \log^* t:=\min\{r\in\mathbb N_0:\log_2^{(r)}(t)\le 1\},
  \]
  where \(\log_2^{(0)}(t):=t\) and \(\log_2^{(r)}\) is the \(r\)-fold iterate of the base-two logarithm for \(r\ge1\).

  Let \((X,\Sigma)\) be a measurable instance space and let \(C\subseteq\{0,1\}^X\) be a binary concept class. A partition \(\mathcal P\) of \(X\) is *Cartesian for \(C\)* if, with \(C_B:=\{c|_B:c\in C\}\), the restriction map
  \[
  c\longmapsto (c|_B)_{B\in\mathcal P}
  \]
  is a bijection from \(C\) onto \(\prod_{B\in\mathcal P}C_B\). A Cartesian partition is *finest* if it refines every Cartesian partition of \(X\); it is then canonical up to relabeling.

  Under `assump:canonical-product` and `assump:vc-one-factors`, write the canonical partition as
  \[
  X=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^kX_i,\qquad C_i:=\{c|_{X_i}:c\in C\},
  \]
  put
  \[
  d_i:=\operatorname{LD}(C_i),\qquad
  s_i:=1+\log^*(d_i+1),
  \]
  and define
  \[
  M_{\oplus}(C):=\sum_{i=1}^k s_i,
  \qquad
  \omega_i:=\frac{s_i}{M_{\oplus}(C)}.
  \]
  For each candidate global sample size \(n\in\mathbb N:=\{1,2,\ldots\}\), define the factor budget
  \[
  m_{n,i}:=\max\{8,\lceil4n\omega_i\rceil\}.
  \]

  Under `assump:countably-coded-evaluation`, let \(\Sigma_i:=\{A\cap X_i:A\in\Sigma\}\), define
  \[
  x\equiv_i x'
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  c_i(x)=c_i(x')\ \text{for every }c_i\in C_i,
  \]
  and write \(Q_i:=X_i/{\equiv_i}\) and \(\kappa_i:X_i\to Q_i\) for the quotient map. Give \(Q_i\) its discrete sigma-field \(2^{Q_i}\). Every \(c_i\in C_i\) has a unique quotient representative \(\bar c_i:Q_i\to\{0,1\}\) satisfying \(c_i=\bar c_i\circ\kappa_i\); set
  \[
  \bar C_i:=\{\bar c_i:c_i\in C_i\}\subseteq\{0,1\}^{Q_i}.
  \]
  Since each quotient is countable and each quotient cell is measurable, every target \(c\in C\) is \(\Sigma\)-measurable.
  Equip
  \[
  \mathcal H_i:=\{0,1\}^{Q_i}
  \]
  with its product Borel sigma-field \(\mathscr H_i\), generated by finite evaluation cylinders. Since \(Q_i\) is finite or countable, \((\mathcal H_i,\mathscr H_i)\) is standard Borel; this is a consequence of the quotient assumption and is not a standard-Borel assumption on \((X_i,\Sigma_i)\). Equip \(\mathcal H^{\oplus}:=\prod_{i=1}^k\mathcal H_i\) with \(\mathscr H^{\oplus}:=\bigotimes_i\mathscr H_i\). A quotient tuple \(\bar h=(\bar h_1,\ldots,\bar h_k)\) decodes to the possibly improper hypothesis
  \[
  h_{\bar h}(x):=\bar h_i(\kappa_i(x))\qquad(x\in X_i).
  \]
  This decoded hypothesis is \(\Sigma\)-measurable.

  The learning model is realizable, distribution-free binary PAC learning. A target is an arbitrary tuple \(c=(c_1,\ldots,c_k)\in\prod_iC_i=C\). Let \(D\) be an arbitrary probability measure on \((X,\Sigma)\), let \(\rho_i:=D(X_i)\), and, only when \(\rho_i>0\), let \(D_i:=D(\,\cdot\mid X_i)\) and \(\bar D_i:=(\kappa_i)_\#D_i\). No balance, positive-mass, product-distribution, finite-support, or public-knowledge condition is imposed on \(D\), \((\rho_i)_i\), or \((D_i)_i\). For any \(\Sigma\)-measurable binary hypothesis \(h\), define
  \[
  R_D(h,c):=\Pr_{x\sim D}[h(x)\ne c(x)].
  \]
  In particular, for every quotient tuple \(\bar h\),
  \[
  R_D(h_{\bar h},c)
  =\sum_{i:\rho_i>0}\rho_i
    \Pr_{q\sim\bar D_i}[\bar h_i(q)\ne\bar c_i(q)]
  =\sum_{i=1}^k\sum_{q\in Q_i}
    D(\kappa_i^{-1}(\{q\}))
    \mathbf 1\{\bar h_i(q)\ne\bar c_i(q)\}.
  \]
  Thus quotient pushforward preserves factor and global risk exactly, and the risk is an \(\mathscr H^{\oplus}\)-measurable function of the quotient output. This identity does not restrict \(D\).

  Let \(Z:=X\times\{0,1\}\) with sigma-field \(\Sigma\otimes2^{\{0,1\}}\). Datasets have fixed size and replacement adjacency: \(S,S'\in Z^n\) are adjacent when one record is replaced. An unrestricted randomized learner may use any measurable output space \((\Omega,\mathscr F)\), any Markov kernel \(A_n:Z^n\rightsquigarrow\Omega\), and any decoder \(\omega\mapsto h_\omega\) into the \(\Sigma\)-measurable binary hypotheses, provided that, for every \(r\in\mathbb N\), \(x_1,\ldots,x_r\in X\), and \(b\in\{0,1\}^r\), the finite-evaluation cylinder
  \[
  \{\omega:(h_\omega(x_1),\ldots,h_\omega(x_r))=b\}
  \]
  is in \(\mathscr F\); when a PAC guarantee is asserted, its displayed risk event is also required to be measurable. The learner may be joint across factors, randomized, computationally unbounded, and improper. It is \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP if, for all adjacent \(S,S'\), including nonrealizable datasets, and all \(E\in\mathscr F\),
  \[
  A_n(S,E)\le e^\varepsilon A_n(S',E)+\delta.
  \]

  Fix global accuracy and failure probability
  \[
  \alpha_0=\beta_0=\frac1{16}.
  \]
  Suppressing the fixed measurable-space convention from the notation, define \(\operatorname{SC}_{\varepsilon,\delta}(C)\) as the least \(n\in\mathbb N\) for which an unrestricted \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP learner satisfies, simultaneously for every \(c\in C\) and every probability measure \(D\) on \((X,\Sigma)\),
  \[
  \Pr_{S\sim D_c^n,\,A_n}
  \left[R_D(h_{A_n(S)},c)\le\frac1{16}\right]
  \ge\frac{15}{16},
  \]
  where \(D_c\) is the law of \((x,c(x))\) for \(x\sim D\).

  For the intended upper learner, fix
  \[
  \alpha_{\mathrm{fac}}=\frac1{64},\qquad
  \beta_{\mathrm{fac}}=\frac1{4096},\qquad
  \varepsilon_{\mathrm{fac}}=\frac\varepsilon2,
  \qquad
  \delta_{\mathrm{fac}}=\frac\delta2.
  \]
  Let \(K_Y>0\) denote a universal numerical constant large enough for Yan's VC-one private-median/choosing bounds and the retained fixed-confidence calibration, and set
  \[
  q_i:=\left\lceil
  K_Y\frac{s_i}{\varepsilon}
  \log^2\!\left(\frac{e s_i}{\varepsilon\delta}\right)
  \right\rceil,
  \qquad
  Q_{\oplus}:=\sum_{i=1}^kq_i.
  \]
  Fix, independently of the target and data, a reference \(\bar f_i^\circ\in\bar C_i\), an enumeration of \(Q_i\), and a padding record \(\bar z_i^\circ=(\xi_i^\circ,b_i^\circ)\in Q_i\times\{0,1\}\). Given \(S=((x_j,y_j))_{j=1}^n\), let \(J_i(S):=|\{j:x_j\in X_i\}|\), and form \(\bar T_i(S)\in(Q_i\times\{0,1\})^{q_i}\) from the first \(q_i\) routed records \((\kappa_i(x_j),y_j)\) with \(x_j\in X_i\), in sample order, padding a shortage with copies of \(\bar z_i^\circ\).

  On \(\bar T_i(S)\), run a fixed, permutation-symmetrized, fully totalized version of Yan's VC-one mechanism for \(\bar C_i\), at privacy \((\varepsilon_{\mathrm{fac}},\delta_{\mathrm{fac}})\), with output in \((\mathcal H_i,\mathscr H_i)\). Totalization uses the pre-data enumeration and \(\bar f_i^\circ\) to fix tie-breaking and fallback outputs on inconsistent inputs, empty version spaces or cores, choosing failure, and every other otherwise partial branch. Denote this quotient-first transition rule by \(\bar A_i^{\mathrm{Yan}}\). Use independent internal randomness across factors, output the tuple
  \[
  \bar A_n^{\oplus,Q}(S)
  :=\bigl(\bar A_i^{\mathrm{Yan}}(\bar T_i(S))\bigr)_{i=1}^k
  \in\mathcal H^{\oplus},
  \]
  and decode it piecewise through the \(\kappa_i\). Factor utility may be used only on \(\{J_i(S)\ge q_i\}\), where no padding occurs.

  For later weighted accounting, when \(\rho_i>0\) define the generated bad-factor event
  \[
  \mathsf B_i:=\{J_i<q_i\}
  \cup
  \left\{J_i\ge q_i:\;
  \Pr_{q\sim\bar D_i}
  [\bar A_i^{\mathrm{Yan}}(\bar T_i)(q)\ne\bar c_i(q)]
  >\alpha_{\mathrm{fac}}\right\},
  \]
  set \(\mathsf B_i=\varnothing\) when \(\rho_i=0\), and put
  \[
  W_{\mathrm{bad}}:=\sum_{i=1}^k\rho_i\mathbf1_{\mathsf B_i},
  \qquad
  W_{\mathrm{short}}:=\sum_{i=1}^k\rho_i\mathbf1\{J_i<q_i\}.
  \]
  These are generated proof quantities, not assumptions; the upper proof must control their weighted mass directly for arbitrary \((\rho_i)_i\), without a balance condition or a union bound over all factors.

  The following are named proof obligations, not theorem-facing assumptions: (i) quotienting preserves the full Cartesian target freedom and each factor's VC and Littlestone dimensions, and the product satisfies \(\operatorname{VC}(C)=k\), \(\operatorname{LD}(C)=\sum_i d_i\), and, when all factors are finite, \(\log|C|=\sum_i\log|C_i|\); (ii) on each finite-or-countable discrete quotient sample space, every totalized version-space, core, depth, block-summary, tie-breaking, selector, and output-cylinder probability used by \(\bar A_i^{\mathrm{Yan}}\) is measurable, so the displayed transition rule is a Markov kernel; (iii) this measurable quotient-first wrapper satisfies the exact factor privacy and unpadded factor-utility interfaces at quota \(q_i\), with version-space/core consistency and selector properties derived rather than assumed; (iv) quotient routing, prefix-and-padding, permutation/multiset coupling, pullback, the at-most-two-affected-factor adjacency certificate, and basic composition yield a global \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP kernel on all datasets; (v) the weighted occupancy and utility argument controls \(W_{\mathrm{bad}}\) under every block-mass vector and proves the global PAC clause without balance or finite support; (vi) the ceilings and logarithms give the stated public bound on \(Q_{\oplus}\) with no cardinality term; (vii) the lower proof derives the VC and Alon--Livni--Malliaris--Moran factor interfaces, finite hard priors, weighted adversarial mixture with masses \(\omega_i\), overflow control at \(m_{n,i}\), and the one-use hidden-factor simulation in which each factor input row is inserted into at most one global row, so a replacement changes at most one global row; and (viii) both directions retain their exact \(k=1\) baselines. None of these generated facts, including measurability of a version space, core, selector, learner, or output kernel, may be promoted to an additional assumption.

- Technical assumptions:
  - `assump:canonical-product` (Canonical disjoint Cartesian factorization): \(C\) admits a finite finest Cartesian partition \(\mathcal P_C=\{X_1,\ldots,X_k\}\) of the whole domain \(X\), with \(1\le k<\infty\), and the restriction map identifies \(C\) with the full product \(\prod_{i=1}^kC_i\). This is a static property of \(C\), independent of any learner, dataset, distribution, event, or generated object.
  - `assump:vc-one-factors` (Nonconstant finite-Littlestone VC-one factors): Every \(C_i\) is nonconstant, satisfies \(\operatorname{VC}(C_i)=1\), and has finite \(d_i=\operatorname{LD}(C_i)\). No ordering, finite-domain, finite-cardinality, properness, or computational assumption is imposed.
  - `assump:countably-coded-evaluation` (Finite-or-countable measurable evaluation quotient): Every block \(X_i\) belongs to \(\Sigma\), the evaluation quotient \(Q_i=X_i/{\equiv_i}\) is finite or countable, and every quotient cell \(\kappa_i^{-1}(\{q\})\), \(q\in Q_i\), belongs to \(\Sigma_i\). Equivalently, \(\kappa_i:(X_i,\Sigma_i)\to(Q_i,2^{Q_i})\) is measurable. This is a primitive static condition on the class's evaluation structure. It does not assume measurability, existence, or regularity of any data-dependent version space, core, depth, summary, selector, learner, or generated output, and it does not require \(X_i\) to be finite, countable, countably generated, or standard Borel.
  - `assump:global-privacy-range` (Approximate-DP parameter range): The privacy parameters satisfy \(0<\varepsilon\le1/10\) and \(0<\delta<1\). The upper clause has no further restriction on \(\delta\).
  - `assump:candidate-delta-budget` (Candidate-wise lower-bound budget): For the particular candidate \(n\in\mathbb N\) to which the lower clause is applied,
    \[
    0<\delta\le
    \min\left\{
    \frac{1}{n\log(n+1)},
    \min_{1\le i\le k}
    \frac{c_\delta}{m_{n,i}^2\log(m_{n,i}+1)}
    \right\},
    \]
    where \(c_\delta>0\) is the universal constant in the Alon--Livni--Malliaris--Moran unrestricted lower interface. This is a primitive numerical condition checked separately at the actual candidate \(n\), not a uniform-in-\(n\) schedule or a condition on a realized or generated object.

## Formalized Goal

- Goal statement:
  Prove that there exist universal constants \(C_{\mathrm{up}}\ge1\), \(C_{\mathrm{quota}}\ge1\), and \(c_{\mathrm{low}}>0\) such that, uniformly over every measurable class satisfying `assump:canonical-product`, `assump:vc-one-factors`, and `assump:countably-coded-evaluation`, and every \((\varepsilon,\delta)\) satisfying `assump:global-privacy-range`, the following single conditional two-sided direct-sum theorem holds.

  **Upper clause.** For every \(0<\delta<1\), the specified quotient-first routed rule \(\bar A_n^{\oplus,Q}\) is a measurable Markov kernel into \((\mathcal H^{\oplus},\mathscr H^{\oplus})\), is \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP on every pair of adjacent labeled datasets, and, for every integer
  \[
  n\ge\left\lceil C_{\mathrm{up}}Q_{\oplus}\right\rceil,
  \]
  satisfies
  \[
  \Pr_{S\sim D_c^n,\,\bar A_n^{\oplus,Q}}
  \left[R_D(h_{\bar A_n^{\oplus,Q}(S)},c)\le\frac1{16}\right]
  \ge\frac{15}{16}
  \]
  for every target \(c\in C\) and every probability measure \(D\) on \((X,\Sigma)\). Consequently,
  \[
  \operatorname{SC}_{\varepsilon,\delta}(C)
  \le\left\lceil C_{\mathrm{up}}Q_{\oplus}\right\rceil,
  \qquad
  Q_{\oplus}
  \le C_{\mathrm{quota}}
  \frac{M_{\oplus}(C)}{\varepsilon}
  \log^2\!\frac{eM_{\oplus}(C)}{\varepsilon\delta}.
  \]

  **Candidate-wise lower clause.** For every candidate integer \(n\ge1\) satisfying `assump:candidate-delta-budget`, every \((\varepsilon,\delta)\)-DP learner in the unrestricted measurable finite-evaluation-cylinder model above that attains the \((1/16,1/16)\) PAC guarantee with \(n\) samples must satisfy
  \[
  n\ge c_{\mathrm{low}}M_{\oplus}(C).
  \]
  Equivalently, if an admissible candidate has \(n<c_{\mathrm{low}}M_{\oplus}(C)\), then for every such learner there exist a full-product target \(c\in C\) and an allowed arbitrary-support block-mixture distribution \(D\) with \(D(X_i)=\omega_i\) for which
  \[
  \Pr_{S\sim D_c^n,\,A_n}
  \left[R_D(h_{A_n(S)},c)>\frac1{16}\right]
  >\frac1{16}.
  \]
  No properness, efficiency, quotient-coded-output, or factorwise-learning restriction is permitted in this lower quantifier.

  **Conditional characterization and baseline.** If `assump:candidate-delta-budget` holds at \(n_*=\operatorname{SC}_{\varepsilon,\delta}(C)\), then
  \[
  c_{\mathrm{low}}M_{\oplus}(C)
  \le\operatorname{SC}_{\varepsilon,\delta}(C)
  \le\left\lceil C_{\mathrm{up}}Q_{\oplus}\right\rceil
  \le O\!\left(
  \frac{M_{\oplus}(C)}{\varepsilon}
  \log^2\!\frac{eM_{\oplus}(C)}{\varepsilon\delta}
  \right).
  \]
  At \(k=1\), the upper construction must reduce to the measurable quotient-first Yan factor learner with the same quota order, and the lower construction must reduce to the one-factor VC/Alon--Livni--Malliaris--Moran baseline; neither conclusion may be replaced by a finite-support, set-theoretic-only, proper, or weaker surrogate.

  **Explicit-rate interpretation.** The exposed variables are \(k\), \((d_i,s_i,q_i,Q_i)_i\), \(M_{\oplus}(C)\), \(\varepsilon\), \(\delta\), and the lower candidate \(n\) through \((m_{n,i})_i\). The fixed quantities are the displayed accuracy and confidence constants, the factor privacy split, the logarithm convention, and the universal source constants \(K_Y,c_\delta\). All hidden constants are universal and may depend only on those fixed numerical conventions, never on \(X,\Sigma,C,k,d_i,|C_i|,|Q_i|,D,\rho_i,n,\varepsilon\), or \(\delta\). The utility mode is high probability over the i.i.d. sample and learner randomness, privacy is pointwise in adjacent datasets and measurable output events, and the lower clause is an impossibility statement. The horizon mode is fixed-sample and candidate-wise, with no asymptotic, all-time, or uniform-in-candidate upgrade. The metric is distributional 0-1 risk. No factor cardinality, quotient cardinality, finite-support, balance, properness, or computational dependence is hidden, and \(\operatorname{VC}(C)\), \(\operatorname{LD}(C)\), \(\log^*\operatorname{LD}(C)\), and \(\log|C|\) are not identified.
