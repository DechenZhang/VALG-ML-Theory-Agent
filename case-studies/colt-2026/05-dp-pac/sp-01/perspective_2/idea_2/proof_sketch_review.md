## Review Rationale

The deepest defects precede local proof work. Primary-source inspection shows that RSC is a privacy accountant for already-private computations and that Lyu's present (d^5) learner already uses sparse vector to remove naive composition across stages. Its utility simultaneously depends on reusing one fixed block family, which the proposed disjoint-slice scheduler does not preserve. Consequently the branch has neither a source-compatible utility object nor a raw factor for the promised scheduler amortization to remove. Repair requires changing the procedure or adding a new teacher-count/block-size mechanism, not reorganizing the zero-step sketch. The aligned decision is `IDEA_FAIL`, score 2, retry target `/subagent-idea-generator`, and retry mode `new_idea`.
