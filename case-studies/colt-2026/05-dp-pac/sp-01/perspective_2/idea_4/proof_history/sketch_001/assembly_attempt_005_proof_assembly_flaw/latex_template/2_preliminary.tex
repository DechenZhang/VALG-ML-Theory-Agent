\section{Preliminaries}

The quotient map $\kappa$ and its recordwise extension $T_N$ are the common
interface for the three learners.  Every law releases an element of
$(H_C,\mathcal H_C)$ and applies $\operatorname{Dec}_C$ only after release;
the exact raw--quotient risk identity is Proposition~\ref{prop:step-002-risk}.
Fix a deterministic $\bar c_0\in\bar C$ for totalization.

For $d\ge1$, let
\[
 (k,m,N,p_0,\ldots,p_d,L,B,\tau_{\rm AT},\tau_{\rm SS})
\]
denote the least-feasible positive-branch tuple supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-001-calibration} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-001-teacher}.  Thus $k\ge2$, $N=km$,
$p_r=2^rNd$, the exact source list cap is $L$, and the teacher margin is
\[
 \frac{k}{2}-\tau_{\rm AT}\ge B+\tau_{\rm SS}+2.
\]
The tuple retains all integer ceilings and the fixed source allocations
$\varepsilon_{\rm SS}=\varepsilon/8$ and
$\delta_{\rm SS}=\delta/2$; its finiteness and calibration conditions are
proved in the cited appendix results, rather than imposed as additional
assumptions.

The quotient-first law $K_C^{\mathrm{VC\text{-}Lyu}}$ maps a quotient labeled
sample in $Z_Q^N$ to $H_C$ by using the fixed teacher blocks, the exact valid
source restrictions and essential-SOA lists at stages $r=0,\ldots,d$, one
stopped AboveThreshold transcript with threshold $k/2$, and one Sparse Sample
draw at its first reported stage.  The mechanism uses the independent
Laplace tests and categorical weights specified in
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-010-interfaces}; malformed, empty, or failed states
follow the fixed deterministic continuation and terminate at $\bar c_0$.
For $d=0$, $N=0$ and the law is the Dirac mass at the unique member of
$\bar C$.  Its raw pullback is always defined by
\[
 A_N(s,E):=K_C^{\mathrm{VC\text{-}Lyu}}(T_N(s),E).
\]

The old comparison law $K_C^{\mathrm{old\text{-}Lyu}}$ has the same quotient
input and output interface, fixed-stage mechanisms, and totalization, with
the independent old-arm least-feasible tuple and $d$-based calibration from
Lemma~\ref{lem:step-015-dictionary} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:step-015-teacher}.  Its raw pullback is obtained by
the same composition with $T_{N_{\rm old}}$; invalid or failed paths return
$\bar c_0$.

When $M:=|C|<\infty$, the finite comparison law is the everywhere-defined
quotient exponential-weights kernel
\[
K^{\mathrm{fin}}_{C,N}(\bar s,E)=
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{\bar h\in\bar C\cap E}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\sum_{r=1}^N
 \mathbf1\{\bar h(q_r)\ne y_r\}\right]}
 {\displaystyle\sum_{\bar g\in\bar C}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\sum_{r=1}^N
 \mathbf1\{\bar g(q_r)\ne y_r\}\right]}.
\]
It is defined for every quotient input, including $N=0$, and its raw
pullback is the composition with $T_N$.  If $|C|=\infty$, set
$R_{\mathrm{fin}}=+\infty$ and construct no finite surrogate.  The three
laws therefore share the same output space, decoder, privacy quantifier,
and realizable iid risk mode; the minimum in the main theorem chooses one
complete law before data are observed.
