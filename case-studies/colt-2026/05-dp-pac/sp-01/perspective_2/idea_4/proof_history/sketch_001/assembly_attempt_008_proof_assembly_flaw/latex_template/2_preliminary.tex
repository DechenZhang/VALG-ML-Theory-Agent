\section{Preliminaries}

The quotient map $\kappa$ and its recordwise extension $T_N$ are the common
input interface for all three learners.  Every learner releases an element of
$(H_C,\mathcal H_C)$ and applies $\operatorname{Dec}_C$ only after release.
Fix once and for all a deterministic $\bar c_0\in\bar C$ for every fallback
path.  When $d=0$, $\bar C=\{\bar c_0\}$ and both Littlestone-based laws use
$N=N_{\rm old}=0$ and release $\bar c_0$ deterministically.

We next give complete positive-dimensional definitions.  Fix once and for
all a universal constant $c_{\rm AT}\ge1$ large enough that the
AboveThreshold privacy estimate in Lyu~\citep[Lemma~3.2]{lyu2025} holds,
and fix the admissible universal block constants

\[
 C_{\rm blk}:=144000,
 \qquad C_{\rm o}:=2^{20}.
\]

For $d\ge1$, put

\[
 \beta_{\rm tr}=\beta_{\rm AT}=\beta_{\rm SS}=\beta_{\rm gen}:=\beta/4,
 \qquad
 \delta_{\rm AT}=\delta_{\rm SS}:=\delta/2,
 \qquad \varepsilon_{\rm SS}:=\varepsilon/8,
\]
\[
 g_\delta:=\log(4/\delta),
 \qquad
 \eta:=\frac{\varepsilon}
 {4c_{\rm AT}(\sqrt{g_\delta}+g_\delta)},
 \qquad
 \tau_{\rm AT}:=\eta^{-1}\log\frac{d+1}{\beta_{\rm AT}}.
\]

For each integer $t\ge2$, define the VC-sensitive candidate tuple

\[
 a(t):=v+\log(4t/\beta),
 \qquad
 Q(t):=e+\frac{etd^2a(t)}{\alpha v},
\]
\[
 m(t):=\left\lceil C_{\rm blk}\frac{d^2}{\alpha}
 a(t)\log Q(t)\right\rceil,
 \qquad n(t):=tm(t),
 \qquad p_r(t):=2^rn(t)d\quad(0\le r\le d),
\]
\[
 L(t):=p_d(t)^d2^{d^2},
 \qquad
 B(t):=\left\lceil
 \frac{10\log(L(t)/\delta_{\rm SS})}{\varepsilon_{\rm SS}}
 \right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \tau_{\rm SS}(t):=\varepsilon_{\rm SS}^{-1}
 \log\frac{tL(t)+1}{\beta_{\rm SS}}.
\]

The teacher count is the exact least feasible integer

\[
 k:=\min\left\{t\in\mathbb Z:t\ge2,
 \ \frac t2-\tau_{\rm AT}\ge B(t)+\tau_{\rm SS}(t)+2\right\}.
\]

The existence of this minimum is part of the theorem proved below.  After
the minimization, set

\[
 a:=a(k),\quad Q_{\rm blk}:=Q(k),\quad
 m:=m(k),\quad n_0:=km,\quad N:=n_0,
\]
\[
 p_r:=2^rn_0d\ (0\le r\le d),\quad
 L:=p_d^d2^{d^2},\quad B:=B(k),\quad
 \tau_{\rm SS}:=\tau_{\rm SS}(k).
\]

Thus, in particular,

\[
 a=v+\log(4k/\beta),\qquad
 Q_{\rm blk}=e+\frac{ekd^2a}{\alpha v},\qquad
 m=\left\lceil C_{\rm blk}\frac{d^2}{\alpha}
 a\log Q_{\rm blk}\right\rceil,\qquad N=km.
\]

These scalars define $K_C^{\mathrm{VC\text{-}Lyu}}$ as follows.  Draw one
uniform labeled partition of the quotient master sample into the fixed $k$
blocks of size $m$, and reuse it at every stage $r=0,\ldots,d$.  At stage
$r$, use $p_r=2^rNd$ and exactly the restriction, valid
$(p_r,d)$-decomposition, and essential-SOA list conventions of
Lyu~\citep[Definitions~4.2--4.3, Lemmas~4.1 and~4.3, Corollary~4.1,
and the construction in Theorem~3]{lyu2025}.  Empty restrictions receive
the empty list; deterministic tie-breaking fixes every valid decomposition
and list ordering before the sample is seen.  Invalid, nonfinite,
non-$H_C$, or oversized lists are replaced by the empty list.

For the resulting stage lists $\mathcal L_1^r,\ldots,\mathcal L_k^r$, set

\[
 q_r:=\max_{\bar h\in H_C}
 |\{i\in[k]:\bar h\in\mathcal L_i^r\}|,
\]

with $q_r=0$ when all lists are empty.  Run one stopped AboveThreshold
transcript, drawing independent $Z_r\sim\operatorname{Lap}(1/\eta)$ and
reporting Above exactly when $q_r+Z_r\ge k/2$.  At the first Above stage,
make one Sparse Sample call on the selected lists: each actual union item
$u$ has score $s(u)=|\{i:u\in\mathcal L_i^r\}|$, the distinct failure
symbol $\perp$ has score $B$, and the output mass is proportional to
$\exp(\varepsilon_{\rm SS}s(u))$, exactly as in
Lyu~\citep[Algorithm~1 and Lemma~3.1]{lyu2025}.  An actual union item is
released.  Every empty, invalid, failed, no-success, exhausted, or residual
path releases $\bar c_0$.  This fixes a pointwise law on every quotient
input before sampling; its measurable-kernel realization is the law denoted
$K_C^{\mathrm{VC\text{-}Lyu}}$.

The old comparison arm is independently calibrated.  Set

\[
 \beta_{\rm tr,o}=\beta_{\rm AT,o}=\beta_{\rm SS,o}
 =\beta_{\rm gen,o}:=\beta/4,
 \qquad
 \delta_{\rm AT,o}=\delta_{\rm SS,o}:=\delta/2,
\]
\[
 \varepsilon_{\rm SS,o}:=\varepsilon/8,
 \qquad
 \eta_{\rm o}:=\frac{\varepsilon}
 {4c_{\rm AT}(\sqrt{g_\delta}+g_\delta)},
 \qquad
 \tau_{\rm AT,o}:=\eta_{\rm o}^{-1}
 \log\frac{d+1}{\beta_{\rm AT,o}}.
\]

For every integer $t\ge2$, define

\[
 a_{\rm o}(t):=d+\log(4t/\beta),
 \qquad
 Q_{\rm o}(t):=e+\frac{etd^2a_{\rm o}(t)}{\alpha d},
\]
\[
 m_{\rm o}(t):=\left\lceil C_{\rm o}\frac{d^2}{\alpha}
 a_{\rm o}(t)\log Q_{\rm o}(t)\right\rceil,
 \quad n_{\rm o}(t):=tm_{\rm o}(t),
 \quad p_{\rm o,r}(t):=2^rn_{\rm o}(t)d,
\]
\[
 L_{\rm o}(t):=p_{\rm o,d}(t)^d2^{d^2},
 \quad
 B_{\rm o}(t):=\left\lceil
 \frac{10\log(L_{\rm o}(t)/\delta_{\rm SS,o})}
 {\varepsilon_{\rm SS,o}}
 \right\rceil,
 \quad
 \tau_{\rm SS,o}(t):=\varepsilon_{\rm SS,o}^{-1}
 \log\frac{tL_{\rm o}(t)+1}{\beta_{\rm SS,o}}.
\]

Its least feasible teacher count and exact horizon are

\[
 k_{\rm o}:=\min\left\{t\in\mathbb Z:t\ge2,
 \ \frac t2-\tau_{\rm AT,o}
 \ge B_{\rm o}(t)+\tau_{\rm SS,o}(t)+2\right\},
\]
\[
 a_{\rm o}:=a_{\rm o}(k_{\rm o}),\quad
 Q_{\rm o}:=Q_{\rm o}(k_{\rm o}),\quad
 m_{\rm o}:=m_{\rm o}(k_{\rm o}),\quad
 N_{\rm old}:=n_{\rm o}:=k_{\rm o}m_{\rm o},
\]
\[
 p_{\rm o,r}:=2^rn_{\rm o}d\quad(0\le r\le d),\quad
 L_{\rm o}:=L_{\rm o}(k_{\rm o}),\quad
 B_{\rm o}:=B_{\rm o}(k_{\rm o}),\quad
 \tau_{\rm SS,o}:=\tau_{\rm SS,o}(k_{\rm o}).
\]

The law $K_C^{\mathrm{old\text{-}Lyu}}$ is the same quotient-first,
fixed-block, $d+1$-stage procedure just defined, using this old tuple and
Lyu's original $d$-based restrictions and essential-SOA lists from the
same cited construction.  It uses one stopped AboveThreshold transcript,
one selected-stage Sparse Sample call, and the identical pre-sampling
totalization to $\bar c_0$. Its scores, Laplace scale, threshold, and
categorical weights are the preceding VC-arm formulas with
$(k,p_r,L,B,\eta,\varepsilon_{\rm SS},\tau_{\rm SS})$ replaced by
$(k_{\rm o},p_{\rm o,r},L_{\rm o},B_{\rm o},\eta_{\rm o},
\varepsilon_{\rm SS,o},\tau_{\rm SS,o})$.
Hence both named laws are exact pointwise procedures rather than aliases
for appendix conclusions.

When $M:=|C|<\infty$, the finite comparison law is the everywhere-defined
quotient exponential-weights kernel

\[
K^{\mathrm{fin}}_{C,N}(\bar s,E)=
\frac{\displaystyle\sum_{\bar h\in\bar C\cap E}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\sum_{r=1}^N
 \mathbf1\{\bar h(q_r)\ne y_r\}\right]}
 {\displaystyle\sum_{\bar g\in\bar C}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac{\varepsilon}{2}\sum_{r=1}^N
 \mathbf1\{\bar g(q_r)\ne y_r\}\right]}.
\]

Its certified horizon is
\[
 N_{\rm fin}:=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&d=0,\\[2pt]
 \displaystyle\left\lceil
 \frac{4(\log M+\log(1/\beta))}{\varepsilon\alpha}
 \right\rceil,&d\ge1.
 \end{cases}
\]
It is defined for every quotient input, including $N=0$, and its raw
pullback is its composition with $T_N$.  If $|C|=\infty$, set
$R_{\mathrm{fin}}=+\infty$ and construct no finite surrogate.  All three
arms therefore share the output space, decoder, privacy quantifier, and
realizable iid population-risk mode; the minimum theorem selects one whole
law before observing the data.
