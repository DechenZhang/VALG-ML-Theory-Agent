\section{Preliminaries}

The quotient map $\kappa$ is the only interface between the raw and coded
experiments.  We use $T_N$ for its recordwise extension and use
$\operatorname{Dec}_C$ only after a private release.  The exact equality
between raw and quotient risks is proved in Proposition~\ref{prop:step-002-risk}.

For $d\ge1$, set $\gamma=\alpha/16$ and split the confidence budget as
\[
 \beta_{\mathrm{tr}}=\beta_{\mathrm{AT}}=\beta_{\mathrm{SS}}
 =\beta_{\mathrm{gen}}=\beta/4,qquad
 \delta_{\mathrm{AT}}=\delta_{\mathrm{SS}}=\delta/2.
\]
The quotient-first VC-sensitive law $K_C^{\mathrm{VC\text{-}Lyu}}$ uses the
fixed-block source construction on $\bar C$: it chooses the least feasible
teacher count $k\ge2$, block size $m$, master size $n_0=km$, and $N=n_0$;
forms the $d+1$ exact source restrictions and essential lists on quotient
records; runs one AboveThreshold process over the stage scores; and invokes
Sparse Sample once after the first successful stage.  Empty, invalid, or
failed states are totalized to a fixed $\bar c_0\in\bar C$.  For $d=0$ it
uses $N=0$ and the unique quotient concept.  These scalar choices and all
their ceiling inequalities are proved in the first appendix subsection and
are not additional assumptions.

For a fixed quotient master sample $\bar S$ and a data-independent uniform
partition into $k$ blocks, write
\[
 \mathcal E_{\bar C}(\bar S)=
 \left\{(\mathbf 1\{\bar c(q_r)\ne y_r\})_{r=1}^{n_0}:\bar c\in\bar C\right\},
 \qquad
 \Pi_{\bar C}(n)=\max_{\bar S\in Z_Q^n}|\mathcal E_{\bar C}(\bar S)|.
\]
This notation is introduced to state the finite VC trace bound; it is a
finite-sample trace family, not a measurability assumption on an infinite
class.  The appendix proves
$|\mathcal E_{\bar C}(\bar S)|\le(en_0/v)^v$ on the positive branch.

When an actual output is produced, an analysis-only occurrence mark
$J\in\{0,1,\ldots,k\}$ records one producer block whose all-stage candidate
union contains the output; $J=0$ is reserved for fallback paths.  The marked
law is used only for the finite holdout proof.  Proposition~\ref{prop:step-004-projection}
proves that summing over $J$ gives exactly the released marginal, so the
mark is not part of the learner output or its privacy interface.

The two comparison laws use the same quotient input, output space, decoder,
and raw pullback.  $K_C^{\mathrm{old\text{-}Lyu}}$ uses the source coarse
$d$-trace calibration, while $K_C^{\mathrm{fin}}$ is the finite-class
exponential-weight law on $\bar C$ when $|C|<\infty$ and is disabled by the
convention $R_{\mathrm{fin}}=+\infty$ otherwise.  Their independent privacy
and PAC certificates are proved before the minimum is taken.
