\section{Proof Sketch}

The proof starts with the static evaluation quotient.  The quotient
factorization, preservation of VC and Littlestone dimensions, measurable
record map, iid pushforward, and exact decoder-risk identity are established
in Propositions~\ref{prop:step-002-factorization}--\ref{prop:step-002-risk}.
Because the quotient input is finite or countably discrete, the pointwise
totalized procedure promotes to a Markov kernel; the corresponding event and
output coordinates are measurable by Propositions~\ref{prop:step-003-coding}
and~\ref{prop:step-003-events}.

On the positive-dimensional branch, the scalar dictionary and all fallback
paths are fixed before sampling.  The trace family is bounded by Sauer--Shelah
and the fixed-trace without-replacement estimates produce the simultaneous
source event.  The ceiling-aware fixed point charges its failure to the
allocated confidence share.  The source/current stage map then gives exact
factor-two restriction inclusions, finite essential lists, and irreducible
leaf witnesses.  A finite DDim potential drops by one at every accurately
Below stage; at dimension zero the realizable target supplies a common list
item, so a score-$k$ stage is reached within the declared finite horizon.

The one AboveThreshold transcript and one Sparse Sample call use the teacher
margin to produce an actual quotient function except on their two allocated
mechanism events.  The list-locality argument proves all-input privacy,
independently of realizability.  The selected function has an exact SOA
identity and master empirical error at most $\alpha/8$.  An analysis-only
finite occurrence mark identifies a producer block; its projection is exact,
and the independent complement of that block gives the one-sided holdout
bound.  The finite union over marks and candidate lists yields the quotient
PAC guarantee, which transfers to raw risk by the quotient identity.

Finally, the VC-arm auxiliaries are eliminated with explicit inequalities,
the old-Lyu arm is normalized independently, and the finite exponential-weight
arm is proved on the same quotient interface.  Only after all three arms have
the same output space, decoder, privacy mode, and PAC mode is the deterministic
minimum taken.  The full derivations appear in the following appendix
subsections; the final proof cites their theorem-style results and performs
only this direct composition.
