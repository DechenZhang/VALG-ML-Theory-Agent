\begin{abstract}
We prove a nonasymptotic sample lower bound for central approximate-private,
distribution-free realizable PAC learning of tagged products of finite
threshold classes.  At fixed constant accuracy, confidence, and
$\varepsilon$, with $\delta$ in the stated candidate regime, the bound is
$\Omega(k\log_2^*N)$.  On the tower diagonal
$k=r$, $N=\operatorname{Tow}_2(r)$, it becomes
$\Omega(r^2)=\Omega(\VC\log_2^*\LD)$ for unrestricted randomized improper
learners.  The result separates this family from the generic additive scale,
but it does not establish the stronger exponential or log-cardinality lower
bounds posed by the general open problem.
\end{abstract}
