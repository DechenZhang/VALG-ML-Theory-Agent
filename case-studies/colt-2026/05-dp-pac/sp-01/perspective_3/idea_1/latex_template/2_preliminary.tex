\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:preliminaries}

Let $\Delta([N])$ be the simplex of probability laws on $[N]$, and set
\[
\mathcal I_N=[N+1]\times\Delta([N]).
\]
For $(t,Q)\in\mathcal I_N$, let $Q^{\tau_t}$ be the law of
$(X,\tau_t(X))$ for $X\sim Q$, and define the one-block population risk
\[
R_Q(g,\tau_t)=\Pr_{X\sim Q}\{g(X)\ne\tau_t(X)\},
\qquad g\in\{0,1\}^{[N]}.
\]
For a deterministic vector
$((t_i,Q_i))_{i=1}^k\in\mathcal I_N^k$, write
\[
P_{\boldsymbol Q}(i,x)=\frac1kQ_i(x),
\qquad
c_{\boldsymbol t}(i,x)=\tau_{t_i}(x).
\]
This notation is used for fixed product instances; when the vector itself is
random, every i.i.d. assertion is conditional on its realized value.

For $h\in\mathcal H_{k,N}$ and $j\in[k]$, define the block restriction
\[
(D_jh)(x)=h(j,x),\qquad x\in[N],
\]
and, for deterministic $\boldsymbol t,\boldsymbol Q$, the block error
\[
e_j(h;\boldsymbol t,\boldsymbol Q)
=\sum_{x=1}^NQ_j(x)\mathbf 1\{h(j,x)\ne\tau_{t_j}(x)\}.
\]

The finite spaces used in the simulator are
\[
\mathsf Z_M=([N]\times\{0,1\})^M,
\qquad
\mathsf S_n=(X_{k,N}\times\{0,1\})^n,
\qquad
\mathsf G_N=\{0,1\}^{[N]}.
\]
A randomized map between any of these finite spaces is identified with its
stochastic kernel.  All differential-privacy comparisons use ordered
replacement-adjacent input pairs and arbitrary output events.

Finally, $\VC(C)$ and $\LD(C)$ denote the VC and Littlestone dimensions of a
binary class $C$.  The proof below uses complete binary instance trees for
the latter and gives the needed tree and mistake-budget arguments directly.
