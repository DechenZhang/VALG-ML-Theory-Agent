## Review Rationale

The sketch is ready for step-level proof work. Its central amplification is not based on an algorithm-dependent hard instance: compact minimax produces one finite prior hard for every private one-block kernel, including the later simulator. The ideal experiment then makes the hidden coordinate independent of the full iid instance vector and of the conditional product-sample/output law, so arbitrary tag asymmetry does not break the average-risk identity. The executable experiment differs only on a rigorously bounded overflow event and maps one input replacement to at most one global replacement pathwise. These interfaces preserve the verified unrestricted ALMM theorem, the fixed-instance PAC quantifier, and every exposed rate and boundary convention. All remaining work is local derivation of the 16 already source-supported claims, so `ACCEPTED` with score 9 is the aligned outcome.
