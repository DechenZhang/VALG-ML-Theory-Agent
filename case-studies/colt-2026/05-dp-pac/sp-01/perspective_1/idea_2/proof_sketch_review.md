## Blocking Issues

Downstream, `A_i^{Yan}`, `A_n^oplus`, the pointwise DP output-event quantifier, and the upper PAC probability are not theorem-facing objects. The smallest repair is an idea/theorem-contract change, not a later proof step.

2. **The obstruction already breaks the required baseline (setting.md:53-72; proof_sketch.md:73, 265, 609).** It occurs at `k=1`, `VC=LD=1`, before routing, occupancy, composition, or factor aggregation. A finite-support restriction, conditional utility statement, or set-theoretic-only learner would replace the inherited upper baseline and hence weaken the exact goal. This rules out a target-preserving same-setting repair.

3. **Upper generated-output consumers are consequently unauthorized (proof_sketch.md:610-616, 632-639, 648).** The stable IDs and numerical interfaces are present, but `step_003`, `step_005`, `step_006`, and the upper part of `step_015` cannot be classified as theorem-facing proof steps while their sole factor-kernel producer is unavailable. This is a dependency consequence of issue 1, not an independent arithmetic or flow typo.
