\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:preliminaries}

For $x>1$, define $\log_2^{(0)}x=x$ and
$\log_2^{(r+1)}x=\log_2(\log_2^{(r)}x)$ whenever the latter is defined.
We use
\[
\log^*x:=\min\{r\geq0:\log_2^{(r)}x\leq1\}.
\]
This is the only asymptotic scale in the main theorem.  The lower bound is
deterministic once the fixed-sample PAC and privacy antecedents hold; the
probability in the antecedent is the joint probability over the iid sample
and the learner's internal randomness, and the relevant metric is zero-one
risk $L_Q$.
