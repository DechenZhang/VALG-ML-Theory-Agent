\section{Main Theorem}\label{sec:main}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional sublogarithmic approximate-private learning]
\label{thm:main}
Under Assumption~\ref{assump:polynomial-global-stability}, fix
\(\varepsilon_0\in(0,1)\).  Let
\(\{X_\kappa\}_{\kappa\in\mathbb N}\) be any sequence of domains and,
for every \(\kappa\), let
\(C_\kappa\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X_\kappa}\) be a nonempty finite binary
concept class.  Suppose that
\[
|C_\kappa|\to\infty
\]
and, for every \(p\in\mathbb N\), there is \(\kappa_0(p)\) for which
\[
L_\kappa>d_\kappa^p
\qquad(\kappa\geq\kappa_0(p)).
\]
There is a constant \(K_a\geq2\), depending only on
\(a,\alpha_0,\beta_0\) and the universal numerical constant in the
stable-selection bound, and independent of
\(\kappa,X_\kappa,C_\kappa,|C_\kappa|\), every realizable
distribution, and \(\varepsilon_0\), such that the following holds.
For every \(\kappa\), there is an arbitrary-output,
computationally unrestricted kernel
\[
M_\kappa:
Z_{X_\kappa}^{N_\kappa}
\rightsquigarrow\mathcal H_{X_\kappa}
\]
that is distribution-free realizable
\((\alpha_0,\beta_0)\)-PAC in population zero-one risk and
\((\varepsilon_0,\delta_\kappa)\)-differentially private under
symmetric ordered replacement adjacency, where
\[
\delta_\kappa
=
\exp\!\left[
-q_\kappa^{2a+2}
-\log\log(e^e+L_\kappa)
\right],
\]
\[
\log\frac1{\delta_\kappa}
=q_\kappa^{2a+2}+\log\log(e^e+L_\kappa),
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
N_\kappa
&=
\left\lceil
K_a\varepsilon_0^{-2}q_\kappa^{2a+2}
\left(
1+q_\kappa^{2a+2}
+\log\log(e^e+L_\kappa)
\right)
\right\rceil\\
&\leq
1+K_a\varepsilon_0^{-2}q_\kappa^{2a+2}
\left(
1+q_\kappa^{2a+2}
+\log\log(e^e+L_\kappa)
\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The probability in the PAC statement is joint over the iid sample
and all learner randomness; privacy is pointwise for every adjacent
pair and measurable output event.  The input arity is exactly
\(N_\kappa\) at each class, while the sequence-asymptotic conclusions
are
\[
N_\kappa=L_\kappa^{o(1)}=o(L_\kappa)
\]
and, for every fixed real \(\rho>0\),
\[
\delta_\kappa N_\kappa^\rho\longrightarrow0.
\]
If an allowed schedule satisfies
\(\bar\delta(N_\kappa)\geq\delta_\kappa\), then the same learner is
\((\varepsilon_0,\bar\delta(N_\kappa))\)-differentially private.
In particular, for every fixed \(c>0\), eventually
\[
\delta_\kappa
\leq\frac{c}{N_\kappa^2\log(eN_\kappa)}.
\]
At the exact schedule or any eventually larger allowed schedule,
there are no \(c_*>0\) and \(\kappa_*\) such that every eligible
arbitrary-output, computationally unrestricted,
distribution-free realizable \((\alpha_0,\beta_0)\)-PAC learner for
\(C_\kappa\) uses at least \(c_*L_\kappa\) records for every
\(\kappa\geq\kappa_*\).

All exposed variables are displayed above.  The only hidden
numerical dependence is the stated dependence of \(K_a\); \(a\),
\(\alpha_0,\beta_0,\varepsilon_0\), the sequence, the zero-one risk,
and the adjacency convention are fixed while \(\kappa\to\infty\).
This is a conditional result: it assumes, and does not prove,
Assumption~\ref{assump:polynomial-global-stability}.
\end{theorem}
