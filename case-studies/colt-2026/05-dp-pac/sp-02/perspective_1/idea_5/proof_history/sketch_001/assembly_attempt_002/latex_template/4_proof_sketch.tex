\section{Proof Sketch}\label{sec:proof-sketch}

The proof first conjugates binary labels and hypotheses by the
pointwise bijection \(y\mapsto2y-1\).  This preserves realizability,
zero-one loss, iid laws, exact atom probabilities, measurable output
events, and replacement adjacency.  The stable-selection theorem of
\citet{bun2021equivalence} can therefore be applied to the producer
in Assumption~\ref{assump:polynomial-global-stability}.  Its
construction depends on the fixed producer and public parameters,
not on the realizable distribution or its frequent atom, which yields
one distribution-free arbitrary-output learner.

The resulting two-term sample bound is then specialized without
discarding any logarithmic or confidence term.  With
\(b=2a+2\), every term is dominated explicitly by
\(q^b(1+T_a(q,L))\), and a constant \(K_a\) with only the declared
dependence suffices.  Prefix projection pads the learner to exactly
\(N_a(q,L,\varepsilon_0)\) inputs while preserving both utility and
privacy.

Next, the privacy parameter is evaluated exactly:
\[
\delta_a(q,L)
=\frac{e^{-q^b}}{\log(e^e+L)},\qquad
\log(1/\delta_a(q,L))
=q^b+\log\log(e^e+L).
\]
The cardinality factor remains active when \(q\) is bounded.
For a class sequence, the full quantifier order in the
superpolynomial condition and
\(\ell_\kappa\leq L_\kappa/\log2\) imply
\(q_\kappa=L_\kappa^{o(1)}\).  Direct exponent allocation through
\(T_a\), the product defining \(N_a\), and its ceiling then proves
\(N_\kappa=L_\kappa^{o(1)}=o(L_\kappa)\).

Finally, writing \(x_\kappa=q_\kappa^b\) and
\(u_\kappa=u(L_\kappa)\), the exact privacy product factors into an
\(x_\kappa\)-term that is uniformly bounded on \([1,\infty)\) and an
\(u_\kappa\)-term that tends to zero:
\[
\delta_\kappa N_\kappa^\rho
\leq C_{\rho,\varepsilon_0,K_a}
\bigl[e^{-x_\kappa}x_\kappa^\rho(1+x_\kappa)^\rho\bigr]
\bigl[e^{-u_\kappa}(1+u_\kappa)^\rho\bigr].
\]
This proves privacy negligibility for every fixed \(\rho>0\),
including bounded \(q_\kappa\).  Privacy monotonicity is used only
toward a larger additive allowance, and the exact learners with
\(N_\kappa/L_\kappa\to0\) contradict every eventual positive linear
lower bound.  The complete derivations and the final assembly appear
in Section~\ref{sec:proof-details}.
