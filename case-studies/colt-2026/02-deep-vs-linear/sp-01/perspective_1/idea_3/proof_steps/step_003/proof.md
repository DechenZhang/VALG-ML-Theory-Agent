## Cited Result Applications

1. **Accepted all-history containment dependency.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, the current-notation conclusion of accepted Invariant~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step002-closed-tube} is: for every \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\) and every deterministic history
   \[
   \omega=((x^{(0)},y^{(0)}),\ldots,(x^{(T-1)},y^{(T-1)}))
   \in(\mathcal X\times\{-1,+1\})^T,
   \]
   the exact states generated by the setting update satisfy
   \[
   \|\theta^{(t)}-\theta^{(0)}\|_\infty
   \le t\eta G_r(\theta^{(0)})\le r,
   \qquad 0\le t\le T.
   \]
   Its proof/review pair is accepted and supplies only this derived membership invariant here. It does not supply gate stability, continuity, or a path representation; those are proved below.
2. **Continuity of finite algebraic/ReLU compositions.** A finite sum and product of continuous real functions is continuous, and the coordinate map \(v\mapsto\max\{0,v\}\) is continuous. Therefore a recursively defined finite network forward coordinate is continuous in its parameter coordinates whenever the preceding forward coordinates are continuous. The affine parameter segment used below has continuous coordinates, so this fact applies at every hidden layer, including points where a preactivation is zero (although such points are excluded on \(E_r\) after the margin argument).
3. **Intermediate value theorem.** If \(q:[0,1]\to\mathbb R\) is continuous and \(q(0)q(1)<0\), then there exists \(s_\star\in(0,1)\) with \(q(s_\star)=0\). We apply it only to the exact scalar preactivation paths in Lemma~\ref{lem:p1-i3-step003-continuous-preactivation}; its continuity is established there and its zero would lie in the same closed ball.

## Local Derivation

### unit_001: lemma

**Lemma (Continuous exact preactivation paths on the closed ball).** \label{lem:p1-i3-step003-continuous-preactivation}

**Statement.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, fix any deterministic \(\theta^{(0)}\in\mathbb R^S\), any finite \(r>0\), any \(\theta\in B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\), and any \(x\in\mathcal X\). If \(L\ge2\), define
\[
\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)
:=\theta^{(0)}+s(\theta-\theta^{(0)}),
\qquad 0\le s\le1.
\]
Then \(\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)\in B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\) for every \(s\in[0,1]\). For each hidden layer \(1\le\ell\le L-1\) and coordinate \(j\in[n_\ell]\), the exact scalar path
\[
q_{\ell,j,x,\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)
:=u_{\ell,j}(\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s),x)
\]
is continuous on \([0,1]\). More explicitly,
\[
q_{1,j}(s)
=\sum_{k=1}^{n_0}\gamma_{1,jk}(s)x_k,
\]
and, for \(2\le\ell\le L-1\),
\[
q_{\ell,j}(s)
=\sum_{k=1}^{n_{\ell-1}}
\gamma_{\ell,jk}(s)\,\sigma(q_{\ell-1,k}(s)).
\]
These formulas are the exact forward preactivation equations along the segment, not a derivative or a linearized approximation. If \(L=1\), there are no hidden preactivation paths and the assertion is vacuous.

**Proof / justification.** For every vectorized parameter coordinate \(a\),
\[
\left|[\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)]_a-[\theta^{(0)}]_a\right|
=s\left|[\theta]_a-[\theta^{(0)}]_a\right|
\le sr\le r.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus the entire segment, including its endpoints and any point with \(\|\theta-\theta^{(0)}\|_\infty=r\), lies in the closed coordinate ball. Each coordinate of \(\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)\) is affine, hence continuous in \(s\).

At layer one, \(x\) is fixed and the displayed finite sum for \(q_{1,j}\) is continuous. Suppose the paths at hidden layer \(\ell-1\) are continuous. By the continuity fact stated above, each \(s\mapsto\sigma(q_{\ell-1,k}(s))\) is continuous; multiplying it by the affine coordinate \(s\mapsto\gamma_{\ell,jk}(s)\) and summing finitely many \(k\)'s yields a continuous \(q_{\ell,j}\). Finite induction over \(\ell\) proves continuity through \(L-1\). The recursive formulas coincide with the setting's forward definitions at \(\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)\), so \(q_{\ell,j}(s)\) is exactly the claimed preactivation path. No \(\rho_\kappa\), selected gradient, or nonzero-weight condition is used. \(\square\)

### unit_002: proposition

**Proposition (Strict gate constancy on a positive-margin ball).** \label{prop:p1-i3-step003-ball-gates}

**Statement.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, Lemma~\ref{lem:p1-i3-step003-continuous-preactivation}, and the local conditional hypothesis \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\), let \(m:=M_r(\theta^{(0)})\). If \(L\ge2\), then \(m>0\) and, for every \(\theta\in B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\), every \(x\in\mathcal X\), every \(1\le\ell\le L-1\), and every \(j\in[n_\ell]\),
\[
|u_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)|\ge m,
\qquad
\operatorname{sgn}(u_{\ell,j}(\theta,x))
=\operatorname{sgn}(u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(0)},x)),
\]
where \(\operatorname{sgn}\) is used only on nonzero reals. Consequently,
\[
\mathbf 1\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)>0\}
=\mathbf 1\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(0)},x)>0\}.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(L=1\), the hidden-index set
\[
\{(\ell,j):1\le\ell\le L-1,\ j\in[n_\ell]\}
\]
is empty and the statement is vacuous; the setting defines \(M_r=+\infty\), so no gate condition is being assumed.

**Proof / justification.** Assume \(L\ge2\). Since \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\), the definition of \(E_r\) gives \(m=M_r(\theta^{(0)})>0\). By the definition of the infimum in \(M_r\), for every \(\vartheta\in B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\), every \(x\), and every hidden \((\ell,j)\),
\[
|u_{\ell,j}(\vartheta,x)|\ge m.
\tag{3}
\]
Fix \(\theta,x,\ell,j\) in the displayed domains and use Lemma~\ref{lem:p1-i3-step003-continuous-preactivation}. Along the exact segment, (3) gives
\[
|q_{\ell,j,x,\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s)|
=|u_{\ell,j}(\gamma_{\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s),x)|
\ge m>0,
\qquad 0\le s\le1.
\tag{4}
\]
In particular, both endpoint values are nonzero. If their ordinary signs differed, then
\[
q_{\ell,j,x,\theta^{(0)},\theta}(0)
q_{\ell,j,x,\theta^{(0)},\theta}(1)<0.
\]
The intermediate value theorem would produce \(s_\star\in(0,1)\) with
\[
q_{\ell,j,x,\theta^{(0)},\theta}(s_\star)=0,
\]
contradicting (4). Hence the signs agree. Since neither endpoint is zero, equality of ordinary signs is exactly equality of the strict \(>0\) indicators, proving (2).

The argument includes \(\theta=\theta^{(0)}\), every point on the ball boundary, and any zero coordinates of the parameter matrices. No weight coordinate is required to be nonzero; only the preactivation margin is used. If zero parameter coordinates cause some hidden preactivation to vanish anywhere in the ball, then \(M_r=0\), so \(\theta^{(0)}\notin E_r\); that excluded case is not silently folded into (2). The margin \(m\) is defined entirely from forward preactivations and is independent of \(\kappa\). The proof never evaluates \(\rho_\kappa(0)\), so the conclusion is unchanged for every fixed \(\kappa\in[0,1]\). \(\square\)

### unit_003: invariant

**Invariant (All-history initialization gate signature).** \label{inv:p1-i3-step003-history-gates}

**Statement.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, the accepted Invariant~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step002-closed-tube}, and Proposition~\ref{prop:p1-i3-step003-ball-gates}, fix any \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\) and any deterministic labeled history \(\omega\in(\mathcal X\times\{-1,+1\})^T\), and let \(\theta^{(t)}\) be the exact states generated by the setting update. If \(L\ge2\), then for every \(x\in\mathcal X\), every \(1\le\ell\le L-1\), every \(j\in[n_\ell]\), and every \(0\le t\le T\),
\[
|u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(t)},x)|
\ge M_r(\theta^{(0)})>0,
\qquad
\mathbf 1\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(t)},x)>0\}
=\mathbf 1\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(0)},x)>0\}.
\tag{5}
\]
For \(L=1\), there are no hidden gates, so (5) is an empty universal statement and \(M_r=+\infty\) by definition.

**Proof / justification.** The accepted dependency gives
\[
\|\theta^{(t)}-\theta^{(0)}\|_\infty
\le t\eta G_r(\theta^{(0)})\le r,
\qquad 0\le t\le T.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus every generated state is in the same closed ball, including \(t=0\), the first transition \(t=1\), and the terminal state \(t=T\), even when equality holds at the radius. Because \(T\ge1\), the first-update instance of (6) is
\[
\|\theta^{(1)}-\theta^{(0)}\|_\infty
\le\eta G_r(\theta^{(0)})
\le T\eta G_r(\theta^{(0)})\le r.
\]
Proposition~\ref{prop:p1-i3-step003-ball-gates} applies to each \(\theta^{(t)}\), every input \(x\), and every hidden coordinate, giving (5). The history and its labels were arbitrary, so this is an all-history statement rather than an almost-sure assertion tied to source-consistent samples. For \(L=1\), the hidden quantifier is empty and no gate activation or continuity argument is needed. The proof uses no assumption of gate stability before (6); it derives the stability from the independently supplied containment and the positive static margin. \(\square\)
