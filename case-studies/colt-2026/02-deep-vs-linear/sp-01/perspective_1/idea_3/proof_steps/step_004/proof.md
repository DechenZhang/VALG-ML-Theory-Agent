## Local Derivation

### unit_001: lemma

**Lemma (Exact path expansion under the initialization gate signature).** \label{lem:p1-i3-step004-path-expansion}

**Statement.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, Proposition~\ref{prop:p1-i3-step003-ball-gates}, and the local conditional hypothesis \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\), fix \(\theta\in B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\) and \(x\in\mathcal X\). For \(p=(i_0,\ldots,i_{L-1})\in\mathcal I_{\rm path}\), define
\[
c_p(\theta)
:=(\theta_L)_{1,i_{L-1}}
  \prod_{\ell=1}^{L-1}(\theta_\ell)_{i_\ell,i_{\ell-1}},
\qquad
\varphi_{\theta^{(0)},p}(x)
:=x_{i_0}\prod_{\ell=1}^{L-1}
  {\bf1}\{u_{\ell,i_\ell}(\theta^{(0)},x)>0\}.
\]
Let
\[
c(\theta):=\bigl(c_p(\theta)\bigr)_{p\in\mathcal I_{\rm path}}
\in\mathbb R^{d_{\rm path}}.
\]
The displayed \(\varphi_{\theta^{(0)},p}(x)\) is exactly the coordinate
\([\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)]_p\) defined in the setting.
Then
\[
f_\theta(x)=\sum_{p\in\mathcal I_{\rm path}}c_p(\theta)
  \varphi_{\theta^{(0)},p}(x)
=\left\langle c(\theta),\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)\right\rangle.
\tag{1}
\]
The empty products in (1) are one when \(L=1\), so the statement includes the gate-free case.

**Proof / justification.** First suppose \(L\ge2\). Write
\[
g_{\ell,j}^{(0)}(x):={\bf1}\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta^{(0)},x)>0\},
\qquad 1\le\ell\le L-1.
\tag{2}
\]
This is a proof-local abbreviation for the gate already present in the setting-defined feature map. Proposition~\ref{prop:p1-i3-step003-ball-gates} gives, for every hidden coordinate,
\[
{\bf1}\{u_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)>0\}=g_{\ell,j}^{(0)}(x),
\qquad
|u_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)|>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently the exact ReLU equation can be written
\[
z_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)=g_{\ell,j}^{(0)}(x)
  \sum_{k=1}^{n_{\ell-1}}
  (\theta_\ell)_{j,k}z_{\ell-1,k}(\theta,x),
\qquad 1\le\ell\le L-1.
\tag{4}
\]

To make the distributive expansion explicit, define proof-local partial path sums \(P_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)\) by
\[
P_{0,i_0}(\theta,x):=x_{i_0}
\]
and, for \(1\le\ell\le L-1\),
\[
P_{\ell,i_\ell}(\theta,x):=
g_{\ell,i_\ell}^{(0)}(x)
\sum_{i_{\ell-1}=1}^{n_{\ell-1}}
(\theta_\ell)_{i_\ell,i_{\ell-1}}
P_{\ell-1,i_{\ell-1}}(\theta,x).
\tag{5}
\]
We prove simultaneously that \(z_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)=P_{\ell,j}(\theta,x)\) and
\[
P_{\ell,i_\ell}(\theta,x)
=\sum_{i_0=1}^{n_0}\cdots\sum_{i_{\ell-1}=1}^{n_{\ell-1}}
 x_{i_0}
 \left(\prod_{q=1}^{\ell}g_{q,i_q}^{(0)}(x)\right)
 \left(\prod_{q=1}^{\ell}(\theta_q)_{i_q,i_{q-1}}\right)
\tag{6}
\]
for every \(1\le\ell\le L-1\), where \(i_\ell\) is the displayed output coordinate and all sums/products are finite.

At \(\ell=0\), \(z_{0,i_0}=x_{i_0}=P_{0,i_0}\), establishing the base. Assume the assertion at layer \(\ell-1\). Substituting \(z_{\ell-1}=P_{\ell-1}\) into (4) gives exactly the recurrence (5), so \(z_{\ell}=P_{\ell}\). Substituting the induction formula (6) at layer \(\ell-1\) into (5), distributing the finite sum, and appending the new factor \(g_{\ell,i_\ell}^{(0)}(x)(\theta_\ell)_{i_\ell,i_{\ell-1}}\) yields (6) at layer \(\ell\). No division or nonzero weight assumption is used.

At the scalar output, use \(n_L=1\) and the setting equation \(f_\theta(x)=\sum_{i_{L-1}=1}^{n_{L-1}}(\theta_L)_{1,i_{L-1}}z_{L-1,i_{L-1}}(\theta,x)\). Applying (6) at \(\ell=L-1\) and distributing the final finite sum gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
f_\theta(x)
&=\sum_{i_0=1}^{n_0}\cdots\sum_{i_{L-1}=1}^{n_{L-1}}
 x_{i_0}
 \left(\prod_{q=1}^{L-1}g_{q,i_q}^{(0)}(x)\right)
 \left[(\theta_L)_{1,i_{L-1}}
       \prod_{q=1}^{L-1}(\theta_q)_{i_q,i_{q-1}}\right] \\
&=\sum_{p\in\mathcal I_{\rm path}}c_p(\theta)
   \varphi_{\theta^{(0)},p}(x),
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
which is (1).

If \(L=1\), \(\mathcal I_{\rm path}=[n_0]\), there are no hidden gates, and the setting gives directly
\[
f_\theta(x)=\sum_{i_0=1}^{n_0}(\theta_1)_{1,i_0}x_{i_0}
=\sum_{i_0\in[n_0]}c_{i_0}(\theta)
  \varphi_{\theta^{(0)},i_0}(x),
\]
because \(c_{i_0}(\theta)=(\theta_1)_{1,i_0}\) and \(\varphi_{\theta^{(0)},i_0}(x)=x_{i_0}\). Thus (1) holds in every allowed depth. The proof uses forward gates only; the fixed kink derivative convention \(\kappa\) never changes the identity.

Zero or canceling weight products merely make some summands in (7) zero or cause exact scalar cancellation. Since (7) is an equality of finite sums, no coefficient is required to be nonzero. \(\square\)

### unit_002: proposition

**Proposition (Exact latter-half aggregate coefficient and tie identity).** \label{prop:p1-i3-step004-aggregate}

**Statement.** Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:fixed-source-witnesses} and~\ref{assump:robust-tube}, Invariants~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step002-closed-tube} and~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step003-history-gates}, Lemma~\ref{lem:p1-i3-step004-path-expansion}, and the local conditional hypothesis \(\theta^{(0)}\in E_r\), fix a deterministic labeled history \(\omega\in(\mathcal X\times\{-1,+1\})^T\) and its exact iterates \(\theta^{(t)}(\omega)\). Define
\[
J_T:=\{\lceil T/2\rceil,\ldots,T\},
\qquad
w_\omega:=\sum_{t\in J_T}c(\theta^{(t)}(\omega))
\in\mathbb R^{d_{\rm path}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Then, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\),
\[
A_\omega(x):=\sum_{t\in J_T}f_{\theta^{(t)}(\omega)}(x)
=\left\langle w_\omega,\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)\right\rangle.
\tag{9}
\]
If \(\omega\) is a source-consistent history for a fixed \((\mathcal D,h^\star)\), then \(A_\omega=A_{\mathcal D,h^\star}\) as defined by the setting on that realized path, and therefore
\[
\operatorname{sign}_{s_0}(A_\omega(x))
=\operatorname{sign}_{s_0}\!\left(\left\langle w_\omega,
\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)\right\rangle\right)
\tag{10}
\]
for every \(x\), including when the common scalar is zero. Thus the two strict-inequality classification indicators against \(h^\star(x)\) are identical pointwise.

**Proof / justification.** By Invariant~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step002-closed-tube}, every \(\theta^{(t)}(\omega)\) lies in \(B_\infty(\theta^{(0)},r)\). Invariant~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step003-history-gates} therefore supplies the same initialization gate signature at every \(t\), and Lemma~\ref{lem:p1-i3-step004-path-expansion} applies to each state and to the evaluation input \(x\). Hence
\[
f_{\theta^{(t)}(\omega)}(x)
=\left\langle c(\theta^{(t)}(\omega)),
\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)\right\rangle,
\qquad t\in J_T.
\tag{11}
\]
The index set \(J_T\) is finite and nonempty because \(T\ge1\), so the coefficient sum in (8) is a finite vector. Summing (11) and using bilinearity of the finite Euclidean inner product gives (9). For a source-consistent history, the definition of the setting's latter-half score is exactly the left side of (9), so (10) follows by applying the same fixed function \(\operatorname{sign}_{s_0}\) to equal real numbers.

When \(T=1\), \(J_T=\{1\}\), and (9) is simply the one-state identity at \(\theta^{(1)}\); no empty aggregate is introduced. If some \(c_p(\theta^{(t)})=0\), or if distinct path contributions cancel in the sum, (9) remains an exact equality. In particular, if \(A_\omega(x)=0\), both sides of (10) equal the prescribed tie label \(s_0\), so no positive-margin assumption is needed and no tie is silently discarded. The construction does not alter the initialization-only feature map: all history, label, and target dependence is confined to \(w_\omega\), which is used only as a coefficient vector.
\(\square\)

## Target-Step Assembly

The \(L=1\) calculation is the same statement with empty gate and product factors.

For an arbitrary history, Invariants~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step002-closed-tube} and~\ref{inv:p1-i3-step003-history-gates} make the lemma applicable at every \(t\in J_T\). Proposition~\ref{prop:p1-i3-step004-aggregate} sums the exact identities and defines \(w_\omega\), yielding
\[
A_\omega(x)=\left\langle w_\omega,\varphi_{\theta^{(0)}}(x)\right\rangle
\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathcal X.
\]
For source-consistent histories this is exactly the setting aggregate \(A_{\mathcal D,h^\star}\), and equality of the real scores preserves the fixed \(\operatorname{sign}_{s_0}\) decision even at zero. Thus the two named local results and the accepted containment/gate dependencies prove precisely the step_004 sketch claim, with no approximation, numerical residual, new feature law, or path-independent optimizer asserted.
