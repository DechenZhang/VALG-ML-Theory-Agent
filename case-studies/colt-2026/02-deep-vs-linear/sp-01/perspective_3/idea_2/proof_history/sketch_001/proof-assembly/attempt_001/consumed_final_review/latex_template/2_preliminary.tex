\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:preliminaries}

The cube involution $x\mapsto -x$ is a bijection of $\mathcal X$.  Hence the
reflected mass function $\mathcal D^-$ and the symmetrization
$\mathcal D^{\mathrm{sym}}$ introduced in Section~\ref{sec:setup} are the
natural distributions for pairing antipodal inputs.  The proof below uses the
identity-coordinate score $x\mapsto\langle v,x\rangle$ and the same fixed
tie-resolved map $\operatorname{sign}_{s_0}$ as the source learner.

For a fixed target $h$ it will also use the representative set
\[
 Q_h:=\{q\in\mathcal X:h(q)=-s_0\}.
\]
Under Assumption~\ref{assump:antipodal-oddness}, this set contains one point
from each antipodal pair; that fact is proved in the appendix rather than
assumed here.  All other convex-hull, support, and trajectory abbreviations
are introduced locally in the corresponding proofs.
