## Setting

- Context: $\mathcal X=\{-1,+1\}^n$, and $\mathcal H\subseteq\{-1,+1\}^{\mathcal X}$ is an arbitrary antipodally odd class: $h(-x)=-h(x)$ for all $h\in\mathcal H$ and $x\in\mathcal X$.
- Model: a fully connected bias-free depth-two ReLU network $f_{a,W}(x)=a^\top\sigma(Wx)$ of arbitrary hidden width $m\ge 1$, with $S=m(n+1)$ trainable weights and the source's independent fan-in-scaled Gaussian initialization.
- Data: one fixed architecture, constant stepsize $\eta$, and positive step count $T$ attain expected classification error at most $\varepsilon$ for every distribution $\mathcal D$ on $\mathcal X$ and every $h^\star\in\mathcal H$, where $2\varepsilon<1/(n+1)$; expectation remains over initialization and one-sample SGD sampling.
- Algorithm: use exactly the source logistic loss, update every layer on one fresh sample at each step, and return the sign of the sum of network outputs over the latter half of the trajectory.
- Regime: arbitrary $n,m,\eta,T,\mathcal D$, and antipodally odd $\mathcal H$ in the high-accuracy regime above; no margin, bounded-displacement, gate-stability, finite-precision, frozen-feature, target-dependent-feature, or altered-oracle condition is imposed.

## Goal

- Theorem type: source-endorsed confident-dimension upper bound, proved through a stronger deterministic representation in a high-accuracy special case.
- Informal statement: if the stated depth-two learner satisfies the exact universal premise for an antipodally odd class $\mathcal H$ with $2\varepsilon<1/(n+1)$, then the identity embedding sign-represents every $h\in\mathcal H$ exactly; consequently $\operatorname{dc}^{1/2}(\mathcal H)\le\operatorname{dc}(\mathcal H)\le n\le S\le TS$, with a feature-map law fixed before every later $\mathcal D$ and $h^\star$.
