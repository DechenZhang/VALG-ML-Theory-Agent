# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple: (analysis_target=deterministic_dimension_complexity_explicit_polynomial_ST_bound, model_class=fully_connected_relu_network_with_S_parameters, data_assumption=universal_all_distribution_all_target_learning_premise, regime=arbitrary_boolean_cube_class_with_epsilon_below_one_quarter, algorithm=gaussian_initialized_logistic_one_sample_sgd_with_latter_half_time_aggregation)

## Added Dimensions

- Conditional bridge condition: require a target-independent law over d-dimensional feature maps that exactly sign-represents each fixed h with probability at least 1/2; the law is chosen before D and h, the dimension d is exposed, and exactness is tie-resolved under the source sign convention. This is the finite-domain, tie-resolved source interface dc^{1/2}(H)<=d: exact representation implies zero error for every D, while taking D uniform on the full finite X turns zero tie-resolved error back into exact representation.
- Amplification route: use the exact T-sample premise to prove VC(H) < 2T, bound log |H| by O(TS) using Sauer-Shelah and n <= S, then concatenate O(log |H|) independent confident maps and apply a union bound over H.
- Target contract: prove the conditional deterministic amplification bound dc(H) <= C*T*S*d; consequently, any explicit confident bound d=poly(S,T) yields Perspective 2's deterministic polynomial endpoint. The condition is a source-endorsed intermediate, not an assumption that H already has one common deterministic map.

## Setting

- Context: X={-1,+1}^n with n>=1 and H is an arbitrary finite binary class H subset of {-1,+1}^X. The deterministic dimension complexity is the least dimension admitting one feature map whose linear separators sign-represent every h. If H is empty, dc(H)=0 and the target is immediate; the nonempty case is used below.
- Model: the exact fully connected, bias-free ReLU network in the source has depth L, n_0=n, n_L=1, widths n_1,...,n_{L-1}, S=sum_i n_i n_{i-1} weights, fan-in-scaled independent Gaussian initialization, and no architectural or initialization change.
- Data: one fixed architecture, eta, and positive integer T attain expected classification error at most epsilon<1/4 for every distribution D on X and every h* in H; expectation is over the stated initialization and one-sample SGD sampling.
- Algorithm: every update is the exact logistic one-sample SGD update from the source, and the returned predictor is the sign of the latter-half sum of network outputs.
- Regime: arbitrary n, H, eta, and exact ReLU trajectory, with T>=1 and the following explicit conditional premise. For an exposed nonnegative integer d, there is a distribution P over maps phi:X->R^d, fixed independently of D and h, such that for every h in H, P[there exists w with sign(<w,phi(x)>)=h(x) for every x in X] >= 1/2. Exact sign agreement includes the source tie convention; a zero-score witness is not silently counted as an exact representation. No dependence on n, eta, D, or h is hidden in d. If d=0, the condition forces every nonempty target to equal the fixed sign(0) classifier and dc(H)=0, so the target is immediate; otherwise d>=1. Positive network widths imply S>=n>=1.

## Goal

- Theorem type: conditional deterministic dimension-amplification theorem.
- Informal statement: under the exact universal ReLU/SGD premise and the explicit d-dimensional confident-map condition above, independent concatenation of r=ceil(log_2(2|H|)) maps gives a single deterministic embedding that sign-represents all of H, and therefore dc(H) <= r*d <= C*T*S*d for a universal C, with no hidden n or eta factor. In particular, any explicit d=poly(S,T) confident bound implies an explicit deterministic poly(S,T) bound. The remaining unconditional deterministic polynomial implication, and the exact linear C*T*S implication, are left open.

- Transfer map: preserves the exact Gaussian/logistic/one-sample-SGD/time-aggregation protocol; imports the source-endorsed confident representation as an explicit conditional interface and adds a Sauer-Shelah plus independent-concatenation derandomization bridge.
- Source alignment: preserves every exact-protocol premise and makes the target change explicit: the conclusion is the conditional bridge dc(H)<=C*T*S*d rather than the unconditional linear target, and a deterministic polynomial endpoint follows only when an explicit d=poly(S,T) confident bound is supplied. It does not claim the unrestricted source target or a standalone probabilistic-dimension result.
- Progress type: conditional
- Materiality: this isolates a valid route from a confident/high-probability intermediate to Perspective 2's deterministic polynomial endpoint. Any future exact-SGD proof of a confident bound d=poly(S,T) immediately yields a deterministic polynomial bound with only an O(TS) amplification factor.
- Significance: the mandated full search found no supported nonduplicate proof or exact-premise counterexample beyond Perspective 1's active exact route, and the unconditional polynomial search retained idea_1's target-dependent-feature, hidden-support, and exponential-unrolling defects. This conditional bridge avoids those defects: P is fixed before D and h, and the only derandomization cost is log |H|. The exact learner premise controls that cost through VC(H)<2T and n<=S, without enumerating label transcripts or hiding n.
- Feasibility: for T>=1, if a 2T-point set were shattered, choose its uniform distribution and average over all labelings. Averaging jointly over the T iid training inputs and an independent uniform test point, the probability that the test point is absent from the training sample is (1-1/(2T))^T>=1/2. Conditional on that unseen event and the observed labeled sample, the test point's random target label remains an independent fair sign, so every learner has conditional error 1/2; hence some target has expected error at least 1/4, contradicting epsilon<1/4. Thus v=VC(H)<2T. For nonempty H, Sauer-Shelah gives |H|<=sum_{i=0}^v binom(2^n,i); for v>=1 this implies log_2|H|<=v*log_2(e*2^n/v)<=2T*(n+log_2 e), while v=0 gives |H|<=1. Since positive widths give n<=S and S,T>=1, r=ceil(log_2(2|H|))<=C*T*S for a universal C. Draw r independent maps from P; for each fixed h the probability that none exactly represents h is at most 2^{-r}, so a union bound leaves positive probability that every h is represented by some block. Concatenating those blocks and zero-padding the corresponding separator proves dc(H)<=r*d. The confident interface is source/literature-derived; its exact-sign/no-tie form is directly checkable in three branch-specific regimes: a point-mass law on any common separating embedding, a finite mixture in which each target is covered by at least half of the maps, and a continuous feature law with a positive finite-domain separation margin on every successful map. No flawed finite-precision SGD-to-feature theorem is used.
- Consistency: the confident law is not allowed to depend on D or h, and the concatenation is a deterministic existence argument after sampling the law; no feature map is trained on target labels. The added condition is explicitly conditional rather than hidden, and it does not assume the desired deterministic map because different h may be covered by different sampled blocks. The exact linear target remains unresolved, and the unconditional polynomial target still requires proving the confident interface from the original SGD trajectory.
