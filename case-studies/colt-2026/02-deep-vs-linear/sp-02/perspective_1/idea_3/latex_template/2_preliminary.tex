\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:preliminaries}

For the ordered catalog in
Assumption~\ref{assump:canonical-rounded-output-catalog}, write
\begin{equation}
\rho_{\varepsilon}:=1-2\varepsilon,
\qquad
\phi(x):=(g_1(x),\ldots,g_L(x)),
\qquad
\Delta_L:=\left\{w\in[0,1]^L:\sum_{i=1}^Lw_i=1\right\}.
\label{eq:main-notation}
\end{equation}
The scalar \(\rho_{\varepsilon}\) names the exact margin appearing in the
main theorem.  The map \(\phi\) is the catalog coordinate map from
\eqref{eq:catalog-map-simplex}, and \(\Delta_L\) is the set of catalog
mixtures used for the target-dependent weights.  These objects are derived
directly from the formalized setting; no admissibility event, selector,
stability condition, or additional assumption is encoded in this notation.
