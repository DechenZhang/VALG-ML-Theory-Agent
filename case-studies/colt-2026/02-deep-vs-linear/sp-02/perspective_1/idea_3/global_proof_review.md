source orientation, the first variable is maximized and the second minimized;
the branch maps them as

\[
M=\Delta_L\ni w,\qquad N=\Delta_F\ni p,\qquad
f(w,p)=p^TAw.

\[
\max_{w\in\Delta_L}\min_{p\in\Delta_F}p^TAw
=\min_{p\in\Delta_F}\max_{w\in\Delta_L}p^TAw.
\]

The diagnostic separately supplies the per-(p) lower bound, the two simplex
vertex identities, continuity and attainment, and the empty-(F) branch.
Sion is not credited with catalog existence, positivity, attainment, FIP, or
global closure. This is an exact source-object and convention match with no
