\section{Theoretical Setup}
\label{sec:theoretical-setup}

\subsection{Problem Setting}

Let \(\mathcal X\) be an arbitrary domain, possibly empty or infinite, and
let \(\mathcal H\subseteq\{+1,-1\}^{\mathcal X}\).  For a distribution
\(\mathcal D\) on \(\mathcal X\), a target \(h\in\mathcal H\), and a
binary predictor \(g:\mathcal X\to\{+1,-1\}\), define
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L_{\mathcal D,h}(g)
:=\Pr_{x\sim\mathcal D}\bigl[g(x)h(x)<0\bigr].
\label{eq:binary-risk}
\end{equation}
All query expectations and risks below are understood in the usual
statistical-query sense when they arise on an actual execution.  No
measurability or output behavior is imposed on a prescribed response sequence
that is not an actual tolerance-valid execution.

The deterministic exact dimension complexity \(\operatorname{dc}(\mathcal
H)\) is the least \(d\in\mathbb N_0\) for which there is one map
\(\psi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfying
\begin{equation}
\forall h\in\mathcal H\ \exists u_h\in\mathbb R^d\ \forall x\in\mathcal X,
\qquad
h(x)\langle u_h,\psi(x)\rangle>0.
\label{eq:dc-definition}
\end{equation}
Its value is \(+\infty\) when no finite \(d\) works.  Thus the required
representation is pointwise and exact, without a distributional exceptional
set.

An unrestricted SQ query is a bounded function
\(q:\mathcal X\times\{+1,-1\}\to[-1,1]\).  For fixed
\((\mathcal D,h)\), an adaptive tolerance-\(\tau\) oracle policy may choose
each real reply from the reached history, but a reply \(a\) to the current
query \(q\) must satisfy
\begin{equation}
\left|a-\mathbb E_{x\sim\mathcal D}q(x,h(x))\right|\le\tau.
\label{eq:sq-validity}
\end{equation}
Validity and the learner guarantee are required separately for every
adversarial policy; there is no averaging over policies.

Let \((\Omega,\mathcal F,\mu)\) be the random-tape probability space of a
learner \(A\), and write \(R\sim\mu\).  The space may be arbitrary, including
nonatomic.  Once a complete tape \(r\in\Omega\) is fixed, the learner's
queries, stopping rule, and terminal binary predictor are deterministic,
although a query may depend on the whole tape and all previous real replies.
For a tolerance-valid policy \(\mathcal O\), write
\[
A_r^{\mathcal O}(\mathcal D,h):\mathcal X\to\{+1,-1\}
\]
for the terminal predictor on that actual execution.

For the tolerance \(\tau\), put
\begin{equation}
K:=\left\lceil\frac{1}{\tau}\right\rceil,
\qquad
G:=\left\{-1+\frac{2j}{K}:0\le j\le K\right\},
\label{eq:rounding-grid}
\end{equation}
and fix before any instance a deterministic nearest-grid map
\(\rho:[-1,1]\to G\), with a fixed rule for midpoint ties.  Then
\begin{equation}
|\rho(v)-v|\le\frac{1}{K}\le\tau
\qquad (v\in[-1,1]).
\label{eq:rounding-radius}
\end{equation}
For each \((\mathcal D,h)\), define the proof-only exact-center policy
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O^{\rho}_{\mathcal D,h}(q)
:=\rho\!\left(\mathbb E_{x\sim\mathcal D}q(x,h(x))\right).
\label{eq:canonical-policy}
\end{equation}
The learner receives only the rounded reply, not the exact expectation used
by the oracle.

For an ordered finite catalog \(\mathcal C=(g_1,\ldots,g_L)\), define
\begin{equation}
\phi_{\mathcal C}(x):=(g_1(x),\ldots,g_L(x))\in\{+1,-1\}^L,
\qquad
\Delta_L:=\left\{w\in[0,1]^L:\sum_{i=1}^Lw_i=1\right\}.
\label{eq:catalog-map-simplex}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[SQ and catalog-family parameter regime]
\label{assump:sq-parameter-regime}
The parameters satisfy
\[
m\in\mathbb N_0,
\qquad \tau\in(0,\infty),
\qquad \varepsilon\in[0,1/4),
\qquad B\in[1,\infty),
\qquad k\in\mathbb N,\quad k\ge1.
\]
The constants \(B\) and \(k\) are fixed parts of the protocol-family
certificate.  They are independent of \(\mathcal X,\mathcal H\), the learner
parameters \((m,\tau,\varepsilon)\), the instance \((\mathcal D,h)\), the
oracle policy and replies, and the learner tape.  The horizon is the fixed
finite upper bound \(m\), including \(m=0\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Universal randomized adversarial-SQ learner]
\label{assump:universal-adversarial-sq}
One learner \(A\), fixed independently of \((\mathcal D,h)\) and of the
oracle policy, makes at most \(m\) adaptive unrestricted queries of tolerance
\(\tau\) and returns a binary predictor.  For every distribution
\(\mathcal D\) on \(\mathcal X\), every \(h\in\mathcal H\), and every
adaptive tolerance-valid adversarial policy \(\mathcal O\), the actual-run
loss is a \(\mu\)-measurable random variable and
\begin{equation}
\mathbb E_{R\sim\mu}
\mathcal L_{\mathcal D,h}
\bigl(A_R^{\mathcal O}(\mathcal D,h)\bigr)
\le\varepsilon.
\label{eq:universal-risk-guarantee}
\end{equation}
The expectation is only over the learner tape.  The premise grants neither
favorable-policy selection nor exact-expectation access to the learner.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Pre-instance canonical-policy output catalog]
\label{assump:canonical-rounded-output-catalog}
As part of the certified protocol, there is a specified finite ordered
catalog
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C_A^{\rho}=(g_1,\ldots,g_L),
\qquad
1\le L\le B\left(1+\frac{m}{\tau^2}\right)^k,
\label{eq:catalog-size}
\end{equation}
fixed after the protocol and \(\rho\) are fixed but before the current
distribution, target, oracle interaction, or learner tape, such that
\begin{equation}
\forall\mathcal D\ \forall h\in\mathcal H\ \forall r\in\Omega,
\qquad
A_r^{\mathcal O^{\rho}_{\mathcal D,h}}(\mathcal D,h)
\in\mathcal C_A^{\rho}
\label{eq:catalog-membership-assumption}
\end{equation}
as equality of terminal functions on \(\mathcal X\), whenever the displayed
canonical interaction is an actual tolerance-valid execution.  This
condition concerns only actual executions against the canonical policy.  It
imposes no catalog, finiteness, rank, or measurability condition on outputs
under other valid policies or under prescribed tolerance-invalid response
strings; the output range under other valid policies may be infinite.
\end{assumption}

\subsection{Formalized Goal}

Under Assumptions~\ref{assump:sq-parameter-regime},
\ref{assump:universal-adversarial-sq}, and
\ref{assump:canonical-rounded-output-catalog}, the objective is to prove that
the deterministic common map
\[
\phi:=\phi_{\mathcal C_A^{\rho}}:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^L,
\qquad
\phi(x)=(g_1(x),\ldots,g_L(x)),
\]
is fixed independently of the current distribution, target, valid oracle
policy, and learner tape, and satisfies
\begin{equation}
\forall h\in\mathcal H\ \exists w_h\in\Delta_L\ \forall x\in\mathcal X,
\qquad
h(x)\langle w_h,\phi(x)\rangle
\ge1-2\varepsilon>\frac12>0.
\label{eq:formalized-margin-goal}
\end{equation}
Each \(w_h\) may depend on \(h\), but not on \(\mathcal D\), the oracle
policy, or the learner tape.  Consequently, the desired exact deterministic
bound is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{dc}(\mathcal H)
\le L
\le B\left(1+\frac{m}{\tau^2}\right)^k.
\label{eq:formalized-dimension-goal}
\end{equation}
This is a conditional result under
Assumption~\ref{assump:canonical-rounded-output-catalog}.  It does not derive
that catalog from \((m,\tau)\) alone and does not resolve the unconditional
open question of obtaining a linear \(O(m/\tau^2)\) dimension bound from the
unrestricted SQ guarantee alone.
