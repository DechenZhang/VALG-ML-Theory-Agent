\section{Proof Sketch}\label{sec:proof-sketch}

For a fixed distribution, target, and arbitrary valid policy, push the learner
tape through the measurable terminal selector.  This produces an exact finite
law on the catalog and exact loss and correlation mixtures, including at zero
queries.  Universal expected accuracy then yields a catalog-mixture
correlation at least $\rho$, and a finite maximum dominates that mixture.

For a nonempty finite $F\subseteq X$, apply the policy-free correlation bound
to every distribution supported on $F$.  A self-contained finite minimax
argument converts the resulting $\forall r\,\exists i$ statement into one
$w\in\Delta_L$ satisfying every point constraint on $F$, without margin
loss.  The empty set is handled directly.  The finite constraint sets are
closed subsets of the fixed compact simplex, and their finite intersections
are exactly the constraint sets associated with finite unions.  Compactness
therefore gives one $w_h$ satisfying all constraints on an arbitrary domain.

Finally, the catalog score is exactly the inner product with $\phi_G$.
Because $\rho>1/2$, its signed margin gives strict signs, and the resulting
$L$-coordinate common map is an admissible witness in the definition of
$\operatorname{dc}(H)$.  Appending the primitive catalog budget gives the
stated dimension chain.  The complete arguments appear in
Propositions~\ref{prop:step-001-catalog-law},
\ref{prop:step-002-policy-free-correlation},
\ref{prop:step-003-finite-feasibility},
\ref{prop:step-004-global-weight},
\ref{prop:step-005-exact-representation}, and
\ref{prop:step-006-dimension-chain}.
