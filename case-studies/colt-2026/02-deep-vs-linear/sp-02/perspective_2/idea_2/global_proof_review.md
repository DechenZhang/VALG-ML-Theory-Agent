## Citation And Tool Audit

The sole theorem-critical external citation was checked independently in the original source: Maurice Sion, On General Minimax Theorems, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 8(1) (1958), Theorem 3.4, DOI 10.2140/pjm.1958.8.171. The source theorem states that for compact convex spaces \(M,N\) and a quasi-concave-convex, upper-semicontinuous/lower-semicontinuous payoff, \(\sup_M\inf_N f=\inf_N\sup_M f\).

The branch instantiation is exact: \(M=K_A\) is the maximizing compact convex set, \(N=\Delta(S)\) is the minimizing compact convex set for nonempty finite \(S\), and
\[
L_h(f,p)=\sum_{x\in S}p(x)h(x)f(x)
\]
is continuous affine in each variable. Thus it is upper semicontinuous and quasiconcave in \(f\), and lower semicontinuous and quasiconvex in \(p\). The source orientation directly gives
\[
\max_{f\in K_A}\min_{p\in\Delta(S)}L_h(f,p)
=\min_{p\in\Delta(S)}\max_{f\in K_A}L_h(f,p).
\]
Both extrema are attained by compactness and continuity. The diagnostic correctly records that Sion does not produce \(K_A\), the per-\(p\) correlation witness, the simplex extreme-point identity, attainment, or arbitrary-domain closure; those are separately supplied by B2, B3, B4, and B5.
