\section{Theoretical Setup}

\subsection{Problem Setting}

Let \(\mathcal X\) be an arbitrary, possibly empty or infinite, domain, and
let \(\mathcal H\subseteq\{-1,+1\}^{\mathcal X}\).  Write
\(\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\) for the probability laws on \(\mathcal X\); in
particular, all finitely supported laws and all point masses on a nonempty
domain are available.  Protocol maps are assumed measurable whenever needed
for the expectations below.  For \(\mathcal D\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\),
\(h\in\mathcal H\), and \(g:\mathcal X\to\{-1,+1\}\), set
\[
\mathcal L_{\mathcal D,h}(g)
:=\Pr_{x\sim\mathcal D}[g(x)\ne h(x)].
\]

We use exact sign representation only with nonzero scores.  Thus
\(\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^d\) represents \(h\) with weight
\(w_h\in\mathbb R^d\) when
\(h(x)\langle w_h,\varphi(x)\rangle>0\) for every \(x\).  With the infimum
of the empty set understood as \(+\infty\), define
\[
\operatorname{dc}(\mathcal H)
:=\inf\!\left\{d\in\mathbb N_0:
\begin{array}{l}
\exists\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^d\ \ \forall h\in\mathcal H\ 
\exists w_h\in\mathbb R^d\ \ \forall x\in\mathcal X,\\[-2pt]
h(x)\langle w_h,\varphi(x)\rangle>0
\end{array}\right\}.
\]

Represent all randomness of one fixed learner \(A\) by a seed
\(U\sim\mu_A\) on a seed space \(\Omega_A\).  Conditional on \(U=u\), at
each round \(t\le m\), the learner either stops or selects, as a function of
\(u\) and the preceding public query-response transcript, an unrestricted
bounded query
\[
q_t:\mathcal X\times\{-1,+1\}\to[-1,1].
\]
After at most \(m\) queries it returns a binary predictor.  The seed contains
all query-selection and output randomness.

Put \(I_\tau=[-1-\tau,1+\tau]\).  A deterministic complete response rule
\(R\) assigns a value in \(I_\tau\) to every public query-bearing history
\((q_1,v_1,\ldots,q_{t-1},v_{t-1},q_t)\) admitted by \(A\), including
histories not reached for a particular seed or preceding reply sequence.  It
observes the public queries and replies but not the hidden seed except through
that public transcript.  Let \(\mathfrak R_A^{\mathrm{all}}\) denote all such
rules.  For \(m=0\), this family contains the unique empty rule.  For
\(u\in\Omega_A\) and \(R\in\mathfrak R_A^{\mathrm{all}}\), let
\(g_{u,R}:\mathcal X\to\{-1,+1\}\) be the terminal predictor.

For \(\mathcal D\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\) and \(h\in\mathcal H\), call
\(R\) \((\mathcal D,h,\tau)\)-valid if, at every round reached by every
seed interacting with \(R\),
\[
\left|v_t-\mathbb E_{x\sim\mathcal D}q_t(x,h(x))\right|\le\tau.
\]
Write \(\mathfrak R_{A,\tau}(\mathcal D,h)\) for these valid complete rules.
This is an adaptive adversarial policy class: the accuracy premise below is
pointwise in \(R\), and no distribution over responses is introduced.
Any randomized response policy is covered conditionally on each realization
of its own randomness.

For every complete rule define its pre-instance, seed-averaged terminal
response
\[
F_R(x):=\mathbb E_{U\sim\mu_A}[g_{U,R}(x)]\in[-1,1]
\]
and the static mean-response space and rank
\[
V_A:=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}
\{F_R:R\in\mathfrak R_A^{\mathrm{all}}\}
\subseteq\mathbb R^{\mathcal X},
\qquad
r_A:=\dim V_A\in\mathbb N_0\cup\{\infty\}.
\]
These objects are fixed before any distribution, target, valid policy, or
realized seed is selected.  When \(r_A<\infty\), define in the unique
finite-dimensional topology of \(V_A\)
\[
K_A:=\overline{\operatorname{conv}}
\{F_R:R\in\mathfrak R_A^{\mathrm{all}}\}\subseteq V_A.
\]

\subsection{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[Primitive parameter regime]
\label{assump:parameter-regime}
The parameters satisfy \(m\in\mathbb N_0\), \(\tau>0\), and
\(\varepsilon\in[0,1/4)\).  The numerical constants \(B,k\in\mathbb R\)
satisfy \(B\ge1\) and \(k\ge1\), are fixed before the learner instance, and
are fixed independently of
\(\mathcal X,\mathcal H,m,\tau,\varepsilon,\mathcal D,h\), all response
rules, and all learner seeds.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Fixed randomized adaptive unrestricted-SQ interface]
\label{assump:adaptive-sq-interface}
The learner \(A\), fixed before \(\mathcal D\), \(h\), and the response
policy, has seed law \(\mu_A\), makes at most \(m\) adaptive bounded
unrestricted queries of the form above, may use the complete preceding
real-valued public transcript, and returns a binary predictor.  Interaction
with every deterministic complete response rule is well defined, and all seed
averages and displayed population expectations exist.  No additional
restriction is imposed on the query family, adaptivity, response precision,
seed space, or terminal-predictor span.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Every-valid-policy universal guarantee]
\label{assump:universal-adversarial-guarantee}
For every \(\mathcal D\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), every
\(h\in\mathcal H\), and every
\(R\in\mathfrak R_{A,\tau}(\mathcal D,h)\),
\[
\mathbb E_{U\sim\mu_A}
\mathcal L_{\mathcal D,h}(g_{U,R})\le\varepsilon.
\]
The response rule is universally quantified and adversarial; the sole
averaging is over \(U\).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Static polynomial mean-response-rank certificate]
\label{assump:mean-response-rank}
The rank over all deterministic complete response rules is finite and obeys
\[
r_A=\dim\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}
\{F_R:R\in\mathfrak R_A^{\mathrm{all}}\}
\le B\bigl(1+m/\tau^2\bigr)^k.
\]
This certificate is fixed before \(\mathcal D,h\), a selected valid rule, and
the learner seed.  It constrains only the seed-averaged responses \(F_R\), not
the cardinality or span of the individual terminal predictors, and it does
not assume a representation of \(\mathcal H\).
\end{assumption}

\subsection{Formalized Goal}

The objective is to choose one basis of \(V_A\), independently of every
learning instance, whose coordinate map represents every \(h\in\mathcal H\)
with deterministic pointwise signed margin \(1-2\varepsilon\), and to prove
the exact dimension chain
\[
\operatorname{dc}(\mathcal H)
\le r_A\le B\bigl(1+m/\tau^2\bigr)^k.
\]
Only the target weight may depend on \(h\).  The desired statement is
conditional on Assumption~\ref{assump:mean-response-rank}, has no probability
qualification or domain-size dependence, and must cover empty domains or
classes, zero rank, \(m=0\), \(\varepsilon=0\), \(B=1\), and \(k=1\).
