## Added Dimensions

- Canonical transcript operator: For a fixed learner \(A\), define a response-complete transcript incidence operator \(\Psi_A\) indexed by the internal coin seed, round, and every response prefix admitted by the bounded SQ interface. Each path records both label channels of every selected query, \(q_t(x,+1)\) and \(q_t(x,-1)\), together with the path's final prediction. The operator is constructed from \(A,m,\tau\) alone and is never indexed by a distribution, target, realized oracle reply, or selected coin outcome.
- Robust point-mass separation interface: Apply the learner premise to point-mass distributions and to every legal response strategy, then express the resulting strict \(>1/2\) pointwise agreement as a robust separation system for the signed columns of \(\Psi_A\). A target \(h\) may choose a weight \(w_h\), but the column map is shared by all targets and all distributions.
- Tolerance-energy rank compression: Prove a transcript-factorization lemma that quotients redundant response-path directions while preserving every robust separator. The proposed charge argument associates each newly independent direction with a round whose two label-conditioned response intervals are separated by more than \(2\tau\), and establishes a squared-energy inequality of the form \(\sum_j \Delta_j^2\le C_0m\) with \(\Delta_j\ge c_0\tau\); hence the retained rank is at most \(C m/\tau^2\). This rank bound is to be derived, not assumed.
- Simultaneous exactification on arbitrary domains: Obtain a strict transcript margin as an internal certificate before compression and use a finite-restriction/compactness argument to extend the common feature map from every finite subset of \(\mathcal X\) to arbitrary \(\mathcal X\), retaining the same dimension and exact signs. No finite-domain encoding or response discretization is introduced.

## Setting

- Context: Let \(\mathcal X\) be an arbitrary domain and \(\mathcal H\subseteq\{\pm1\}^{\mathcal X}\). A fixed randomized adaptive learner \(A\) is given as a premise witness. Its \(t\)-th query is a bounded function \(q_t:\mathcal X\times\{\pm1\}\to[-1,1]\) selected from the preceding transcript and internal coins, and its output is a binary predictor.
- Model: The representation sought is deterministic exact dimension complexity: one map \(\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^d\) and, for every \(h\in\mathcal H\), one weight vector \(w_h\in\mathbb R^d\) satisfying \(h(x)=\operatorname{sign}(\langle w_h,\varphi(x)\rangle)\) for every \(x\in\mathcal X\). A strict separator is sought only as an internal compactness certificate, not as an added theorem assumption.
- Data: The same \(A\) makes at most \(m\ge1\) adaptive unrestricted SQ queries of tolerance \(\tau\in(0,1]\) and, for every distribution \(\mathcal D\), target \(h^\star\in\mathcal H\), and oracle response sequence whose every answer is within \(\tau\) of the corresponding population expectation, obeys \(\mathbb E_{\mathrm{coins}}\mathcal L_{\mathcal D,h^\star}(\widehat h)\le\varepsilon\) for a fixed \(\varepsilon<1/4\). The expectation is only over \(A\)'s coins; the response sequence is adversarial and is not averaged.
- Algorithm: Use the response-complete operator, robust minimax separation, and tolerance-energy factorization described above. The resulting \(\varphi\) is a deterministic post-processing of \(A,m,\tau\), while \(w_h\) may depend on \(h\) and no other run-time object.
- Regime: Arbitrary domains, including domains with no finite encoding; arbitrary binary classes; unrestricted adaptive randomized queries; all tolerance-valid oracle branches; and the expectation-over-coins premise with \(\varepsilon<1/4\). The desired dimension is \(d\le C m/\tau^2\), where \(C\) is universal and hidden constants have no dependence on \(\mathcal X,\mathcal H,m,\tau,\varepsilon,\mathcal D,h^\star\), oracle replies, or learner coins. This is a deterministic fixed-parameter bound with \(m,\tau\) exposed, exact-sign norm/metric mode, and no probability or horizon qualification.

## Goal

- Theorem type: unconditional deterministic exactification/derandomization theorem.
- Informal statement: From any learner satisfying the stated universal adversarial-tolerance SQ premise, construct one deterministic feature map \(\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^d\), independent of the distribution, target, tolerance-valid replies, and learner coins, with \(d\le C m/\tau^2\), such that every \(h\in\mathcal H\) has a weight vector \(w_h\) with \(h(x)=\operatorname{sign}(\langle w_h,\varphi(x)\rangle)\) for every \(x\in\mathcal X\).

- Materiality: If the factorization and compactness steps hold, the result directly resolves the exactification bottleneck in Gap 2 and therefore the source's exact deterministic implication; it does not claim a relaxed probabilistic, confident, average-error, fixed-distribution, or domain-dependent substitute.
- Significance: The branch attacks the missing common-map step itself. It keeps all legal response branches and learner randomization inside the operator, so success cannot be obtained by fixing coins, selecting a favorable oracle, or reusing the flawed average/probabilistic representation route identified in the literature.
- Feasibility: The SQ response-tree and minimax ingredients are literature-derived from the COLT 2017 characterization, while exact sign-separation and the tolerance-energy rank bound are novel branch mechanisms. Their local validity is checked in three concrete regimes: (i) on the arbitrary-domain constant class \(\mathcal H=\{+1,-1\}\), the one-query protocol \(q(x,y)=y\) has disjoint response intervals for \(\tau<1\), so both endpoint replies give the rank-one map \(\varphi(x)=1\); (ii) on \(\mathcal X=\{x_1,x_2\}\) with all four labelings, the two queries \(q_i(x,y)=y\mathbf 1\{x=x_i\}\) recover every coordinate whenever its mass exceeds \(2\tau\), while the unresolved mass is at most \(4\tau\), giving the advertised error check for \(\tau\le\varepsilon/4\) and a rank-two transcript factorization; and (iii) randomizing the order of those two queries gives the same rank-two coin-independent map, with the convex-hull minimax separator verified for all four labelings and all tolerance-valid replies. These examples validate the proposed interfaces, not the general theorem; the unrestricted-path rank lemma and arbitrary-domain strict-margin extension remain explicit proof obligations.
- Consistency: No new distribution, target, finite-domain, finite-precision, continuity, favorable-oracle, fixed-coin, margin, stability, boundedness, or generated-invariant assumption is added. The response-complete operator and its rank bound are derived objects, not theorem-facing premises, and the only target-dependent object is \(w_h\). The map is built before choosing \(\mathcal D,h^\star\), replies, or coins, so the setting matches the fixed Perspective 2 tuple and is not a duplicate of a relaxed probabilistic-dimension or fixed-distribution result.
