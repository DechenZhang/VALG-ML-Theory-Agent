## Review Rationale

The sketch is mathematically useful as a failure diagnosis: its point-mass and finite Gordan prefix is valid, and its three central counterchecks are also valid. It is not viable as a proof sketch. The proposed tolerance-energy argument controls the wrong scope, the proposed quotient has no exact separator-descent relation, and finite strict representability has no generic arbitrary-domain real compactness theorem. These are the mechanisms introduced by the idea itself, so repairing them requires replacing the core construction rather than decomposing an existing supported interface into smaller steps.

`IDEA_FAIL` is therefore the smallest sound route for this branch. This verdict does not assert that the parent open implication is false. It asserts that preserving this roadmap would require a theorem-contract change: adding unsupported primitive common-energy/common-kernel and global-coherence or margin/cardinality sources, changing domain scope or metric, or weakening deterministic exact representation. A genuinely new derivation from the unchanged SQ premise remains logically possible, but it belongs to `/subagent-idea-generator`, not to a second sketch attempt that merely renames the same missing claims.
