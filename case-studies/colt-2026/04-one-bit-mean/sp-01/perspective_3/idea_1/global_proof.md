## Attempted Theorem Claim

This is **exact-goal mode**. Fix a known \(k>1\). There are constants
\(a_k,b_k,c_k,\alpha_k,\beta_k,C_k>0\), depending only on \(k\), with
\(c_k<1\), such that the exact protocol in setting.md has the following
property. For every known \(\lambda\geq\sigma>0\), every
\(0<\epsilon\leq c_k\sigma\), every \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\), and every
\(D\in{\cal D}(k,\lambda,\sigma)\), it uses a fixed non-stopping sample split,
transmits exactly one bit from every independent sample, and fixes every
randomized measurable query before observing any response. Its estimator
\(\widehat\mu\) obeys

\[
 \Pr_D\{|\widehat\mu-\mu(D)|>\epsilon\}\leq\delta
\]

under the joint law of the samples and all localization and refinement
randomness, and its deterministic sample count satisfies

\[
 n\leq C_k\left[\log\frac{\lambda}{\sigma}+
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}\log\dfrac1\delta,&k>2,\\[0.35em]
 \dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}\log\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}
       \log\dfrac1\delta,&k=2,\\[0.35em]
 \left(\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\right)^{k/(k-1)}
       \log\dfrac1\delta,&1<k<2.
 \end{cases}\right]
\]

The only theorem-facing assumptions are assump:parameter-domain,
assump:moment-class, assump:independent-samples, and
assump:precommitted-protocol. The probability mode is unconditional high
probability over samples and protocol randomness, uniformly in \(D\); the
horizon is fixed; the metric is absolute value; and hidden constants may depend
only on \(k\). Localization success, core membership, recentered moments, ring
geometry, cancellation, activation, variance, tail control, concentration, and
rate simplification are conclusions, not assumptions.

## Whole-Proof Draft

Let \({\cal L}\) be the sigma-field generated by the complete localization
block, including its samples, seed, queries, and returned bits. Its decoded
scalar \(c\), and hence every path quantity \(j_s,b_s,m_s,d_s,\kappa_s\), is
\({\cal L}\)-measurable. The entire refinement block is independent of
\({\cal L}\). Conditioning below is therefore on \({\cal L}\), not on the
refinement public seed. This distinction is essential: the theorem averages
over protocol randomness, and conditional on \({\cal L}\) the independent
per-sample refinement seeds remain part of iid observations.

### Block G1: localization, scalar wrapper, core, and recentering (step_001)

Instantiate Lau and Scarlett, arXiv:2604.07796v2, Theorem 16, source label
thm: alternative localization, at \(\eta=\delta/4\). Its source hypothesis
\(\mathbb E|X-\mu|\leq\sigma\) follows from assump:moment-class by
Lyapunov:

\[
 \mathbb E|X-\mu|
 \leq (\mathbb E|X-\mu|^k)^{1/k}\leq\sigma.
\]

The source's independent observations are the samples in \(I_{\rm loc}\), and
its deterministic non-adaptive binary functions \(Q_t\) are the Borel query
sets \({\cal B}_t=\{x:Q_t(x)=1\}\). Thus the source convention agrees with the
current one-bit, independent-sample, precommitted-query convention. The source
returns an interval \(I\ni\mu\), except on an event of probability at most
\(\delta/4\), with \(|I|\leq100\sigma\) in the nontrivial branch and the stated
smaller bound in the trivial branch. It also gives

\[
 N_{\rm loc}\leq C_{{\rm loc},k}
 \left(1+\log\frac{\lambda}{\sigma}+\log\frac4\delta\right).
\]

The source does not return the scalar required by the refinement decoder. The
accepted current-notation wrapper defines \(c=\operatorname{mid}(I)\), with
the fixed source-decoder tie rule and an always-defined failure output. On

\[
 {\cal E}_{\rm loc}:=\{|c-\mu|\leq50\sigma\},
\]

one has \(\Pr({\cal E}_{\rm loc})\geq1-\delta/4\). The nearest-base-center
rule gives \(|c-m_0|\leq h_0/2\), including ties. Choose \(a_k\geq200\), so
\(50\sigma\leq h_0/4\). Then

\[
 |m_0-\mu|\leq |m_0-c|+|c-\mu|
 \leq\frac{3h_0}{4},
\]

which is the exact same-target bridge from the source interval through \(c\)
to the center consumed by refinement. Consequently, on
\({\cal E}_{\rm loc}\),

\[
 M_k(c):=\mathbb E|X-m_0|^k
 \leq2^{k-1}\left(\mathbb E|X-\mu|^k+|\mu-m_0|^k\right)
 \leq C_k^{\rm rec}\sigma^k,                         \tag{G1}
\]

where \(C_k^{\rm rec}=2^{k-1}[1+(3a_k/4)^k]\). This block produces, before
any consumer uses them, the localization event, scalar center, core margin,
recentered moment, block independence, and localization cost.

### Block G2: half-open dyadic path and measurable query geometry (step_002)

For every integer \(j\), including negative \(j\),

\[
 J_{s,j}=[(j-1)h_s,(j+2)h_s).
\]

Euclidean division with the floor convention gives, for every \(s\geq1\),

\[
 j_{s-1}=2j_s+b_s,\qquad b_s\in\{0,1\}.
\]

This remains true for negative indices. The two possible child paddings are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 J_{s-1,2j}&=[(j-\tfrac12)h_s,(j+1)h_s),\\
 J_{s-1,2j+1}&=[jh_s,(j+\tfrac32)h_s),
 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{G2.1}
\]

and both are subsets of \(J_{s,j}\). Hence
\(J_{0,j_0}\subseteq\cdots\subseteq J_{S,j_S}\). Since
\(R_0=J_{0,j_0}\) and \(R_s=J_{s,j_s}\setminus J_{s-1,j_{s-1}}\),

\[
 J_{s,j_s}=J_{s-1,j_{s-1}}\mathbin{\dot\cup}R_s,
 \qquad
 J_{S,j_S}=\mathop{\dot\bigcup}_{s=0}^S R_s.           \tag{G2.2}
\]

The half-open convention makes these identities exact at every endpoint.

For a direct negative-index-safe descendant calculation, write
\(j_0=2^sj_s+q_s\), where \(q_s\in\{0,\ldots,2^s-1\}\), and put
\(t_s=(q_s+1/2)/2^s\in(0,1)\). Then

\[
 m_0=(j_s+t_s)h_s,\qquad
 d_s=m_s-m_0=(1/2-t_s)h_s,
\]

so \(|d_s|<h_s/2\). At \(s=S\), \(m_0\) lies strictly inside
\(P_{S,j_S}\), while \(J_{S,j_S}\) contains one additional \(H\)-cell on
both sides. Therefore

\[
 [m_0-H,m_0+H]\subset J_{S,j_S}.                       \tag{G2.3}
\]

If \(j'\equiv j\pmod4\) and \(j'\ne j\), the corresponding length-\(3h_s\)
paddings are disjoint: for example, \(J_{s,j+4}\) begins at
\((j+3)h_s\), while \(J_{s,j}\) ends at \((j+2)h_s\). Thus every countable
sum defining \(F_i\) has at most one active summand. Each ring is Borel, the
countable pointwise sum is Borel, and the same coordinate-map representation
is jointly measurable in the countable-product seed and \(x\). Moreover,

\[
 \left|\frac{x-m_{s,j}}{2h_s}\right|\leq\frac34
 \quad(x\in J_{s,j}),
\]

while the mass amplitude is one. Hence \(F_i\) is Borel and takes values in
\([-1,1]\) for every realized seed. The fixed tie partitions also make
\(c\mapsto j_0(c)\) and the decoder path measurable. This block supplies the
exact objects, with no surrogate-set residual, used by all later blocks.

### Block G3: dither identity, target-mask cancellation, and exact telescope (step_003)

For \(U\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-1,1]\) and every \(f\in[-1,1]\), direct
integration gives

\[
 \mathbb E_U[\mathbf1\{f\geq U\}-\mathbf1\{0\geq U\}]=\frac f2,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_U\!\left[(\mathbf1\{f\geq U\}-\mathbf1\{0\geq U\})^2\right]
   =\frac{|f|}{2}.                                      \tag{G3.1}
\]

Fix a realization of \({\cal L}\) and \(X_i=x\). At level zero, target
color has probability \(1/4\). On \(R_0\), target-mask self-correlation and
(G3.1) give

\[
 p_0\frac14\frac{16h_0}{p_0}
 \frac{x-m_0}{4h_0}=x-m_0.                              \tag{G3.2}
\]

If the active base ring is a same-color alias \(j\ne j_0\), multiplication
by \(\rho_{i,0,j_0}\) instead gives the cross moment
\(\mathbb E[\rho_{i,0,j_0}\rho_{i,0,j}]=0\).

For \(s\geq1\), the joint level, target-color, target-branch, and type
probability is \(p_s/16\). For the coordinate type, (G3.1) and the coefficient
in \(W_i\) yield

\[
 \frac{p_s}{16}\frac{16}{p_s}(4h_s)
 \frac{x-m_s}{4h_s}=x-m_s
\]

on \(R_s\). For the mass type they yield

\[
 \frac{p_s}{16}\frac{16}{p_s}(2d_s)\frac12=d_s.
\]

Their sum is \(x-m_s+d_s=x-m_0\). Every active non-target ring has an
independent mask \(\rho_{i,s,j}\), \(j\ne j_s\), and hence zero mean after
multiplication by the retained target mask. This cancellation includes all
same-color aliases, not only a selected candidate cell. Summing the target
contributions using (G2.2) gives the exact conditional identity

\[
 \mathbb E[W_i(c)\mid X_i=x,{\cal L}]
   =(x-m_0)\mathbf1\{x\in J_{S,j_S}\}.                  \tag{G3.3}
\]

Thus, with

\[
 \theta(c):=\mathbb E[W_i(c)\mid{\cal L}]
   =\mathbb E[(X-m_0)\mathbf1_{J_{S,j_S}}(X)\mid{\cal L}],
\]

independence of a fresh refinement sample from \({\cal L}\) gives the exact
same-target decomposition

\[
 \mu-m_0=\theta(c)+
 \mathbb E[(X-m_0)\mathbf1_{J_{S,j_S}^c}(X)\mid{\cal L}]. \tag{G3.4}
\]

There is no localization, fine-scale, ring-gap, or alias residual in (G3.4).

### Block G4: target and all-alias activation ledger (step_004)

For \(s\geq1\), define the complete retained activation set

\[
 {\cal A}_s(c):=\bigcup_{j\equiv j_s\ ({\rm mod}\ 4)}
 {\cal R}_{s,j,b_s}.
\]

Let \(r=|x-m_0|\). In the target case \(j=j_s\), (G2.1) and the descendant
location of \(m_0\) show that the subtracted child padding contains the full
radius-\(h_s/2\) neighborhood of \(m_0\): \(b_s=0\) gives
\(0<t_s<1/2\), while \(b_s=1\) gives \(1/2<t_s<1\), and the corresponding
child endpoints in (G2.1) have at least that margin. Hence

\[
 x\in R_s\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r\geq h_s/2
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_s\leq2r.                 \tag{G4.1}
\]

For an alias \(j=j_s+4q\), \(q\ne0\), the nearest alias padding is separated
from the path cell \(P_{s,j_s}\), which contains \(m_0\), by a complete gap of
at least \(2h_s\). Therefore alias activation gives the stronger

\[
 x\in{\cal R}_{s,j,b_s}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r>2h_s.
                                                                  \tag{G4.2}
\]

The statements are invariant under translation and therefore cover negative
ancestors; half-open endpoints only strengthen the strict alias inequality.
At a fixed scale the same-color paddings are disjoint, so at most one target
or alias ring is active. Combining (G4.1)-(G4.2),

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{s=1}^S h_s^k\mathbf1\{x\in{\cal A}_s(c)\}
 &\leq \sum_{s:\,h_s\leq2r}h_s^k\\
 &\leq \frac{(2r)^k}{1-2^{-k}}
 =:C_{{\rm act},k}r^k.                                  \tag{G4.3}
 \end{aligned}
\]

This is a concrete nonnegative finite-budget relation: the one-scale charge is
\(h_s^k\), its sign is nonnegative, and dyadic growth bounds the total by the
largest eligible scale, uniformly in \(S\). At \(r=0\) the charge is zero.

### Block G5: all-alias second moment and the single \(k=2\) logarithm (step_005)

The second identity in (G3.1) must be applied before aliases are discarded.
For \(s\geq1\), averaging the level/color/branch/type selection and dither,
while retaining every ring in \({\cal A}_s(c)\), gives the explicit bounds

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E[W_i(c)^2\mathbf1\{L_i=s,T_i={\rm coord}\}
    \mid X_i=x,{\cal L}]
 &\leq 96\frac{h_s^2}{p_s}\mathbf1_{{\cal A}_s(c)}(x),\\
 \mathbb E[W_i(c)^2\mathbf1\{L_i=s,T_i={\rm mass}\}
    \mid X_i=x,{\cal L}]
 &\leq 32\frac{d_s^2}{p_s}\mathbf1_{{\cal A}_s(c)}(x)\\
 &\leq32\frac{h_s^2}{p_s}\mathbf1_{{\cal A}_s(c)}(x).
 \end{aligned}                                                   \tag{G5.1}
\]

For example, the coordinate constant follows from selection probability
\(p_s/16\), squared coefficient \((64h_s/p_s)^2\), and
\(\mathbb E\Delta Y_i^2=|\psi|/2\leq3/8\). At level zero the analogous bound
is

\[
 \mathbb E[W_i(c)^2\mathbf1\{L_i=0\}\mid X_i=x,{\cal L}]
 \leq24\frac{h_0^2}{p_0}.                                \tag{G5.2}
\]

Since \(p_s^{-1}=Z_Sh_s^{k-2}\), (G4.3)-(G5.2) imply pointwise

\[
 \mathbb E[W_i(c)^2\mid X_i=x,{\cal L}]
 \leq Z_S\left(24h_0^k+128C_{{\rm act},k}|x-m_0|^k\right). \tag{G5.3}
\]

On \({\cal E}_{\rm loc}\), integration and (G1) give

\[
 \operatorname{Var}(W_i(c)\mid{\cal L})
 \leq\mathbb E[W_i(c)^2\mid{\cal L}]
 \leq C_k^{\rm var}\sigma^kZ_S,                         \tag{G5.4}
\]

where \(C_k^{\rm var}=24a_k^k+128C_{{\rm act},k}C_k^{\rm rec}\) is a valid
k-only choice. At \(k=2\), \(p_s=1/(S+1)\) and \(Z_S=S+1\). The pointwise
sum in (G4.3) is taken before expectation, so (G5.3) contains exactly this one
factor \(S+1\). Bounding each level separately by the full second moment and
then summing would create an invalid second logarithm; that operation is not
used.

### Block G6: outer-tail bridge and exact three-regime normalizer (step_006)

By (G2.3), \(X\notin J_{S,j_S}\) implies \(|X-m_0|>H\). Therefore, on
\({\cal E}_{\rm loc}\), the sole residual in (G3.4) satisfies

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\theta(c)-(\mu-m_0)|
 &\leq\mathbb E[|X-m_0|\mathbf1\{|X-m_0|>H\}\mid{\cal L}]\\
 &\leq H^{1-k}M_k(c)
 \leq C_k^{\rm rec}\frac{\sigma^k}{H^{k-1}}.            \tag{G6.1}
 \end{aligned}
\]

Choose \(b_k^{k-1}\geq4C_k^{\rm rec}\). Since
\(H\geq H_\star=b_k\sigma(\sigma/\epsilon)^{1/(k-1)}\), (G6.1) is at most
\(\epsilon/4\).

After \(a_k,b_k\) are fixed, choose \(c_k\) small enough that

\[
 R:=\frac{H_\star}{h_0}
 =\frac{b_k}{a_k}\left(\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\right)^{1/(k-1)}
 \geq2.
\]

Then \(S=\lceil\log_2R\rceil\) gives

\[
 H_\star\leq H=h_02^S<2H_\star.                         \tag{G6.2}
\]

The finite geometric sum is evaluated without dropping terms:

\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 k>2:&
 h_0^{2-k}\leq Z_S\leq h_0^{2-k}/(1-2^{2-k}),\\[0.3em]
 k=2:& Z_S=S+1\asymp_k\log(\sigma/\epsilon),\\[0.3em]
 1<k<2:&
 H^{2-k}\leq Z_S\leq H^{2-k}/(1-2^{k-2}).
 \end{array}                                               \tag{G6.3}
\]

For the middle line, (G6.2), fixed \(a_k,b_k,c_k\), and
\(\sigma/\epsilon\geq1/c_k>1\) absorb the additive constants when converting
base-two to natural logarithms. With

\[
 A_k:=\frac{\sigma^kZ_S}{\epsilon^2},
\]

(G6.2)-(G6.3) yield, with both upper and lower k-only constants,

\[
 A_k\asymp_k
 \begin{cases}
 \dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2},&k>2,\\[0.35em]
 \dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}\log\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon},&k=2,\\[0.35em]
 \left(\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\right)^{k/(k-1)},&1<k<2.
 \end{cases}                                               \tag{G6.4}
\]

In particular \(A_k\geq a_{*,k}>0\) throughout the parameter domain. If the
law is supported in \(J_{0,j_0}\), it is supported in \(J_{S,j_S}\), and the
residual in (G6.1) is exactly zero rather than merely \(O(\epsilon)\).

### Block G7: conditional median-of-means under the public-randomness law (step_007)

Conditional on \({\cal L}\), the path is fixed, while the refinement samples
and the complete per-sample seeds \((L_i,C_i,T_i,B_i,U_i,\rho_i)\) remain
independent and identically distributed across \(i\). Hence the
\(W_i(c)\) are conditionally iid under the joint sample-and-protocol law. On
\({\cal E}_{\rm loc}\), their common mean is \(\theta(c)\) by (G3.3) and their
variance is at most \(V_k=C_k^{\rm var}\sigma^kZ_S\) by (G5.4).

Choose \(\beta_k\geq16C_k^{\rm var}\). For every preassigned group,

\[
 \Pr\left\{|\overline W_g(c)-\theta(c)|>\epsilon/2\mid{\cal L}\right\}
 \leq\frac{4V_k}{B_{\rm ref}\epsilon^2}
 \leq\frac14.                                             \tag{G7.1}
\]

The group-failure indicators are conditionally independent. If their odd
median is farther than \(\epsilon/2\) from \(\theta(c)\), at least
\(G_\delta/2\) groups fail. Hoeffding's inequality gives

\[
 \Pr\left\{
 \left|\operatorname{median}_g\overline W_g(c)-\theta(c)\right|>\epsilon/2
 \mid{\cal L}\right\}
 \leq e^{-G_\delta/8}\leq\delta/2                         \tag{G7.2}
\]

for a universal sufficiently large \(\alpha_k\) (for example any choice at
least \(4\) under the displayed normalization). Combining (G7.2) with (G6.1),

\[
 |\widehat\mu-\mu|
 \leq\left|\operatorname{median}_g\overline W_g-\theta(c)\right|
     +|\theta(c)-(\mu-m_0)|
 \leq\frac{3\epsilon}{4}<\epsilon                         \tag{G7.3}
\]

except with conditional probability at most \(\delta/2\). No union bound over
candidate cells occurs. Conditioning additionally on all public refinement
masks would ask for a stronger fixed-seed theorem and would destroy the common
mask-averaged mean; that probability mode is not the formalized goal and is not
used here.

### Block G8: unconditional confidence, legality, ceilings, and assembly (step_008)

For query legality, the sample split and groups are fixed first; the
localization code queries are deterministic and non-adaptive; and every
refinement level, color, type, child branch, dither, and countable mask family
is drawn before the first response. By G2, each realized \(F_i\) is bounded and
Borel, hence \(A_i=\{x:F_i(x)\geq U_i\}\) is Borel. The only transmitted
refinement message is \(Y_i\). The value \(Y_i^0\) is computed from stored
public randomness. Decoding \(c\), selecting a path, forming \(W_i(c)\), and
taking the median occur only after the full transcript and do not alter any
query. Thus the construction has zero adaptive transitions and exactly one bit
per sample.

Uniformity of (G7.2) over every successful localization transcript permits
integration:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \Pr\{|\widehat\mu-\mu|>\epsilon\}
 &\leq\Pr({\cal E}_{\rm loc}^c)
  +\mathbb E\left[\mathbf1_{{\cal E}_{\rm loc}}
     \Pr\{|\widehat\mu-\mu|>\epsilon\mid{\cal L}\}\right]\\
 &\leq\delta/4+\delta/2\leq\delta.                       \tag{G8.1}
 \end{aligned}
\]

For the deterministic sample count, (G6.4) and \(A_k\geq a_{*,k}\) imply

\[
 B_{\rm ref}=\lceil\beta_kA_k\rceil\leq C_kA_k.
\]

Since \(\delta<1/2\),

\[
 G_\delta=2\left\lceil\alpha_k\log\frac8\delta\right\rceil+1
 \leq C_k\log\frac1\delta,
\]

so \(N_{\rm ref}\leq C_kA_k\log(1/\delta)\). Also

\[
 1+\log\frac4\delta\leq C\log\frac1\delta
 \leq C_ka_{*,k}^{-1}A_k\log\frac1\delta.               \tag{G8.2}
\]

Combining (G8.2) with the localization cost gives

\[
 n\leq C_k\left[\log\frac{\lambda}{\sigma}
              +A_k\log\frac1\delta\right].              \tag{G8.3}
\]

Substitution of the three exact alternatives in (G6.4) is the public rate
bridge and yields the attempted theorem. Every ceiling, additive confidence
cost, and logarithm is accounted for, and all constants depend only on the
fixed \(k\).

For the inherited baseline, if \(x\in J_{0,j_0}\), nesting puts \(x\) in
every target padding \(J_{s,j_s}\) and in every subtracted predecessor
\(J_{s-1,j_{s-1}}\). Hence no target ring \(R_s\), \(s\geq1\), is active.
Every same-color alias padding is disjoint from \(J_{s,j_s}\), so no retained
alias is active either. Thus all higher-level \(W_i\) terms and the outer
residual vanish exactly, and the protocol reduces to the importance-weighted
level-zero dither statistic followed by the same median. If \(X=m_0\), its
level-zero amplitude also vanishes, so \(\Delta Y_i=W_i=0\) and
\(\widehat\mu=m_0=\mu\) on localization success.

### Assembly

G1 produces the only localization-dependent moment control. G2 fixes the exact
sets and legal query interface. G3 identifies the exact truncated mean. G4 and
G5 control all target and alias squares at the required variance scale. G6
controls the sole target-transfer residual and evaluates the rate normalizer.

law. G8 integrates localization failure, certifies precommitment, absorbs
ceilings, and substitutes the three rates. These blocks compose without an
unproved selected-cell event, missing generated condition, changed metric, or
scope upgrade.

## Quantitative Dependence Audit

- **Exposed variables.** The proof retains
  \(k,\lambda,\sigma,\epsilon,\delta,a_k,b_k,c_k,\alpha_k,\beta_k,S,H_\star,H,
  Z_S,(p_s),N_{\rm loc},B_{\rm ref},G_\delta,N_{\rm ref}\). It also names the
  k-only diagnostic constants \(C_k^{\rm rec},C_{{\rm act},k},C_k^{\rm var}\)
  and the source constant \(C_{{\rm loc},k}\).
- **Hidden constants.** Every hidden constant may depend on the fixed \(k\),
  including the selected design constants, and on nothing else. It may not
  depend on \(D,\mu,\lambda,\sigma,\epsilon,\delta,S,H,Z_S\), a sample, a cell,
  a path, a mask, a dither, or a group.
- **Fixed quantities.** \(k\) is fixed when the geometric sums and asymptotic
  equivalences are evaluated. All other source parameters remain arbitrary in
  assump:parameter-domain.
- **Probability mode.** The final claim is unconditional high probability,
  uniform in \(D\), over samples and all protocol randomness. G7 is only a
  conditional-on-localization intermediate statement. Failure budgets are
  \(\delta/4\) and \(\delta/2\); no cellwise union bound occurs.
- **Horizon mode.** Fixed, deterministic, non-stopping horizon
  \(n=N_{\rm loc}+N_{\rm ref}\). No uniform-in-time or fixed-public-seed
  upgrade is claimed.
- **Norm mode.** Absolute value on \(\mathbb R\) for every source-target and
  estimator-target bridge.
- **Auxiliary choices and thresholds.** Choose \(a_k\geq200\), then
  \(b_k^{k-1}\geq4C_k^{\rm rec}\), then \(c_k\) so \(H_\star/h_0\geq2\), then
  \(\beta_k\geq16C_k^{\rm var}\), and a sufficiently large \(\alpha_k\).
  These choices verify the core, tail, block-success, and majority thresholds.
- **Term absorption.** Equations (G6.2)-(G6.4) prove the regime substitutions;
  \(A_k\geq a_{*,k}\) proves
  \(\lceil\beta_kA_k\rceil\lesssim_k A_k\); \(\delta<1/2\) proves
  \(G_\delta\lesssim_k\log(1/\delta)\) and
  \(1+\log(4/\delta)\lesssim\log(1/\delta)\); (G8.2) absorbs the localization
  confidence cost.
- **Public specialization.** The technical rate
  \(C_kA_k\log(1/\delta)\) is converted by the explicit three-case equality
  (G6.4), not by suppressing a term. At \(k=2\), G5 proves that \(Z_S\) occurs
  once.
- **Baseline reduction.** Support in selected \(J_0\) makes all retained
  higher target and alias terms and the outer residual exactly zero. At
  \(X=m_0\), every refinement correction is exactly zero. The original
  conclusion is preserved, rather than replaced by a remainder-only surrogate.
