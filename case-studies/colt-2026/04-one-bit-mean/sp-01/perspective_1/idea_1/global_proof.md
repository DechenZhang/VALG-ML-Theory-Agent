## Attempted Theorem Claim

This is **exact-goal mode**. Fix \(k>1\). There exist constants \(c_k,C_k>0\), depending only on \(k\), and deterministic choices of the \(k\)-only design constants together with fixed integers determined by the known tuple \((\lambda,\sigma,\epsilon,\delta)\), such that the exact protocol in `setting.md` has the following property. For every known \(\lambda\geq\sigma>0\), every \(0<\epsilon\leq c_k\sigma\), every \(\delta\in(0,1/2)\), and every

\[
D\in\mathcal D(k,\lambda,\sigma)
=\left\{D:\ \mu(D)\in[-\lambda,\lambda],\quad
  \mathbb E_D|X-\mu(D)|^k\leq\sigma^k\right\},
\]

the protocol:

1. fixes the localization/refinement split, every localization query, every refinement seed and query, and every median block before any response bit is observed;
2. receives exactly one bit from each of \(n=N_{\rm loc}+N_{\rm ref}\) independent samples; and
3. returns the always-defined decoder
   
   \[
   \widehat\mu=c+\operatorname{median}_{1\leq g\leq q}\overline Z_g(c)
   \]

satisfying the unconditional, joint-data-and-protocol-randomness bound

\[
\sup_{D\in\mathcal D(k,\lambda,\sigma)}
\Pr_{D,\mathrm{protocol}}
\left\{|\widehat\mu-\mu(D)|>\epsilon\right\}\leq\delta
\]

and the deterministic fixed-horizon count

\[
n\leq C_k\left[\log\frac{\lambda}{\sigma}+
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}\log\dfrac1\delta,&k>2,\\[0.4em]
\dfrac{\sigma^2}{\epsilon^2}\log\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\log\dfrac1\delta,&k=2,\\[0.4em]
\left(\dfrac{\sigma}{\epsilon}\right)^{k/(k-1)}\log\dfrac1\delta,&1<k<2.
\end{cases}\right].
\]

The theorem-facing assumptions are exactly `assump:parameter-domain`, `assump:moment-class`, and `assump:iid-independent-randomness`. Localization success, the recentered moment, scale geometry, stable offsets, digit inactivity, conditional moments, variance, residual bias, concentration, and all rate absorptions are derived conclusions. The probability mode is unconditional high probability, the horizon is deterministic and fixed, and the norm is absolute value on \(\mathbb R\). No support, symmetry, density, likelihood, bounded-tail, stopping-time, conditional-final-theorem, or asymptotic qualification is introduced.

## Whole-Proof Draft

Write \(\mu=\mu(D)\), let \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\) be the sigma-field generated by the localization samples and localization transcript, and put

\[
\Delta_j^c(x):=D_j^c(x)-D_j^c(c),\qquad
R:=|x-c|,\qquad t:=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon}.
\]

The proof is organized into eight blocks. Conditioning on \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\), rather than only on the scalar \(c\), makes the independence interface explicit; because the refinement block is independent of \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\), all displayed fixed-\(c\) formulas are the same formulas claimed by the sketch.

### Block B1: cited localization and actual-center moment (`step_001`, `step_002`)

The sole theorem-critical external source is Lau and Scarlett, *Order-Optimal Sequential 1-Bit Mean Estimation in General Tail Regimes*, arXiv:2604.07796v2 (May 25, 2026), Theorem 16, source label `thm: alternative localization`, with the construction and proof in Appendix `appendix: two-stage` and code lemma `lem: balanced codebook`. Its source convention is the same scalar mean/range convention used here: \(\lambda\geq\sigma\), \(\delta_{\rm loc}\in(0,1/2)\), \(\mu\in[-\lambda,\lambda]\), independent samples, and

\[
\mathbb E|X-\mu|\leq\sigma.
\]

The last hypothesis follows from `assump:moment-class` by Lyapunov:

\[
\mathbb E|X-\mu|\leq
\left(\mathbb E|X-\mu|^k\right)^{1/k}\leq\sigma.
\tag{GP-1}
\]

At confidence \(\delta_{\rm loc}=\delta/4\), the cited procedure is deterministic and fully non-adaptive. In its nontrivial branch it sets \(h=20\sigma\),

\[
N=\left\lceil\frac{2\lambda}{h}\right\rceil
=\left\lceil\frac{\lambda}{10\sigma}\right\rceil,
\qquad
\ell=\left\lceil10000\left(\log N+\log\frac4\delta\right)\right\rceil,
\]

uses the fixed code queries \(Q_r(x)=c_{b(x),r}\), and returns the union \(I\) of at most five contiguous bins around a deterministic-tie-broken nearest-Hamming decoder. Each source bit \(Q_r(X_r)\) is exactly the current indicator bit for the Borel clipped-bin union \(\mathcal B_r=\{x:Q_r(x)=1\}\). The source proof gives

\[
\Pr\{\mu\notin I\}\leq\delta/4,
\qquad |I|\leq100\sigma.
\tag{GP-2}
\]

If \(2\lambda\leq20\sigma\), it instead returns \(I=[-\lambda,\lambda]\) with zero samples and zero failure probability. Define the current scalar decoder by the mandatory wrapper \(c=\operatorname{mid}(I)\), with the same deterministic tie rule as the source. On

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm loc}:=\{|c-\mu|\leq50\sigma\},
\]

source containment gives

\[
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\rm loc})\geq1-\delta/4,
\qquad |c-\mu|\leq\frac{|I|}{2}\leq50\sigma.
\tag{GP-3}
\]

This is an object-target bridge from the source interval to the actual center consumed by every later digit, not an oracle-center substitution. The source does not output a scalar center, a recentered (k)-moment, or a refinement conclusion; those non-outputs are not attributed to it.

On \(\mathcal E_{\rm loc}\), the elementary power inequality supplies the exact current-center moment budget

\[
\mathbb E|X-c|^k
\leq2^{k-1}\left(\mathbb E|X-\mu|^k+|\mu-c|^k\right)
\leq C_k^{\rm rec}\sigma^k,
\quad C_k^{\rm rec}:=2^{k-1}(1+50^k).
\tag{GP-4}
\]

The refinement sample \(X\) in (GP-4) is independent of \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\), so this is uniform for every realized localized center and does not condition the final theorem on a generated event.

### Block B2: scales, stable cells, and bounded precommitted digits (`step_003`-`step_005`)

Take the explicit diagnostic choices

\[
\gamma_k=\frac18,
\qquad
\overline C_k^{\rm tail}:=\frac{11}{3}\left(\frac83\right)^{k-1}C_k^{\rm rec},
\]

\[
b_k\geq\max\left\{4,(8\overline C_k^{\rm tail})^{1/(k-1)}\right\},
\qquad 0<c_k\leq e^{-1}.
\tag{GP-5}
\]

Then \(h_0=\epsilon/8<\sigma\), \(H_*\leq H<2H_*\), and

\[
h_{J-1}=H/2\geq H_*/2
=\frac{b_k}{2}\sigma t^{1/(k-1)}>\sigma.
\tag{GP-6}
\]

Thus both fine and coarse groups are nonempty for every theorem parameter, so (m=2), every (p_j) is positive, and (sum_jp_j=1). Direct dyadic summation gives

\[
W_{\rm f}=\sum_{h_j\leq\sigma}\frac{h_j}{\sigma}\leq2,
\tag{GP-7}
\]

and

\[
W_{\rm c}\leq C_k
\begin{cases}
1,&k>2,\\
\log t,&k=2,\\
(H/\sigma)^{2-k},&1<k<2.
\end{cases}
\tag{GP-8}
\]

For \(k>2\), this is the decreasing series with ratio \(2^{2-k}<1\); for \(k=2\), it is the finite number of coarse levels and \(H/\sigma<2b_kt\); and for \(1<k<2\), it is the increasing series dominated by its last term. The condition \(t\geq e\) absorbs the additive \(k\)-only terms into \(\log t\) in the middle regime.

For stable shifts, if (u=\{c/h_j\}), the four sets corresponding to (a=0,1/4,1/2,3/4) are respectively

\[
[3/8,5/8),\quad[5/8,7/8),\quad
[7/8,1)\cup[0,1/8),\quad[1/8,3/8).
\tag{GP-9}
\]

They form a disjoint half-open partition of ([0,1)). Hence (a_j(c)) exists uniquely, is Borel measurable, and, with (q_j=Q_j^c(c)),

\[
c-q_j\in[3h_j/8,5h_j/8),
\qquad q_j+h_j-c\in(3h_j/8,5h_j/8].
\tag{GP-10}
\]

This is a positive two-sided cell margin for the actual decoded center, including all grid and half-open endpoint cases.

For any (a,b), write (e_j(x)=x-Q_{j,a}(x)\in[0,h_j)) and (e_{j+1}(x)=x-Q_{j+1,b}(x)\in[0,2h_j)). Then

\[
F_{j,a,b}(x)=e_{j+1}(x)-e_j(x)\in(-h_j,2h_j).
\tag{GP-11}
\]

Thus every refinement threshold preimage is Borel. If (f_x=F(x)/h_j) and (f_c=F(c)/h_j), both lie in ((-1,2)). Direct integration for (U\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-1,2]) gives the exact identities

\[
\mathbb E_U\left[3h_j
  (\mathbf1\{f_x\geq U\}-\mathbf1\{f_c\geq U\})\right]
=F(x)-F(c),
\tag{GP-12}
\]

\[
\mathbb E_U\left[\left(3h_j
  (\mathbf1\{f_x\geq U\}-\mathbf1\{f_c\geq U\})\right)^2\right]
=3h_j|F(x)-F(c)|.
\tag{GP-13}
\]

The square identity follows because the indicators differ exactly when (U) lies between (f_x) and (f_c), an interval of probability (|f_x-f_c|/3). Threshold ties are (U)-null, while at (x=c) the two indicators agree pathwise for every (U). Abdalla--Chen arXiv:2601.07074 is technique lineage only; (GP-11)-(GP-13) are current-notation derivations and need no theorem transfer from that paper.

### Block B3: exact telescope, residuals, and activation ledgers (`step_006`, `step_007`)

Because the same selected (Q_{j+1}^c) occurs in adjacent digits, finite cancellation yields

\[
T_c(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\Delta_j^c(x)
=\left(Q_0^c(x)-Q_0^c(c)\right)
 -\left(Q_J^c(x)-Q_J^c(c)\right).
\tag{GP-14}
\]

Adding and subtracting the bottom quantization errors gives the exact same-target decomposition

\[
x-c=T_c(x)+R_0^c(x)+R_H^c(x),
\qquad |R_0^c(x)|<h_0.
\tag{GP-15}
\]

No independence or nesting of the selected grids is needed for (GP-14); only equality of the adjacent current-notation quantizer is used.

Let (R=|x-c|). From (GP-10), if (R\leq3h_j/8), both (Q_j^c) and (Q_{j+1}^c) have the same values at (x) and (c), because the latter has margin (3h_{j+1}/8=3h_j/4). Consequently

\[
R\leq3h_j/8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Delta_j^c(x)=0.
\tag{GP-16}
\]

The interval (GP-11) has length (3h_j), so

\[
|\Delta_j^c(x)|<3h_j,
\qquad
\Delta_j^c(x)\neq0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_j<\frac{8R}{3}.
\tag{GP-17}
\]

Summing the nonnegative one-level charges pathwise before integrating gives

\[
\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm f}}|\Delta_j^c(x)|
\leq16\min\{R,\sigma\}.
\tag{GP-18}
\]

Indeed, when (R\leq\sigma), active dyadic scales sum to less than (2(8R/3)), and when (R>\sigma), all fine scales sum to less than (2sigma); multiplication by the digit bound (3h_j) gives the displayed common constant. Similarly,

\[
\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm c}}h_j^{k-1}|\Delta_j^c(x)|
\leq C_k^{\rm act}R^k,
\qquad
C_k^{\rm act}:=\frac{3(8/3)^k}{1-2^{-k}}.
\tag{GP-19}
\]

Here every active summand is below (3h_j^k), and the active dyadic (k)-powers form a finite geometric series with largest possible scale below (8R/3). These are concrete finite ledgers: the accumulated defects have nonnegative sign, one-step charges (3h_j) and (3h_j^k), and finite budgets (16\min\{R,\sigma\}) and (C_k^{\rm act}R^k).

At the top scale, (GP-10) also gives

\[
R\leq3H/8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_H^c(x)=0.
\tag{GP-20}
\]

At equality on the left, the lower cell boundary is included; the right cell margin is strict. Thus (GP-16) and (GP-20) include their stated boundary points.

### Block B4: exact dictionary expectation and the three conditional variance regimes (`step_008`-`step_010`)

On fixing \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\), the center \(c\) is fixed and all refinement samples and seeds retain their iid laws. For a sampled level \(j\), the selected pair occurs with probability \(1/16\), independently of \(X,U,L\). Using (GP-12), the exact inverse-probability factors cancel in the first moment:

\[
\mathbb E[Z_i(c)\mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc}]
=\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}p_j\frac1{16}\frac{16}{p_j}
  \mathbb E\Delta_j^c(X)
=\mathbb ET_c(X)=:\theta(c).
\tag{GP-21}
\]

Using (GP-13), the square retains exactly one inverse level probability:

\[
\mathbb E[Z_i(c)^2\mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc}]
=48\sum_{j=0}^{J-1}
  \frac{h_j\,\mathbb E|\Delta_j^c(X)|}{p_j}.
\tag{GP-22}
\]

The constant (48) is

\[
p_j\times\frac1{16}\times\frac{16^2}{p_j^2}\times3
=\frac{48}{p_j}.
\]

This independent constant check is essential: replacing (GP-22) by a levelwise worst-case or dropping the remaining (p_j^{-1}) would change the rate.

For fine levels, (p_j=(1/2)(h_j/\sigma)/W_{\rm f}), hence (h_j/p_j=2W_{\rm f}\sigma). Equations (GP-7), (GP-18), and (GP-22) give

\[
48\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm f}}
\frac{h_j\mathbb E|\Delta_j^c(X)|}{p_j}
=96W_{\rm f}\sigma\,
  \mathbb E\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm f}}|\Delta_j^c(X)|
\leq C\sigma^2.
\tag{GP-23}
\]

No fine-level count appears. For coarse levels,

\[
p_j=\frac12\frac{(h_j/\sigma)^{2-k}}{W_{\rm c}},
\qquad
\frac{h_j}{p_j}=2W_{\rm c}\sigma^{2-k}h_j^{k-1}.
\]

Thus (GP-4), (GP-19), and (GP-22) yield

\[
48\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_{\rm c}}
\frac{h_j\mathbb E|\Delta_j^c(X)|}{p_j}
\leq96W_{\rm c}\sigma^{2-k}C_k^{\rm act}
\mathbb E|X-c|^k
\leq C_kW_{\rm c}\sigma^2.
\tag{GP-24}
\]

Combining (GP-8), (GP-23), and (GP-24), and using variance at most raw second moment, gives a deterministic envelope \(V_k\), chosen with a sufficiently large \(k\)-only coefficient and \(V_k\geq\sigma^2\), such that uniformly on \(\mathcal E_{\rm loc}\),

\[
\operatorname{Var}(Z_i(c)\mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc})\leq V_k
\leq C_k
\begin{cases}
\sigma^2,&k>2,\\
\sigma^2\log t,&k=2,\\
\sigma^kH^{2-k},&1<k<2.
\end{cases}
\tag{GP-25}
\]

For a rare atom at distance (R), (GP-19) charges all activated coarse scales to (R^k); at (k=2), the only nonsummable-in-scale contribution is the explicitly retained finite normalizer (#\mathcal J_{\rm c}=O(\log t)). At (X=c), every term in (GP-21)-(GP-24) is zero pathwise.

### Block B5: residual-to-mean bridge (`step_011`)

Taking expectations in (GP-15) and using (GP-21) gives the exact target transfer

\[
(\mu-c)-\theta(c)
=\mathbb ER_0^c(X)+\mathbb ER_H^c(X).
\tag{GP-26}
\]

The bottom residual satisfies

\[
|\mathbb ER_0^c(X)|<h_0=\epsilon/8.
\tag{GP-27}
\]

On the support (R>3H/8) of the top residual, floor geometry gives

\[
|R_H^c(X)|\leq R+H\leq\frac{11}{3}R.
\]

Therefore (GP-4), (GP-5), and (H\geq H_*=b_k\sigma t^{1/(k-1)}) imply

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\mathbb ER_H^c(X)|
&\leq\frac{11}{3}\mathbb E\left[R\mathbf1\{R>3H/8\}\right]\\
&\leq\frac{11}{3}\left(\frac83\right)^{k-1}
  \frac{\mathbb ER^k}{H^{k-1}}\\
&\leq\overline C_k^{\rm tail}\frac{\sigma^k}{H^{k-1}}
\leq\frac{\overline C_k^{\rm tail}}{b_k^{k-1}}\epsilon
\leq\epsilon/8.
\end{aligned}
\tag{GP-28}
\]

Every residual in the transfer from the produced telescope mean to the consumed target (mu-c) is now controlled in the theorem norm and at the theorem scale:

\[
|\theta(c)-(\mu-c)|\leq\epsilon/4.
\tag{GP-29}
\]

There is no population/reference/surrogate mismatch: (c) is the actual source midpoint, (T_c) uses that same center and selected dictionary, and (GP-26) is an equality for the actual target.

### Block B6: fixed-block conditional median concentration (`step_012`)

Choose, before any message is observed,

\[
s=\left\lceil\frac{32V_k}{\epsilon^2}\right\rceil,
\qquad
q=2\left\lceil8\log\frac4\delta\right\rceil+1,
\qquad N_{\rm ref}=qs.
\tag{GP-30}
\]

Conditional on \(\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\) and on \(\mathcal E_{\rm loc}\), the block means are independent, have common mean \(\theta(c)\), and have variance at most \(V_k/s\). Chebyshev gives for each block

\[
\Pr\left\{|\overline Z_g(c)-\theta(c)|>\epsilon/2
  \mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\right\}
\leq\frac{4V_k}{s\epsilon^2}\leq\frac18.
\tag{GP-31}
\]

If the odd-\(q\) median is more than \(\epsilon/2\) from \(\theta(c)\), at least \(q/2\) blocks are bad. Hoeffding's inequality for the independent bad-block indicators gives

\[
\Pr\left\{
\left|\operatorname{median}_g\overline Z_g(c)-\theta(c)\right|>\epsilon/2
\mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\right\}
\leq\exp\left(-2q(1/2-1/8)^2\right)
=e^{-9q/32}\leq\delta/4.
\tag{GP-32}
\]

The last inequality follows from (q\geq16\log(4/\delta)+1). Combining (GP-29) and (GP-32), on the conditional refinement success event,

\[
|\widehat\mu-\mu|
\leq\left|\operatorname{median}_g\overline Z_g(c)-\theta(c)\right|
 +|\theta(c)-(\mu-c)|
\leq3\epsilon/4<\epsilon.
\tag{GP-33}
\]

This is uniform over every \(c\) with \(|c-\mu|\leq50\sigma\). It is a finite-horizon conditional lemma, not the final theorem.

### Block B7: unconditional probability closure (`step_013`)

The localization and refinement blocks are disjoint and independent by `assump:iid-independent-randomness`. Since (GP-32)-(GP-33) hold uniformly for every localized transcript,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pr\{|\widehat\mu-\mu|>\epsilon\}
&\leq\Pr(\mathcal E_{\rm loc}^c)
 +\mathbb E\left[
   \mathbf1_{\mathcal E_{\rm loc}}
   \Pr\{|\widehat\mu-\mu|>\epsilon\mid\mathcal F_{\rm loc}\}
  \right]\\
&\leq\delta/4+\delta/4
=\delta/2\leq\delta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{GP-34}
\]

The estimator remains defined on \(\mathcal E_{\rm loc}^c\); that event is retained in the union bound rather than excluded from the theorem. Taking the supremum over \(D\in\mathcal D(k,\lambda,\sigma)\) is legal because every preceding constant and conditional bound is uniform over that class.

### Block B8: public rate and zero-transition protocol closure (`step_014`)

Let (L_\delta=\log(1/\delta)) and (L_4=\log(4/\delta)). Since (delta<1/2),

\[
L_4\leq3L_\delta,
\qquad L_\delta\geq\log2.
\tag{GP-35}
\]

\[
N_{\rm ref}=qs\leq C L_\delta\left(1+\frac{V_k}{\epsilon^2}\right)
\leq C_kL_\delta
\begin{cases}
t^2,&k>2,\\
t^2\log t,&k=2,\\
t^{k/(k-1)},&1<k<2.
\end{cases}
\tag{GP-36}
\]

For the third line, (H<2b_k\sigma t^{1/(k-1)}) gives

\[
\frac{\sigma^kH^{2-k}}{\epsilon^2}
\leq C_k t^{2+(2-k)/(k-1)}
=C_k t^{k/(k-1)}.
\tag{GP-37}
\]

The source uses (N_{\rm loc}=0) when (lambda\leq10\sigma). Otherwise, its exact nontrivial count and (N\leq\lambda/(5\sigma)) give

\[
N_{\rm loc}\leq
C\left(\log\frac{\lambda}{\sigma}+L_4+1\right).
\tag{GP-38}
\]

The (L_4+1) terms in (GP-38) are dominated by the positive refinement budget in every regime using (t\geq e) and (GP-35). Combining (GP-36)-(GP-38) proves the exact displayed (C_kr_k) count, including (lambda=sigma), (epsilon=c_ksigma), (k=2), and (delta\uparrow1/2).

Finally, the source queries are deterministic Borel clipped-bin unions. Each refinement query is the Borel set

\[
\mathcal A_i=\left\{x:
F_{L_i,A_i,B_i}(x)/h_{L_i}\geq U_i\right\},

determined solely by known parameters and a seed drawn before any response. The index split, all source/refinement seeds, every query, and the median partition are fixed before data bits are received. The decoded (c) changes only the computable baseline, selected pair, and inverse-probability weight in (Z_i(c)); it never changes a query. At the algebraic baseline (x=c), (GP-12)-(GP-15) give

\[
\Delta_j^c(c)=T_c(c)=R_0^c(c)=R_H^c(c)=Z_i(c)=0
\tag{GP-39}

pathwise. This preserves the accepted zero-displacement baseline exactly. It does not assert that the random source midpoint must equal (mu) for every degenerate law; when (c\neq\mu), the same telescope estimates the nonzero displacement (mu-c).
