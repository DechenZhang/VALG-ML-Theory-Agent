\section{Preliminaries}

For a Borel map $F$ and probability law $P$, $F_\#P$ denotes the
pushforward law.  We write $\operatorname{supp}(P)$ for topological
support, $\operatorname{aff}(A)$ for affine hull, $\mathcal L^k$ for
$k$-dimensional Lebesgue measure, and $\mathcal H_L^k$ for the induced
$k$-dimensional Hausdorff volume on a $k$-dimensional affine space $L$.
These notions are used only in the structural witness clause of the main
theorem and its proof.

All quantitative bounds below are literal finite-dimensional, static
inequalities.  There is no temporal horizon, limiting horizon, confidence
parameter, or hidden multiplicative constant.  Polynomial evaluations and
derivatives use ordinary absolute value, coefficient and latent spaces use
Euclidean norm when a norm is needed, interval size is Lebesgue length, and
$C_{\mathcal A}$ is normalized by that same length.
