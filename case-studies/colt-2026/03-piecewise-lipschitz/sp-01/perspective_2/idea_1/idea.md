## Added Dimensions

- Theorem subtype: a class-uniform sufficient-condition theorem based on two coefficient-side conditional anti-concentration charts.
- Conditional-density package: with \(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\) fixed independently of \((d,R)\), control the mean conditional \(L^\infty\)-density norms of \(\alpha_0\) given all other coefficients and of \(\alpha_{d-1}\) given all other coefficients; no almost-sure uniform slice cap is imposed.
- Chart refinement: use the constant coefficient on \(|\theta|\leq 1\) and the next-to-leading coefficient on \(|\theta|\geq 1\), avoiding powers of \(|\theta|\) that grow with \(d\).
- Indexed example regime: all integers \(d\geq 1\) and all \(R\geq 1\), with the lower-radius restriction explicit.

## Setting

- Context: Let \(\Theta\subseteq\mathbb R\) be the source's compact parameter domain, and retain every positive-length interval \(I\subseteq\Theta\).
- Model: For every \(d\geq 1\) and \(R\geq 1\), consider \(\phi_\alpha(\theta)=\theta^d+\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\alpha_j\theta^j\) with \(\alpha\in[-R,R]^d\).
- Data: For a law \(\mu\), let regular conditional laws admit Lebesgue densities and define the random slice caps
  \[
  K_0^\mu=\bigl\|f^\mu_{\alpha_0\mid\alpha_{1:d-1}}(\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{1:d-1})\bigr\|_\infty,
  \qquad
  K_\infty^\mu=\bigl\|f^\mu_{\alpha_{d-1}\mid\alpha_{0:d-2}}(\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{0:d-2})\bigr\|_\infty.
  \]
  Fix \(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\), with both entries finite and independent of \(d,R\), and define \(\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\) to consist exactly of the laws \(\mu\) supported on \([-R,R]^d\) satisfying \(\mathbb E_\mu K_0^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_0\) and \(\mathbb E_\mu K_\infty^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_\infty\). For \(d=1\), both quantities are the density norm of the sole coefficient. This primitive coefficient-law condition contains no root event, interval, root intensity, or value of \(C_{\mathcal D}\).
- Data: The indexed class is nonempty for every claimed \((d,R)\) whenever \(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\geq1/2\). For \(d=1\), take the sole coefficient uniform on \([-R,R]\); for \(d=2\), take \(\alpha_0,\alpha_1\) independently uniform on \([-R,R]\); and for \(d\geq3\), take the two endpoint coefficients independently uniform on \([-R,R]\), independent of an interior block \((\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_{d-2})=(RS,\ldots,RS)\) generated by an independent Rademacher \(S\). Both mean slice caps equal \(1/(2R)\leq1/2\); the law is ambiently singular for \(d\geq3\) and has dependent middle coordinates for \(d\geq4\).
- Algorithm: No learner is specialized. The analytic protocol is a deterministic split of each interval into its intersections with \([-1,1]\), \((1,\infty)\), and \((-\infty,-1)\), followed by conditional one-dimensional image-length bounds.
- Regime: The guarantee is simultaneous over every \(\mu\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\) and every positive-length \(I\subseteq\Theta\), for all \(d\geq1\) and \(R\geq1\). The only auxiliary parameters are the displayed fixed mean slice caps; there is no hidden dependence on \(d,R,I,\Theta\), the conditioning values, or \(\mu\).

## Goal

- Theorem type: Explicit uniform polynomial root-hitting upper bound under an independently verifiable coefficient-side sufficient condition.
- Informal statement: Prove that the indexed family above satisfies
  \[
  C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
  \leq
  \max\!\left\{
  \bar\kappa_0\!\left(d+\frac{R d(d-1)}2\right),
  \bar\kappa_\infty\!\left(1+\frac{R d(d-1)}2\right)
  \right\}
  \leq P_\eta(d,R),
  \]
  where, for \(\bar\kappa_*=\max\{\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\}\), the explicitly displayed polynomial is
  \[
  \boxed{P_\eta(d,R)=\bar\kappa_*d+\frac{\bar\kappa_*}{2}R d^2.}
  \]
  Its total degree is \(3\), its degree and coefficients are uniform in \((d,R)\), and its coefficients depend only on the fixed, coefficient-side parameters \(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\).

- Significance: Among the screened coefficient-side packages with a complete uniform proof interface, mean conditional \(L^\infty\) control is the weakest: the tower argument needs only \(\mathbb E K_0^\mu\) and \(\mathbb E K_\infty^\mu\), not almost-sure slice caps or a joint endpoint density. A single endpoint chart loses polynomial control in one of the two \(|\theta|\) regimes, while fixed-point small-ball and root-intensity routes do not control interval hitting noncircularly.
- Feasibility: On \(|\theta|\leq1\), condition on \(\alpha_{1:d-1}\) and sweep \(\alpha_0=-\theta^d-\sum_{j=1}^{d-1}\alpha_j\theta^j\), whose derivative is at most \(d+Rd(d-1)/2\). On \(|\theta|\geq1\), condition on \(\alpha_{0:d-2}\), divide by \(\theta^{d-1}\), and sweep \(\alpha_{d-1}=-\theta-\sum_{j=0}^{d-2}\alpha_j\theta^{j-d+1}\), whose derivative is at most \(1+Rd(d-1)/2\). Standard conditional disintegration and the tower property convert the random slice caps to their means; the three disjoint interval pieces yield the maximum, not the sum, of the chart constants. The fixed-root sweep is literature-derived from [D2], disintegration is standard, and the mean-cap relaxation is a direct tower-property consequence rather than an unsupported new mechanism.
- Consistency: The mean caps are primitive, pre-root properties of the coefficient law and are verified by the independent endpoint innovations in the indexed witness family; arbitrary singular dependence remains allowed in the middle block. The bounds use only cube support, remain valid at tangencies and multiple roots, and introduce no generated invariant, support premise, or hidden root statistic. All constants and the nonempty family are uniform over the claimed indexed regime, and the empty tracker shows no same-perspective duplicate.
