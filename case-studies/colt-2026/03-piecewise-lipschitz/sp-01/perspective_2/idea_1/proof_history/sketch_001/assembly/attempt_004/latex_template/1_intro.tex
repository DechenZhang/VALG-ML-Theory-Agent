\section{Theoretical Setup}

\subsection{Problem setting}

Fix a source parameter domain $\Theta\subseteq\R$, and let
\[
\mathcal I(\Theta)
:=\{I\subseteq\Theta:I\text{ is an interval and }0<|I|<\infty\},
\]
where $|I|$ denotes one-dimensional Lebesgue length and every endpoint
convention is allowed.  Fix
$\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\in[0,\infty)^2$.  For an integer
$d\geq1$, a radius $R\geq1$, and
$\alpha=(\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_{d-1})\in[-R,R]^d$, define the monic
polynomial
\[
\phi_\alpha(\theta)
:=\theta^d+\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\alpha_j\theta^j
\]
and the interval root-hitting event
\[
H_{d,I}:=\{\alpha\in[-R,R]^d:\text{there is }\theta\in I
\text{ with }\phi_\alpha(\theta)=0\}.
\]

For a Borel probability law $\mu$ supported on $[-R,R]^d$, whenever the
indicated regular conditional laws have Lebesgue densities, set
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_0^\mu
&:=\left\|f^\mu_{\alpha_0\mid\alpha_{1:d-1}}
  (\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{1:d-1})\right\|_{L^\infty(\R)},\\
K_\infty^\mu
&:=\left\|f^\mu_{\alpha_{d-1}\mid\alpha_{0:d-2}}
  (\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{0:d-2})\right\|_{L^\infty(\R)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are random essential-supremum slice caps, evaluated at the random
conditioning coordinates.  When $d=1$, both conditioning tuples are empty
and both symbols denote the $L^\infty$ norm of a density of the sole
coefficient $\alpha_0$.

Writing $\mathcal P(\R^d)$ for the Borel probability laws on $\R^d$, define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}:=\{\mu\in\mathcal P(\R^d):\;&
\mu([-R,R]^d)=1;\\
&\mu(\alpha_0\in\cdot\mid\alpha_{1:d-1})\text{ and }
\mu(\alpha_{d-1}\in\cdot\mid\alpha_{0:d-2})
\text{ admit Lebesgue densities a.s.};\\
&\mathbb E_\mu K_0^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_0,
\quad \mathbb E_\mu K_\infty^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_\infty\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This definition imposes two mean conditional-density caps.  It imposes no
almost-sure uniform slice cap, ambient joint density, independence,
affine-latent representation, root statistic, or prescribed value of the
root-hitting constant.  That constant is
\[
C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
:=\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
  \sup_{I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)}
  \frac{\mu(H_{d,I})}{|I|},
\]
with value zero when either displayed indexing set is empty.

For a separate nonemptiness statement, define a witness law
$\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}$ as follows.  If $d=1$, take
$\alpha_0\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-R,R]$.  If $d=2$, take
$\alpha_0$ and $\alpha_1$ independently uniform on $[-R,R]$.  If $d\geq3$,
let $U_0,U_\infty\sim\operatorname{Unif}[-R,R]$ and let $S$ be Rademacher,
all mutually independent, and set
\[
\alpha_0=U_0,\qquad \alpha_{d-1}=U_\infty,
\qquad \alpha_j=RS\quad(1\leq j\leq d-2).
\]
Every witness is supported on $[-R,R]^d$.  The proof below also verifies
that it is ambiently singular for $d\geq3$ and has dependent middle
coordinates for $d\geq4$.

\subsection{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[Compact parameter domain]
\label{assump:compact-parameter-domain}
$\Theta\subseteq\R$ is a fixed compact source parameter domain.  The result
is uniform over every $I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)$, and its constants do not
depend on $I$ or on $\Theta$.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Indexed regime]
\label{assump:indexed-regime}
$d\in\N$ with $d\geq1$, $R\geq1$, and the finite pair
$\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\in[0,\infty)^2$ is fixed
independently of $d$ and $R$.  No lower bound on either cap parameter is
assumed for the root-hitting estimate; the threshold $1/2$ is used only in
the separate witness clause.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Compact cube support and monicity]
\label{assump:compact-cube-support}
Every theorem-quantified law $\mu$ satisfies $\mu([-R,R]^d)=1$, and its
polynomial is exactly the monic $\phi_\alpha$ above.  This is only a support
condition and does not impose an ambient density.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[Mean endpoint conditional-density caps]
\label{assump:mean-endpoint-conditional-caps}
The two endpoint regular conditional laws admit Lebesgue densities almost
surely, and their random essential-supremum norms satisfy exactly
\[
\mathbb E_\mu K_0^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_0,
\qquad
\mathbb E_\mu K_\infty^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_\infty.
\]
These are mean bounds only.  Together with
Assumption~\ref{assump:compact-cube-support}, they exhaust the membership
requirements defining $\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}$.
\end{assumption}

\subsection{Objective and scope}

The objective is an explicit, class-uniform polynomial upper bound for
$C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}$, together with the sharper interval-piece bound
from which it follows.  The coefficients may depend on the fixed $\eta$ but
on no other hidden quantity.  Under the separate condition
$\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\geq1/2$, the witness above should certify
nonemptiness for every $d\geq1$ and $R\geq1$.

This is a material partial result for the source problem.  It gives a
sufficient coefficient-side condition and an explicit $O_\eta(Rd^2)$ rate
throughout the restricted regime $R\geq1$.  It neither claims necessity nor
covers laws without both endpoint mean caps or the regime $0<R<1$.
No learner or online algorithm is specialized.
