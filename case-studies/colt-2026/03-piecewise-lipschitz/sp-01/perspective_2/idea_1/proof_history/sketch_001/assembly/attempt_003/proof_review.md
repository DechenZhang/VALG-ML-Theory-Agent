# Proof Review

## Formalized Setting

- Basic setup:
  Fix the source parameter domain \(\Theta\subseteq\mathbb R\), and let
  \[
  \mathcal I(\Theta)
  :=\{I\subseteq\Theta:I\text{ is an interval and }0<|I|<\infty\},
  \]
  where \(|I|\) is Lebesgue length and arbitrary endpoint conventions are
  allowed. Fix once and for all
  \(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\in[0,\infty)^2\), with both
  entries finite. For an integer \(d\geq1\), a radius \(R\geq1\), and
  \(\alpha=(\alpha_0,\ldots,\alpha_{d-1})\in[-R,R]^d\), define the monic
  polynomial
  \[
  \phi_\alpha(\theta)
  :=\theta^d+\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\alpha_j\theta^j
  \]
  and the interval root-hitting event
  \[
  H_{d,I}:=\{\alpha\in[-R,R]^d:\text{ there exists }\theta\in I
  \text{ such that }\phi_\alpha(\theta)=0\}.
  \]

  For a Borel probability law \(\mu\) supported on \([-R,R]^d\), whenever
  the indicated regular conditional laws have Lebesgue densities, write
  \[
  \begin{aligned}
  K_0^\mu
  &:=\left\|f^\mu_{\alpha_0\mid\alpha_{1:d-1}}
      (\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{1:d-1})\right\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb R)},\\
  K_\infty^\mu
  &:=\left\|f^\mu_{\alpha_{d-1}\mid\alpha_{0:d-2}}
      (\,\cdot\mid\alpha_{0:d-2})\right\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb R)}.
  \end{aligned}
  \]
  These are random essential-supremum slice caps, evaluated at the random
  conditioning coordinates. For \(d=1\), both conditioning tuples are empty
  and both quantities mean the \(L^\infty\)-norm of the density of the sole
  coefficient \(\alpha_0\).

  Let \(\mathcal P(\mathbb R^d)\) denote the Borel probability laws on
  \(\mathbb R^d\). Define, exactly and without additional membership conditions,
  \[
  \begin{aligned}
  \mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}:=\{\mu\in\mathcal P(\mathbb R^d):\;&
  \mu([-R,R]^d)=1;\\
  &\mu(\alpha_0\in\cdot\mid\alpha_{1:d-1})
    \text{ and }
    \mu(\alpha_{d-1}\in\cdot\mid\alpha_{0:d-2})
    \text{ admit Lebesgue densities a.s.};\\
  &\mathbb E_\mu K_0^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_0,
    \quad
    \mathbb E_\mu K_\infty^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_\infty\}.
  \end{aligned}
  \]
  Thus membership imposes both mean endpoint conditional-density caps, but
  no almost-sure uniform slice cap, ambient joint density, independence,
  affine-latent representation, root event, root intensity, interval
  statistic, or preassigned value of \(C_{\mathcal D}\).

  The class-uniform root-hitting constant is
  \[
  C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
  :=\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
    \sup_{I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)}
    \frac{\mu(H_{d,I})}{|I|},
  \]
  with value \(0\) when either indexing set of the displayed suprema is empty.

  No learner or online algorithm is specialized. The analytic protocol is the
  following deterministic two-chart construction: split each
  \(I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)\) into the disjoint pieces
  \[
  I_0:=I\cap[-1,1],\qquad
  I_+:=I\cap(1,\infty),\qquad
  I_-:=I\cap(-\infty,-1),
  \]
  so \(|I_0|+|I_+|+|I_-|=|I|\). The coefficient sweeps are
  \[
  s_0(\theta;\alpha_{1:d-1})
  :=-\theta^d-\sum_{j=1}^{d-1}\alpha_j\theta^j
  \quad (|\theta|\leq1)
  \]
  and, after dividing by \(\theta^{d-1}\),
  \[
  s_\infty(\theta;\alpha_{0:d-2})
  :=-\theta-\sum_{j=0}^{d-2}\alpha_j\theta^{j-d+1}
  \quad (|\theta|\geq1).
  \]
  Empty sums are zero. A root in the first chart is equivalent to
  \(\alpha_0=s_0(\theta;\alpha_{1:d-1})\); a root in either outer chart is
  equivalent to
  \(\alpha_{d-1}=s_\infty(\theta;\alpha_{0:d-2})\). Define
  \[
  \begin{aligned}
  B_0(d,R)&:=d+\frac{R d(d-1)}2,\\
  B_\infty(d,R)&:=1+\frac{R d(d-1)}2,\\
  M_\eta(d,R)&:=\max\{\bar\kappa_0 B_0(d,R),
                            \bar\kappa_\infty B_\infty(d,R)\},\\
  \bar\kappa_*&:=\max\{\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\},\\
  P_\eta(d,R)&:=\bar\kappa_*d+\frac{\bar\kappa_*}{2}R d^2.
  \end{aligned}
  \]
  The deterministic bridge to the probability bound is the pair of
  image-length estimates generated by
  \[
  \sup_{|\theta|\leq1}|\partial_\theta s_0(\theta;\alpha_{1:d-1})|
  \leq B_0(d,R),
  \qquad
  \sup_{|\theta|\geq1}|\partial_\theta s_\infty(\theta;\alpha_{0:d-2})|
  \leq B_\infty(d,R).
  \]

  For the nonemptiness assertion only, define a witness law
  \(\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}\) as follows. If \(d=1\), take
  \(\alpha_0\sim\mathrm{Unif}[-R,R]\). If \(d=2\), take
  \(\alpha_0,\alpha_1\) independently uniform on \([-R,R]\). If \(d\geq3\),
  take \(U_0,U_\infty\sim\mathrm{Unif}[-R,R]\) and a Rademacher variable
  \(S\), all mutually independent, and set
  \[
  \alpha_0=U_0,\qquad \alpha_{d-1}=U_\infty,
  \qquad \alpha_j=RS\quad(1\leq j\leq d-2).
  \]
  Every witness is supported on \([-R,R]^d\). For \(d\geq3\) the witness is
  ambiently singular, and for \(d\geq4\) its middle coordinates are dependent.

- Technical assumptions:
  - `assump:compact-parameter-domain` (Compact source parameter domain):
    \(\Theta\subseteq\mathbb R\) is the fixed compact parameter domain from
    the source problem. The theorem is uniform over every
    \(I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)\), and its constant has no dependence on
    \(I\) or \(\Theta\).
  - `assump:indexed-regime` (Indexed degree, radius, and fixed cap parameters):
    \(d\in\mathbb N\) with \(d\geq1\), \(R\geq1\), and
    \(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\in[0,\infty)^2\) is finite and
    fixed independently of \(d\) and \(R\). No lower bound such as
    \(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\geq1/2\) is assumed for the root-hitting
    bound; that threshold is used only in the separate nonemptiness clause.
  - `assump:compact-cube-support` (Compact coefficient support and monicity):
    Each theorem-quantified law \(\mu\) satisfies
    \(\mu([-R,R]^d)=1\), and the associated polynomial is exactly the monic
    \(\phi_\alpha\) defined above. This is only a support condition and does
    not impose an ambient density.
  - `assump:mean-endpoint-conditional-caps` (Two mean conditional-density
    caps): The two endpoint regular conditional laws admit Lebesgue densities
    almost surely, and their random essential-supremum norms satisfy exactly
    \[
    \mathbb E_\mu K_0^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_0,
    \qquad
    \mathbb E_\mu K_\infty^\mu\leq\bar\kappa_\infty.
    \]
    These are mean bounds only. Together with
    `assump:compact-cube-support`, they are exhaustive of the law-membership
    requirements defining \(\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\).

## Formalized Goal

- Goal statement:
  For the single fixed \(\eta\) in `assump:indexed-regime`, prove that for
  every integer \(d\geq1\), every \(R\geq1\), every
  \(\mu\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\), and every positive-length interval
  \(I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)\),
  \[
  \begin{aligned}
  \mu(H_{d,I})
  &\leq
  \bar\kappa_0 B_0(d,R)|I_0|
  +\bar\kappa_\infty B_\infty(d,R)(|I_+|+|I_-|)\\
  &\leq M_\eta(d,R)|I|.
  \end{aligned}
  \]
  Consequently, simultaneously over the entire law class and all such
  intervals,
  \[
  \boxed{
  C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
  \leq
  \max\!\left\{
  \bar\kappa_0\!\left(d+\frac{R d(d-1)}2\right),
  \bar\kappa_\infty\!\left(1+\frac{R d(d-1)}2\right)
  \right\}
  \leq
  P_\eta(d,R)
  =\bar\kappa_*d+\frac{\bar\kappa_*}{2}R d^2.}
  \]
  The middle quantity is the exact two-chart maximum, not the sum of the
  chart constants; \(P_\eta\) is the explicitly displayed degree-three
  polynomial whose degree and coefficients are uniform in \((d,R)\), whose
  coefficients depend only on the fixed \(\eta\), and which has no hidden
  constants. In the same theorem, but only under the additional condition
  \(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\geq1/2\), prove that for every claimed
  \((d,R)\), the law \(\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}\) belongs to
  \(\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\): both of its mean slice caps equal
  \(1/(2R)\leq1/2\). Thus the indexed class is nonempty throughout the
  claimed regime under that threshold, without making the threshold an
  assumption of the bound.

## Attempted Claim

Fix one finite
\(\eta=(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty)\in[0,\infty)^2\), independently of
\((d,R)\). For every \(d\geq1\), \(R\geq1\),
\(\mu\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}\), and \(I\in\mathcal I(\Theta)\), the assembled
bundle proves
\[
\mu(H_{d,I})
\leq \bar\kappa_0B_0(d,R)|I_0|
 +\bar\kappa_\infty B_\infty(d,R)(|I_+|+|I_-|)
\leq M_\eta(d,R)|I|.
\]
Consequently, with the stated zero convention for empty supremum index sets,
\[
C_{\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta}}
\leq M_\eta(d,R)
\leq P_\eta(d,R)
=\bar\kappa_*d+\frac{\bar\kappa_*}{2}Rd^2,
\qquad
\bar\kappa_*=\max\{\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\}.
\]
The exact middle bound is the maximum of the two chart coefficients, not
their sum, and every coefficient is displayed.

Separately, only when
\(\bar\kappa_0,\bar\kappa_\infty\geq1/2\), the bundle proves for every
\(d\geq1\) and \(R\geq1\) that
\[
\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}\in\mathcal D_{d,R,\eta},
\qquad
K_0^{\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}}
=K_\infty^{\mu^{\mathrm{wit}}_{d,R}}
=\frac1{2R}\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
The result retains the material_partial scope: it is a sufficient-condition
theorem for \(R\geq1\), without a necessity claim, coverage of laws lacking
both endpoint mean caps, or coverage of \(0<R<1\).
