# IDEA_REPORT.md

## Ideas

### Idea 1

- Parent perspective: Perspective 1, uniform root-hitting finiteness
- Branch path: `perspective_1/idea_1`
- Setting: Monic degree-`d` polynomial laws on `[-R,R]^d` whose conditional law of `alpha_0` given the higher coefficients has a class-uniform finite mean `L^infinity` density cap.
- Goal: Prove `C_D <= bar_kappa_D L_d,R,Theta` uniformly over all member laws and positive-length intervals, and verify nonlinear heteroscedastic random-intercept families.
- Notes: `material_partial`; distinct from Perspective 2 because it targets fixed-parameter finiteness with one conditional chart and makes no polynomial-in-`(d,R)` claim.

### Idea 2

- Parent perspective: Perspective 2, uniform polynomial root-hitting bounds
- Branch path: `perspective_2/idea_1`
- Setting: Indexed monic polynomial law classes for `d >= 1`, `R >= 1` with fixed mean conditional density caps for both endpoint coefficients.
- Goal: Prove the explicit uniform bound `C_D <= bar_kappa_* d + (bar_kappa_*/2) R d^2` and verify a nonempty indexed singular family.
- Notes: `material_partial`; distinct from Perspective 1 because it uses two coefficient charts to obtain an indexed `O_eta(R d^2)` rate.
