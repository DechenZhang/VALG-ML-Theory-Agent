# Idea

## Perspective Anchor

- Tuple: `analysis_target: other_uniform_root_hitting_finiteness; model_class: degree_d_monic_univariate_random_polynomial; data_assumption: bounded_cube_coefficient_law_class; regime: class_uniform_all_positive_length_intervals; algorithm: general_algorithm`

## Added Dimensions

- Coefficient-side refinement: write `alpha=(alpha_0,beta)` with `beta=(alpha_1,...,alpha_{d-1})`. For each `mu`, let `K_mu(beta)` be the essential supremum of a regular conditional density of `alpha_0` given `beta`, and require only the class-uniform integrated envelope `bar_kappa_D:=sup_{mu in D} E_{beta~pi_mu}[K_mu(beta)]<infinity`; no pointwise-uniform cap in `beta` is imposed.
- Quantitative refinement: expose the deterministic evaluation Lipschitz envelope `L_{d,R,Theta}:=sup_{alpha in [-R,R]^d, theta in Theta}|partial_theta phi_alpha(theta)|`, together with the explicit upper bound `d B^{d-1}+R sum_{k=1}^{d-1} k B^{k-1}` for `B=max{1,sup_{theta in Theta}|theta|}`.
- Coverage refinement: certify the condition for fixed-width and heteroscedastic random-intercept laws with an otherwise arbitrary, possibly singular and dependent, higher-coefficient marginal; the heteroscedastic class may have `ess sup_beta K_mu(beta)=infinity` and nonlinear support that is not an affine latent image.

## Setting

- Context: Fix `d>=1`, `R>0`, and a compact one-dimensional parameter domain `Theta`. The target remains the source's interval root-hitting constant, uniformly over every positive-length interval contained in `Theta`.
- Model: `phi_alpha(theta)=theta^d+sum_{k=0}^{d-1} alpha_k theta^k` is monic and `alpha in [-R,R]^d`; no simplicity, root separation, or transversality assumption is imposed on its roots.
- Data: For each `mu in D`, let `pi_mu` be the marginal law of `beta` and assume the regular conditional law of `alpha_0` given `beta` has a Lebesgue density `f_mu(a_0|beta)` for `pi_mu`-almost every `beta`. With `K_mu(beta):=ess sup_{a_0} f_mu(a_0|beta)`, assume `bar_kappa_D:=sup_{mu in D} integral K_mu(beta) pi_mu(d beta)<infinity`. The marginal `pi_mu` is otherwise unrestricted and may be discrete, singular, or arbitrarily dependent across its coordinates; `K_mu` may be unbounded in `beta`.
- Algorithm: No learner is fixed; analyze only the root anti-concentration input to the dispersion recipe.
- Regime: The conclusion is simultaneous over `mu in D` and every interval `I subseteq Theta` with `|I|>0`; all dependence is displayed through `bar_kappa_D`, `d`, `R`, and `Theta` via `L_{d,R,Theta}`. No polynomial-in-`(d,R)` claim is made.

## Goal

- Theorem type: Material-partial coefficient-side sufficient-condition theorem for uniform root-hitting finiteness.
- Informal statement: Prove `C_D <= bar_kappa_D L_{d,R,Theta}<infinity`. If `m_I` is the midpoint of `I` and `phi_alpha` has a root in `I`, the mean-value theorem gives `|phi_alpha(m_I)|<=L_{d,R,Theta}|I|/2`; conditional on `beta`, this confines `alpha_0` to an interval of length at most `L_{d,R,Theta}|I|`, whose conditional probability is at most `K_mu(beta)L_{d,R,Theta}|I|`. Integration over `beta` and then the class supremum give the bound. As part of the same theorem, verify the condition for `alpha_0=G(beta)+rho(beta)U`, where `U` is independent uniform on `[-1,1]`, `rho(beta)>0` almost surely, `|G(beta)|+rho(beta)<=R`, and the law class has `sup_mu E[1/rho(beta)]<infinity`; then `bar_kappa_D<=(1/2)sup_mu E[1/rho(beta)]`.
