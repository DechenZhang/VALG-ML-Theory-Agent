\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}

Write $F=(F_1,\ldots,F_N)$ and let $I_N$ be the $N\times N$ identity
matrix.  Under Assumption~\ref{assump:anchored-unit-range}, define
\[
\gamma_F(\theta):=\frac{F(\theta)}{\|F(\theta)\|_2},
\qquad
\Gamma_{\mathrm{proj}}(F)
:=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\theta\in\Theta}
  \|\gamma_F'(\theta)\|_2.
\]
The appendix derives both the legality and finiteness of these quantities; no
such conclusion is folded into the assumptions.

Set
\[
A:=(2R)^N\kappa,
\qquad
\mathcal D:=\mathcal D_{N,R,\kappa},
\]
and define the ordered all-law, all-interval coefficient
\[
C^{\mathrm{Pf}}_{\mathcal D}(F;\Theta)
:=\sup_{\mu\in\mathcal D_{N,R,\kappa}}
  \sup_{\substack{I\subseteq\Theta\ \mathrm{interval}\\|I|>0}}
  \frac{\Pr_{\alpha\sim\mu}
  [\exists\theta\in I:\langle\alpha,F(\theta)\rangle=0]}{|I|}.
\]
Thus the interval supremum is taken for a fixed law before the law supremum.

For the affine interface, put
\[
\phi^{\mathrm{aff}}_\alpha(\theta)
:=F_0(\theta)+\langle\alpha,F(\theta)\rangle.
\]
Given the partition in Assumption~\ref{assump:affine-chart-data}, index
$\beta\in[-R,R]^{N-1}$ by coordinates $i\neq j$ and define
\[
T_j(\theta,\beta)
:=-\frac{F_0(\theta)}{F_j(\theta)}
  -\sum_{i\neq j}\beta_i\frac{F_i(\theta)}{F_j(\theta)},
\]
\[
\partial_\theta T_j(\theta,\beta)
=-\frac{
\left(F_0'(\theta)+\sum_{i\neq j}\beta_iF_i'(\theta)\right)F_j(\theta)
-\left(F_0(\theta)+\sum_{i\neq j}\beta_iF_i(\theta)\right)F_j'(\theta)
}{F_j(\theta)^2}.
\]
The associated exhaustion sets are
\[
E_{j,m}:=\{\theta\in E_j:|F_j(\theta)|\geq1/m\},
\qquad m\geq1.
\]

All vector and matrix norms below are Euclidean and induced Euclidean norms,
respectively; chart velocities use scalar absolute value.  We write
$\operatorname{Leb}^m$ for $m$-dimensional Lebesgue measure and
$\mathcal H^m$ for Euclidean Hausdorff measure.  The zero-dimensional
conventions are $[-R,R]^0=\{()\}$ and
$\operatorname{Leb}^0(\{()\})=1$.  For an interval $I$, $|I|$ denotes its
Lebesgue length, independently of endpoint convention.
