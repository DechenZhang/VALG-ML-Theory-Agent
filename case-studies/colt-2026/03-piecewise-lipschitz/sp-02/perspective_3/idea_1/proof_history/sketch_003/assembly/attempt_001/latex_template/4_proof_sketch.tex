\section{Proof Sketch}

Compactness of $K_R$ and the no-forced-root condition give a positive
available-coordinate margin on the feasible set.  Quotient differentiation
then proves fixed-family finiteness of $\Gamma_{\rm piv}$.  The extended
pivot speeds are Borel, so the least minimizing coordinate partitions $K_R$
into disjoint Borel cells.  On each cell, solving for the selected coefficient
produces a graph in the original coefficient coordinates whose
$\theta$-velocity is bounded by the selected pivot speed.

The selected denominators are exhausted by the sets
$|F_j|\geq1/m$.  Each exhausted graph is localized into countably many
Lipschitz pieces.  The equal-dimensional area formula bounds its image by
the integral of the exact graph Jacobian.  Descriptive-set results ensure
that the chart images and the full root event are Lebesgue measurable.
Monotone exhaustion recovers every nonzero selected pivot, and disjointness
of the parameter cells converts the chart sum into the single length
$|I\cap K_R|$.  Integrating the resulting coefficient-volume estimate
against the capped full joint density gives \textup{(T1)}.  Separate sharp
nonemptiness tests for the law and interval classes give the literal branch
semantics of \textup{(T2)}.

For the scale-stress family, both coordinate speeds are computed exactly;
the constant pivot gives a global upper bound $1/\delta$ and the value at
$\theta=0$ gives equality.  For monic polynomials, the constant coordinate
controls $|\theta|\leq1$, while the degree-$(d-1)$ coordinate controls
$|\theta|>1$.  Their explicit quotient derivatives give \textup{(T4)}.
Applying \textup{(T1)} on the compact closure of a positive-length bounded
interval gives the exact coefficient in \textup{(T5)}.  Empty and singleton
intervals are handled by direct affine-hyperplane nullity under the full
joint density.
